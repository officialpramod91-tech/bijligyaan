## Sabse Pehle – Safety Planning Karein

Geyser high power device hota hai. Paani aur bijli ek saath ho to risk automatically badh jata hai. Install karne se pehle ye check karein:
- Bathroom me proper earthing ho
- Wall me seepage (nami) na ho
- Geyser ke liye alag (dedicated) power line ho
- Wiring purani ya jali hui na ho

> ⚠️ **Field Warning:** Kabhi bhi geyser ko normal 6A plug me mat lagayein. Maximum shock cases ka reason weak earthing ya galat wiring hoti hai. Ghar me pit kaise check karein aur kitna kharcha aata hai, yahan padhein:

## Ventilation – Sabse Underrated Lekin Life Saving Point

Ye point log ignore kar dete hain… aur recent news me kai cases aaye hain jahan band bathroom me gas geyser chalane se log behosh ho gaye.

### Ye Problem Kyu Hoti Hai?
Gas geyser LPG/PNG jalakar paani garam karta hai. Gas jalne ke liye oxygen chahiye.

Agar bathroom:
- Chhota hai
- Bilkul band hai
- Window nahi hai
- Exhaust fan nahi hai

To oxygen level kam ho sakta hai aur carbon monoxide jaisi harmful gas jama ho sakti hai. Extreme case me ye jaanleva ho sakta hai.

### Gas Geyser Safety Rules:
- Bathroom me window zaroor ho
- Exhaust fan lagayein
- Flue pipe (chimney pipe) bahar tak connected ho
- Bedroom ya closed area me gas geyser na lagayein

### Important Difference Samjhein:
- **Electric Geyser:** Oxygen issue nahi hota
- **Gas Geyser:** Ventilation mandatory hai

Agar bathroom completely band hai, to electric geyser hi safe option hai.

## Geyser Ke Liye Wire Size Kitna Hona Chahiye? (Most Important)

Ab aate hain sabse important sawal par — **geyser ke liye wire size kitna hona chahiye?** Yahi sabse zyada galti hoti hai.

Galat wire ka matlab:
- Wire heat hogi
- Voltage drop hoga
- Bijli ka bill badhega
- Fire ka risk

### Geyser Ke Liye Wire Size Table

Neeche diye gaye table se aap easily samajh sakte hain ki geyser ke liye kaunsa wire size sahi hota hai:

| Geyser Power | Recommended Wire Size (Copper) | Recommended MCB |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **2 kW** | 1.5 mm² – 2.5 mm² | 16 Amp |
| **3 kW** | 2.5 mm² – 4.0 mm² | 20 Amp |
| **4.5 kW** | 4.0 mm² | 25 Amp |

*Important: Geyser ke liye wire size hamesha copper aur ISI mark hona chahiye. Aluminium wire avoid karein. Safety ke liye proper earthing aur 30mA RCCB install karna zaroori hai.*

### Wire Ke Important Rules:
- Sirf copper wire use karein
- Aluminium wire bilkul avoid karein
- ISI mark hona chahiye
- Multi-strand wire better hoti hai

> **Electrician Advice:** Agar aap 2kW geyser use kar rahe hain to 2.5 sq mm minimum hai. Agar 3kW geyser hai to 4 sq mm copper wire use karein.  
> **Yaad rakhein:** Sahi wire ek baar ka kharcha hai, galat wire lifetime ka risk.

## RCCB / ELCB Lagana Kyu Zaroori Hai?

Bahut log sirf MCB laga dete hain, par RCCB nahi lagate — ye sabse badi galti hai.

### RCCB Kya Karta Hai?
- Leakage current detect karta hai
- Electric shock se bachata hai
- Fault aate hi sirf 0.03 second me supply cut kar deta hai

👉 **30mA RCCB** geyser ke liye best hota hai. Family safety ke liye RCCB mandatory samjhein.

## Common Galtiyan Jo Log Karte Hain

- Geyser ko 24 ghante ON rakhna
- Extension board use karna
- Earthing ke bina chalana
- Inverter par geyser chalana
- Aluminium wire lagana

> ⚠️ **Warning:** Inverter par geyser chalana bilkul safe nahi hota. Battery aur inverter transformer dono overheat hokar damage ho sakte hain.

## Geyser Maintenance Tips (Long Life + Kam Bill)

Sirf installation hi kaafi nahi hota:
- 6–12 mahine me servicing karwayein
- Heating element ki scaling (safed papdi) saaf karwayein
- Rusting (zang) dikhe to turant repair karein
- RCCB ka test button mahine me ek baar press karke check karein
- Nahane ke baad geyser switch OFF karein

**Proper maintenance se:** Bijli bill kam, breakdown kam, aur safety zyada rehti hai.

## Final Conclusion

Geyser lagane se pehle teen cheezein kabhi ignore na karein:
1. **Proper Ventilation** (Especially gas geyser me)
2. **Sahi Geyser Ke Liye Wire Size:** 2kW ke liye 2.5 sq mm copper, 3kW ke liye 4 sq mm copper wire
3. **RCCB + MCB Safety:** 30mA RCCB zaroor lagana chahiye

Thodi si planning aapko electric shock, fire aur oxygen risk se bacha sakti hai. **Safety ek baar ka kharcha hai, accident lifetime ka nuksan.**

## FAQ (Geyser Se Related Pooche Jaane Wale Sawal)

### Q1. Geyser ke liye wire size kitna hona chahiye?
1–2kW geyser ke liye 2.5 sq mm aur 2–3kW geyser ke liye 4 sq mm copper wire use karna chahiye.

### Q2. Kya gas geyser bathroom me safe hai?
Haan, lekin proper ventilation, exhaust fan aur window hona mandatory hai.

### Q3. Kya RCCB zaroori hai?
Haan, 30mA RCCB paani ke raste aane wale shock aur current leakage se bachane ke liye mandatory hai.

### Q4. Kya geyser inverter par chala sakte hain?
Nahi, inverter par geyser chalana safe nahi hota aur battery jaldi dead ho sakti hai.
