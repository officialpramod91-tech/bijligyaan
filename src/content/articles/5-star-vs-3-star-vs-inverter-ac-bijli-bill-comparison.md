## Normal (Fixed Speed) AC Kya Hota Hai?

Normal AC ko technical bhasha me **Fixed Speed AC** ya **Non-Inverter AC** kaha jata hai.

### Yeh Kaam Kaise Karta Hai?
Iska compressor cycle hamesha ek hi fixed speed par chalta hai — ya toh 100% full speed par, ya bilkul band:
1. Jab aap room ka temperature 24°C set karte hain, toh compressor poori taaqat se chalta hai.
2. Jaise hi kamra thanda hota hai, compressor ek jhatke se band ho jata hai.
3. Jab kamre me dobara garmi badhti hai, toh compressor phir se heavy starting current khinch kar ON hota hai.

Yahi baar-baar on/off hona sabse zyada bijli (units) waste karta hai.

### Normal AC Kiske Liye Sahi Hai?
- Agar aapka AC din me sirf **1 se 2 ghante** chalna ho
- Budget bohot tight ho
- Guest room ya drawing room ke liye jahan kabhi-kabhi use hota hai

---

## Inverter AC Kya Hota Hai? (Variable Speed Technology)

Bahut se logon ko lagta hai ki *"Inverter AC ka matlab yeh ghar ke inverter/battery par chalega"* — yeh sabse bada misconception hai!

Inverter AC ka matlab hota hai **Variable Speed Compressor Technology**.

### Asli Difference:
- Inverter AC ka compressor room thanda hone par band nahi hota.
- Yeh gaadi ke accelerator ki tarah apni speed ko load ke mutabiq kam ya zyada karta rehta hai.
- Jab kamra garam hota hai toh full power par chalta hai, aur jaise hi thandak ban jaati hai, compressor apni speed ko 30%–40% tak slow kar deta hai.

### Inverter AC Ke Fayde:
- **20% se 35% tak bijli ki seedhi bachat**
- Constant room temperature (kam-zyada thandak nahi lagti)
- Bahut kam awaaz (Silent Operation)

Agar aapka AC roz **6 se 8 ghante** chalne wala hai, toh Inverter AC hi sabse behtar option hai.

---

## 3 Star vs 5 Star AC Me Asli Farq Kya Hai?

Star rating ka seedha connection **ISEER (Indian Seasonal Energy Efficiency Ratio)** rating se hota hai:
- **3 Star AC:** Iska ISEER score lagbhag 3.8 se 3.9 hota hai.
- **5 Star AC:** Iska ISEER score 5.0 se upar hota hai (yaani same thandak dene ke liye yeh kam wattage khinchta hai).

### Bijli Bill Me Kitna Fark Padta Hai?
Same cooling capacity par ek 5-Star AC, 3-Star AC ke mukable lagbhag **20% se 25% kam bijli** khata hai.

Lekin ek shart hai: **Usage lambe samay ka hona chahiye.** Agar aap din me sirf 2 ghante AC chalate hain, toh 5-Star AC khareedne me jo extra ₹8,000–₹10,000 kharch honge, wo agle 6 saal me bhi bill bacha kar recover nahi honge!

## Kaun Sa AC Aapke Liye Sahi Hai? (Selection Rule)

Apne daily usage ghanton ke hisab se AC decide karein:
- **1 se 3 ghante roz:** 3 Star Normal ya 3 Star Inverter AC lein (5-star par extra paise waste na karein).
- **4 se 7 ghante roz:** 3 Star Inverter AC sabse balance aur budget-friendly choice hai.
- **8 se 14 ghante roz (Raat bhar chalne wala):** Hamesha **5 Star Inverter AC** hi lein. Yeh 2 season me apni extra cost bill bacha kar poori kar dega.

## Expert Electrician Advice (Bijli Bill Kam Rakhne Ke Tips)

1. **AC ko 24°C se 26°C par chalayein:** Har 1 degree temperature badhane par lagbhag **6% bijli ki bachat** hoti hai. 18°C par chalane se compressor kabhi slow nahi hota aur bill double aata hai.
2. **Ceiling Fan sath me chalayein:** AC ke sath fan ko 1 ya 2 number speed par chalayein, isse thandi hawa room me fast circulate hoti hai aur thermostat jaldi cut-off hota hai.
3. **Air Filter har 15 din me saaf karein:** Jam hua filter air flow block karta hai jisse compressor par extra load padta hai.
4. **Voltage Fluctuation se bachav:** Inverter AC ke PCB board bohot sensitive hote hain. Sahi safety ke liye dedicated stabilizer zaroor lagayein.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. Kya Inverter AC ko stabilizer ki zarurat hoti hai?
Haan. Halanki aajkal ke Inverter AC me wide operating voltage range aati hai, lekin Indian grids me jab voltage 280V–300V spike karti hai toh internal PCB jal sakti hai. Isliye 4kVA copper stabilizer lagana hamesha safe rehta hai.

### Q2. 3-Star aur 5-Star AC ke bill me mahine me kitna farq padta hai?
Agar AC roz 8 ghante chalta hai, toh 5-Star AC mahine me lagbhag 60 se 70 units kam khata hai, jisse ₹450 se ₹600 tak ka seedha monthly farq padta hai.

### Q3. Kya 5-Star AC lagwane se bijli ka bill zero ho sakta hai?
Nahi. 5-Star AC sirf units kam karta hai, zero nahi. Agar aap garmi me AC chalate hue bill zero karna chahte hain, toh [Rooftop Solar System](/articles/3kw-solar-system-price-india-subsidy/) lagwana hi ekmatra permanent solution hai.

### Q4. Inverter AC kis temperature par chalana sabse efficient hota hai?
Inverter AC ko hamesha 24°C se 26°C ke beech chalana chahiye. Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) ke mutabiq yeh human body ke liye sabse comfortable aur pocket ke liye sabse sasta temperature hai.
