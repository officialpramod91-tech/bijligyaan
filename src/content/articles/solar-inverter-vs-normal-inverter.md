## Solar Inverter vs Normal Inverter: Difference & Working

In dono ke kaam karne ke tarike aur efficiency me zameen-aasman ka fark hota hai:

| Feature | Normal Inverter (Ghar Ka Inverter) | Dedicated Solar Inverter |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Power Source** | Sirf Grid (Sarkari Bijli Board) | Solar Panels + Grid Supply |
| **Battery Charging** | AC Grid se charging karta hai | Pehli priority Suraj (Solar) se hoti hai |
| **Bijli Bill Saving** | **0% Saving** (Ulta charging me bill badhata hai) | **50% se 90% tak seedhi bachat** |
| **Initial Cost** | Sasta (₹4,500 – ₹7,000) | Thoda mehnga (₹15,000 – ₹45,000) |
| **Power Management** | Manual (Bijli gayi toh chala) | Smart Auto-Switching Priority (Solar > Battery > Grid) |
| **Efficiency** | Normal (75% – 80%) | High (92% – 98% with MPPT) |

- **Normal Inverter:** Yeh sirf ek emergency backup system hai. Jab tak sarkari bijli rehti hai, yeh battery charge karta rehta hai aur bill badhata hai.
- **Solar Inverter:** Yeh direct suraj ki dhoop se bijli banakar pehle ghar ka running load chalata hai, bachi hui bijli se battery charge karta hai, aur grid supply par aapki dependence khatam karta hai.

## Kya Purana Inverter Solar Ban Sakta Hai?

👉 **Haan, 100% ban sakta hai!**

Aapko apna achha-bhala purana inverter phenkne ya turant naya inverter lene ki bilkul zaroorat nahi hai. Aap apne existing setup ko **2 asaan aur saste tarikon** se solar setup me badal sakte hain:

---

### Method 1: Solar Charge Controller Lagana (Sabse Budget-Friendly)

Yeh sabse sasta aur popular tareeka hai:
- **Setup Flow:** `Solar Panels → Solar Charge Controller → Existing Battery → Existing Normal Inverter`
- **Kaise Kaam Karta Hai:** Solar panels se aane wali high voltage aur DC current ko controller regulate karke battery me bhejta hai. Battery solar se charge hoti hai aur aapka normal inverter ghar me supply deta hai.

> **MPPT vs PWM Technology:**  
> Market me do tarah ke controller aate hain — PWM aur MPPT. Hamesha **MPPT (Maximum Power Point Tracking)** charge controller hi chunein. Yeh PWM ke muqable **25% se 30% zyada power generate** karta hai aur badal wale mausam me bhi battery fast charge karta hai.

---

### Method 2: Solar Retrofit Device Lagana (Smart Automation Upgrade)

Agar aap chahte hain ki poora system automatically decide kare ki kab solar use karna hai aur kab grid, toh **Solar Retrofit Device** sabse best option hai:
- Yeh ek smart electronic switch box hota hai jo aapke purane inverter aur mains socket ke beech me lag jata hai.
- **Smart Logic:** Din ke waqt yeh mains grid bijli ko disconnect kar deta hai taaki aapka TV, fridge aur pankha solar aur battery par chale. Shaam ko dhoop khatam hone par yeh wapas grid par shift ho jata hai.

## Connection Karne Ka Sahi Tarika (Electrician Safety Rules)

⚠️ Galat sequence me wiring karne se charge controller ka motherboard turant short ho sakta hai:

1. **Step 1 (First Priority):** Sabse pehle **Battery ke Positive (+) aur Negative (-)** taar controller se connect karein taaki controller ko system voltage (12V ya 24V) detect karne ka mauka mile.
2. **Step 2:** Iske baad **Solar Panel ke Positive (+) aur Negative (-)** taar controller me lagayein.
3. **Step 3:** Controller ke output ko battery terminals ke parallel me tight karein.

> ⚠️ **Field Warning:**  
> - Hamesha ISI mark pure **DC Copper Flexible Wire (4 sq mm ya 6 sq mm)** ka use karein. Normal patli AC wire garam hokar resistance badhati hai jisse solar efficiency 20% gir jaati hai.  
> - Reverse polarity (+ ko - me) se bachein aur circuit me ek DC MCB zaroor lagayein.

## Expert Electrician Tips (Conversion Se Pehle Ye 4 Baatein Check Karein)

1. **Battery Ki Health Check Karein:** Agar aapki battery 3 se 4 saal purani hai aur backup kam de rahi hai, toh pehle battery check karwayein. Purani weak battery solar se efficiently charge nahi hoti.
2. **Inverter Ki Age:** Agar aapka purana inverter 8–10 saal purana ho chuka hai, toh usme retrofit lagane ke bajaye direct naya MPPT solar inverter lena long-term me sasta padega.
3. **Dedicated Earthing:** Solar panels ke metal structure ke liye grounding/earthing zaroori hai taaki aakashiya bijli ya surge se inverter safe rahe.
4. **Pehle Home Load Calculate Karein:** Bina load jane panels na khareedein.

## Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawal (FAQs)

### Q1. Kya normal inverter me solar panels directly connect kar sakte hain?
Nahi! Solar panel direct jodhne se unka unregulated high voltage (30V–50V) aapke normal inverter aur battery ko jala dega. Beech me **Solar Charge Controller** lagana 100% zaroori hai.

### Q2. MPPT aur PWM charge controller me kaunsa better hai?
MPPT controller hamesha better hota hai. Yeh PWM ke muqable 25% se 30% zyada bijli banata hai aur subah-shaam kam dhoop me bhi battery ko achha charge karta hai.

### Q3. Kya purana inverter solar banane se AC chal sakta hai?
Single battery (12V) ke normal inverter par AC nahi chal sakta. Inverter AC chalane ke liye kam se kam 2.5kVA–3kVA ka setup aur sahi panel capacity chahiye hoti hai.
