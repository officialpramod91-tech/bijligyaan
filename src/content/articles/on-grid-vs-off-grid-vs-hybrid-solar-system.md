## <a id="on-grid"></a>1. On-Grid Solar System Kya Hota Hai?

On-grid system seedha **bijli vibhag (DISCOM) ki grid** se juda hota hai. Isme battery nahi lagti.

![On-grid solar system working diagram with net meter and grid connection](/images/on-grid-solar-working-process-flowchart.webp)
*Fig: On-Grid solar system ka working flow aur anti-islanding lineman safety protection.*

### On-grid kaise kaam karta hai
1. Din me panel DC bijli banate hain.
2. Grid-tie inverter use AC me badalta hai.
3. Pehle ghar ka load chalta hai.
4. Extra bijli grid me chali jati hai (net meter hisaab rakhta hai).

Ek rough rule: **1 kW solar se aam taur par ~4 units/din**, yaani lagbhag **120 units/mahina** banti hai. Season aur location se ye badalta hai. Isse apne bill ke hisaab se size ka andaza lag jata hai, aur [Solar Sathi AI](https://solar.bijligyaan.in/) se aap ye hisaab turant dekh sakte hain.

### Power cut me on-grid band kyun ho jata hai?
Grid ki light jaane par grid-tie inverter **apne aap band** ho jata hai (anti-islanding). Ye **lineman ki suraksha** ke liye hota hai, taaki line me solar ki bijli wapas na jaye. Isliye on-grid me solar hote hue bhi power cut me light nahi aati.

### On-grid ke fayde
- Battery ka kharcha aur replacement nahi.
- Sabse kam lagat, aur subsidy isi par sabse seedhe milti hai.
- Bill kam hota hai (par hamesha zero nahi, neeche dekhein).

### On-grid ke nuksan
- Power cut me backup nahi.
- Bill ki bachat net metering aur apni state ke billing rules par depend karti hai.

## <a id="off-grid"></a>2. Off-Grid Solar System Kya Hota Hai?

Off-grid system ka **grid se koi connection nahi** hota. Din me panel battery charge karte hain aur raat me battery se load chalta hai.

### Off-grid ke fayde
- Jahan grid nahi hai, wahan ekmatra option.
- Power cut ka tension nahi.

### Off-grid ke nuksan
- **Battery ki wajah se lagat zyada**, aur battery kuch saal me badalni padti hai.
- **Subsidy nahi** (PM Surya Ghar sirf grid-connected systems par).
- Load aur battery ka size sahi na ho to raat me hi battery khatam ho sakti hai.

---

## <a id="hybrid"></a>3. Hybrid Solar System Kya Hota Hai?

Hybrid system me **grid ka connection bhi hota hai aur battery bhi.** Roz ka load solar aur grid se chalta hai, aur power cut me battery se critical load (light, fan, fridge, router) chalta hai.

![Hybrid solar system working diagram with battery backup and critical load division](/images/hybrid-solar-system-working-process-flowchart.webp)
*Fig: Hybrid solar system ka working process: grid export, battery storage aur critical load backup.*

### Hybrid kab sahi hai
- Roz ya lamba power cut aata ho.
- Work-from-home, dukaan ya medical equipment jaisa load ho jo band nahi hona chahiye.

### Dhyan dein
- **Battery ka size aapke backup load aur ghanton par depend karta hai.** Poore ghar ka AC backup par chalana bahut mehnga padta hai.
- Hybrid system me inverter bhi alag type ka hota hai (hybrid inverter). On-grid ko baad me hybrid banana aksar inverter badalne jaisa kaam hota hai: [Solar Inverter vs Normal Inverter](/articles/solar-inverter-vs-normal-inverter/).

## <a id="net-metering"></a>Net Metering Aur DISCOM Process

- **10 kW tak** ke rooftop solar ke liye **technical feasibility study ki zaroorat nahi** hai (Electricity (Rights of Consumers) Amendment Rules, 2024), aur complete application accepted maani jati hai.
- Installation report ke baad **15 din** ke andar commissioning ka timeline central rules me hai. **State ke rules alag ho sakte hain**, isliye apni DISCOM/SERC ke net metering regulations dekhein.
- **Net metering vs net billing** states me alag hota hai. Isliye “bill zero” ka vaada tabhi karein jab apni state ke rules aur fixed charges dekh liye hon.

> Bijli ka bill hamesha zero nahi hota. Fixed ya minimum charges aur billing rules ke hisaab se kuch amount lag sakta hai.

> **Zaroori Jankari:** On-grid system ka sabse bada fayda tabhi milta hai jab extra bijli grid me export ho sake aur meter import-export dono nap sake. Iske pure setup, connection aur display reading ke liye hamara [solar net metering aur bi-directional meter guide](/articles/solar-net-metering-kya-hai/) zaroor padhein.

## <a id="safety"></a>Electrician Ki Nazar Se Safety (Jo Aksar Bhool Jata Hai)

Solar sirf panel nahi hota. Ye ek **bijli ka system** hai, aur galat installation se aag ya shock ka risk hota hai.

### Zaroori safety points
- **Earthing:** panel structure, inverter aur DB ki sahi earthing (IS 3043 reference). Samjhein: [Earthing Kya Hai](/articles/earthing-kya-hai/) aur [Neutral Aur Earth Wire Mein Kya Antar Hai](/articles/neutral-aur-earth-wire-mein-kya-antar-hai/).
- **SPD (surge protection)** DC aur AC dono side par, aur lightning protection (IS/IEC 62305 series reference, latest edition verify karein).
- **DC/AC isolator aur breaker (DCDB/ACDB)** sahi rating ke saath.
- **RCCB aur MCB** sahi jagah par: [RCCB Kya Hai](/articles/ghar-mein-rccb-lagana-kyun-zaruri-hai/) aur [MCB Selection Guide](/articles/mcb-types-selection-guide/).
- **AC wiring ka size aur DB:** [House Wiring Wire Size Chart](/articles/ghar-ki-wiring-wire-size-guide/).
- **BIS/ALMM-listed panels aur certified inverter.** PM Surya Ghar me MNRE-empanelled, DISCOM-registered vendor aur ALMM panels ki shart batayi jati hai.
- **Installation licensed electrician/installer se** aur supply band karke.

CEA (Measures relating to Safety and Electric Supply) Regulations, 2023 ke mutabik consumer ki installation supply se pehle **test aur verify** honi chahiye. Grid se jodne ke technical standards ke liye CEA ke connectivity regulations aur apni DISCOM ke niyam dekhein. **Exact clause ke liye CEA/Gazette ka original text dekhein.**

Ghar ki aag/shock safety ka poora guide: [Ghar Ki Electrical Safety: Shock Aur Aag Se Bachav](/articles/electric-shock-and-fire-safety-guide/).

## <a id="field-cases"></a>Field Cases (Site Se Real Experience )

> ### 🛠️ Field Case 1: “On-grid lagwaya, par dopahar ke power cut me light chali gayi”
> **Kahan Ka Mamla:** Gorakhpur (Residential 3 kW Setup).  
> **Asli Problem:** Ek client ne lakho rupaye kharch karke 3 kW on-grid setup lagwaya. Unhe laga ki ab din me free aur continuous bijli milegi. Par jaise hi summer me dopahar ke waqt transformer breakdown hone par DISCOM ki light gayi, pura solar setup chalte hue bhi ghar andhere me doob gaya. Client ko laga unka inverter kharab hai.  
> **Technical Reason:** Humne client ko samjhaya ki grid-tie inverters me **Anti-Islanding Protection (IEC 62116)** hota hai, jo line par kaam kar rahe linemen ki jaan bachane ke liye main grid band hote hi solar feed cut kar deta hai. Unke area me roz 2–3 ghante power cut tha, isliye baad me unke liye critical circuits (fans, fridge, router) ko separate backup panel par daal kar ek hybrid system propose kiya gaya.  
> **Seekh:** Agar aapke ilaqe me roz lamba ya unannounced power cut hota hai, toh sirf subsidy ke chakkar me on-grid na lein; hybrid setup par vichar karein.

> ### 🛠️ Field Case 2: “Earthing pit aur SPD na hone se inverter PCB blast”

![Solar safety and protection gear dedicated earthing pit DC SPD and DCDB box](/images/solar-safety-protection-gear-guide.webp)
*Fig: Solar installation me zaruri safety gear: Chemical Earthing (<2Ω), DC SPD surge protection aur DCDB isolator box.*

> **Kahan Ka Mamla:** Lucknow (Monsoon Season Site Inspection).  
> **Asli Problem:** Barish ke mausam me aaspas aasmani bijli girne ke baad ek 5 kW solar inverter ne kaam karna band kar diya aur uske motherboard se jalne ki smell aayi.  
> **Technical Reason:** Site par jaakar check kiya toh DC side par koi Type-2 SPD (Surge Protection Device) nahi laga tha, aur solar panels ke aluminum structure ko ghar ki aam purani neutral-loop earthing se jod diya gaya tha. Earth resistance meter se napne par **resistance 18.5 Ω** mila (jo ideal residential me 2–3 Ω se kam hona chahiye). High voltage spike seedha inverter ke MPPT card me enter kar gayi.  
> **Humne Kaise Fix Kiya:** Humne dedicated copper-bonded chemical earthing rod install karwayi (resistance gir kar 1.6 Ω par aaya) aur DCDB box me 1000V DC SPD lagaya.  
> **Seekh:** Solar panels chhat par sabse oonchi metallic surface hote hain. Earthing aur SPD koi optional luxury nahi, balki inverter aur ghar ki life insurance hain.

> ### 🛠️ Field Case 3: “1.5 Ton AC chalte hi hybrid inverter ka overload trip”
> **Kahan Ka Mamla:** Khalilabad (Home-Office Setup).  
> **Asli Problem:** 3 kW Hybrid inverter par light jaate hi 1.5 Ton split AC aur kitchen ka load chalne par inverter beep karke overload error par shut down ho jata tha.  
> **Technical Reason:** Non-inverter compressor start hote waqt running current ka 3–4 guna initial inductive surge (inrush current) leta hai. Client ne load calculation kiye bina pure main distribution board ko seedha inverter ke output terminal se feed kar diya tha.  
> **Humne Kaise Fix Kiya:** AC aur high-load circuits ko main grid busbar par chhod diya, aur sirf lighting, ceiling fans, computer setup aur refrigerator ko hybrid inverter ke critical load sub-panel se connect kiya.  
> **Seekh:** Hybrid system lagwate waqt hamesha pehle load calculation karein aur non-essential heavy appliances ko backup line se alag rakhein. Backup me kaunsa load chalega, ye pehle tay karein: [Home Electrical Load Calculation guide](/articles/home-electrical-load-calculation/).

## Aam Galtiyan Jo Har 10 Me Se 7 Log Karte Hain (Aur Baad Me Pachhtate Hain)

Field me jaate waqt hum aksar dekhte hain ki log lakhon rupaye lagakar solar lagwate hain, lekin basic technical baatein na janne ki wajah se system unki zaroorat ke mutabiq output nahi de pata. Ye 5 aisi galtiyan hain jinse aapko har haal me bachna chahiye:

---

### 1. Sirf Sabse Sasta Quotation Dekhna (Cost vs Quality Trap)
* **Galti:** Do alag-alag installers ke quote me ₹15,000–₹20,000 ka antar dekhkar bina soche saste wale ko chun lena.
* **Asli Nuksan:** Local ya saste vendor aksar **B-grade solar panels**, non-branded string inverter, patli GI sheet wale framing structure aur substandard aluminium wiring de dete hain. 2 saal baad jab panels ka degradation badhta hai ya structure me jung lagta hai, tab vendor service dene se mana kar deta hai.
* **Electrician Advice:** Quotation me brand name (Tier-1 panels, DCR approved), inverter ki warranty (kam se kam 5–7 saal), structure ka gauge (hot-dip galvanized) aur copper solar DC wire ka gauge written me lein.

---

### 2. "Bijli Bill Zero Ho Jayega" Ke Jhoothe Daave Par Bharosa
* **Galti:** Sales agent ke kehne par ye maan lena ki on-grid system lagte hi bijli vibhag ka bill ₹0 aayega.
* **Ground Reality:** On-grid system aapke **energy charges (units consumption)** ko net-metering se offset karta hai. Lekin har rajya ke DISCOM me **Fixed Demand Charges**, meter rent, electricity duty aur regulatory surcharges compulsory hote hain jo har mahine aane hi aane hain.
* **Electrician Advice:** Solar lagane ka maksad monthly bill ko 80%–90% tak reduce karna hai, complete non-existent banana nahi. Fixed charges ko load sizing se pehle apne budget me jodkar rakhein.

---

### 3. Power Cut Ka Pattern Jane Bina On-Grid Chun Lena
* **Galti:** Sirf subsidy lene ke chakkar me us ilaqe me On-Grid lagwa lena jahan din me 3–4 ghante power cut rehta hai.
* **Asli Nuksan:** Jaise humne Field Case 1 me dekha—On-grid inverter me **Anti-Islanding Protection (IEC 62116)** hota hai. Jab DISCOM ki grid supply band hoti hai, toh rooftop par dhoop hone ke bawajood inverter solar generation band kar deta hai taaki lineman safe rahe. Nateeja: Dhoop me bhi ghar me pankhe band milte hain!
* **Electrician Advice:** Agar aapke ilaqe me dhoop ke waqt (subah 10 baje se shaam 4 baje tak) frequent load shedding hoti hai, toh ya toh hybrid system lijiye ya fir apne purane battery inverter ko On-Grid ke sath dual-setup me chalayein.

---

### 4. Off-Grid Ya Standalone System Par Sarkari Subsidy Ki Ummeed
* **Galti:** Off-grid (battery-only) system lagwake PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana portal par subsidy apply karne ki koshish karna.
* **Kanooni Sach:** MNRE guidelines ke mutabiq central financial assistance (subsidy) **sirf aur sirf On-Grid RTS (Rooftop Solar) systems** par milti hai jo DISCOM net meter se connected hote hain. Standalone off-grid battery systems par subsidy nahi milti.
* **Electrician Advice:** Agar subsidy primary target hai, toh grid-tie system chunein. Agar battery backup priority hai, toh subsidy chhodkar direct hybrid/off-grid budget banayein.

---

### 5. Earthing, SPD Aur DCDB Ko "Faltu Kharcha" Samajhna
* **Galti:** Vendor ko bolna—*"Bhai earthing pit ek hi bana do, teen alag-alag banane me faltu kharcha badh raha hai."*
* **Bada Khatra:** IS 3043 standard ke anusar solar setup me kam se kam 3 dedicated earthing pits chahiye hote hain (1 AC side, 1 DC inverter side, 1 Lightning Arrester). Single pit ya zero SPD lagane se aas-paas aasmani bijli girne par ek single voltage spike pure inverter aur motherboard ko fook deta hai (repair cost ₹15,000–₹30,000).
* **Electrician Advice:** Safety gear par bachaye gaye ₹5,000 aane wale waqt me ₹50,000 ka nuksan karate hain. Dedicated chemical earthing (resistance < 2Ω), 1000V DC SPD aur proper 2-pole DC MCB par koi samjhauta na karein.

## <a id="faqs"></a>Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawal (FAQs)

### 1. On-grid, off-grid aur hybrid solar me kya fark hai?
On-grid system grid se juda hota hai aur isme battery nahi hoti. Off-grid system grid se alag hota hai aur battery par chalta hai. Hybrid system me grid ka connection bhi hota hai aur battery backup bhi.

### 2. Kya power cut me on-grid solar chalega?
Nahi. Grid ki light jaane par grid-tie inverter safety ke liye apne aap band ho jata hai, isliye on-grid system power cut me backup nahi deta. Backup ke liye hybrid ya off-grid system chahiye.

### 3. Kya off-grid solar par subsidy milti hai?
Nahi. PM Surya Ghar ki central subsidy sirf grid-connected residential rooftop systems par batayi jati hai. Off-grid systems eligible nahi maane jate.

### 4. Kya hybrid solar par subsidy milti hai?
Kuch sources ke mutabik grid-connected hybrid system me subsidy mil sakti hai, par battery ke liye extra subsidy nahi milti. Exact shart apne DISCOM, vendor aur official portal se likhit me confirm karein.

### 5. 3 kW ke liye kaunsa system sabse sasta padta hai?
Aam taur par on-grid system sabse sasta hota hai. 3 kW ke liye vendor sources me lagbhag ₹1.2–2 lakh (subsidy se pehle) ke ranges mile hain. Hybrid aur off-grid battery ki wajah se mehnge padte hain.

### 6. Kya on-grid se bijli bill zero ho jata hai?
Hamesha zero nahi. Bill apne usage, net metering ya net billing ke rules aur fixed ya minimum charges par depend karta hai. Apni state ke rules aur DISCOM se confirm karein.

### 7. Solar battery kitne saal chalti hai?
Sources me lead-acid battery ki life kuch saal (lagbhag 3–8 saal) aur LFP lithium battery ki life zyada (lagbhag 8–15 saal) batayi gayi hai. Warranty aur cycle life likhit me lein.

### 8. Net metering ke liye kitna time lagta hai?
Central rules ke hisaab se 10 kW tak ke rooftop solar ke liye technical feasibility study ki zaroorat nahi hai, aur installation report ke baad commissioning ka timeline 15 din rakha gaya hai. State ke rules alag ho sakte hain, isliye apni DISCOM se confirm karein.

Agar aapke ghar me **power cut kam** aata hai, to **on-grid** sabse samajhdari ka sauda hai: kam lagat, subsidy aur jaldi payback. Aur kisi bhi system me **earthing, SPD aur RCCB** ko “extra” na samjhein, ye wahi cheezein hain jo aapke solar aur ghar dono ko bachati hain.
