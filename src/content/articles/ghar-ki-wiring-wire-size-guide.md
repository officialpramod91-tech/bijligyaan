## Wire Ka 'Sq mm' (mm²) Kya Hota Hai?

Electrical technical bhasha mein wire ka size **sq mm (square millimeter)** conductor ke cross-section area ko darshata hai. Yeh wire ke bahar ka plastic cover (outer diameter) nahi hota, balki andar ke copper conductor ki asali motai hoti hai.

Simple rule: **Copper jitna mota hoga, current utni hi aasaani se bina overheat hue flow karega.**

### Patli Wire Garam Kyun Hoti Hai?
Har wire mein thoda natural resistance hota hai. Jab patli wire par heavy load daala jata hai, toh usme garmi ($I^2R$ loss) paida hoti hai:
- **Patli Wire + Bada Load:** Wire heattar ki tarah garam hoti hai, PVC insulation naram hokar pighalne lagta hai aur aakhirkar short-circuit ho jata hai.
- **Sahi Size Ki Wire:** Current bina rukawat flow hota hai, wire thandi rehti hai aur bijli ka bill bhi nahi badhta.

## Wire Aur MCB Ka Match (Sabse Zaroori Niyam)

Electrical engineering ka sabse basic rule hai: **Circuit ka load current $\le$ MCB rating $\le$ Wire ki safe capacity.**

Bohot se electricians 1.5 mm² wire par 16A ya 25A ki MCB laga dete hain. Yeh sabse badi laparwahi hai! Agar wire ki safe capacity 14 Ampere hai aur MCB 20 Ampere ki hai, toh wire pehle overheat hokar pighal jayegi, lekin MCB trip hi nahi karegi!

- **1.5 mm² Wire:** Hamesha **10A MCB** ke sath chalayein.
- **2.5 mm² Wire:** Hamesha **16A MCB** ke sath chalayein.
- **4.0 mm² Wire:** Hamesha **20A ya 25A MCB** ke sath chalayein.
- **6.0 mm² Wire:** Hamesha **32A MCB** ke sath protect karein.

## Room-by-Room Practical Wire Guide

### 1. Bedroom Aur Living Room
- **Lights Aur Fans:** 1.5 mm² copper wire sabse best hai. Halanki 1.0 mm² LED load utha sakti hai, lekin nayi wiring mein 1.5 mm² lagana chahiye taaki loop drop aur continuous use par koi risk na rahe.
- **General Sockets (Phone Charger, TV, Music System):** 2.5 mm² wire daalein, kyunki socket mein kab kaunsa appliance laga diya jaye, iska koi bharosa nahi hota.

### 2. Kitchen Wiring (Heavy Load Area)
Kitchen mein microwave, mixer, induction, toaster, chimney aur RO jaise heavy appliances ek sath chalte hain:
- Har 16A power socket ke liye DB se dedicated **2.5 mm²** line kheenche.
- Agar induction cooktop regular chalta hai ya DB se kitchen door hai, toh wahan **4.0 mm²** line le jana hi samajhdari hai.

### 3. Bathroom Aur Water Geyser
- **2 kW Geyser:** 2.5 mm² copper wire aur 16A MCB perfectly safe hai.
- **3 kW Instant / Storage Geyser:** Direct DB panel se bina kisi jodh ke **4.0 mm² wire** aur 20A MCB lagayein.

## Lambi Doori Par Voltage Drop Kaise Check Karein?

Agar aapke distribution board se appliance ki doori **20–25 meter (70+ feet)** se zyada hai, toh taar ka resistance badh jata hai aur end point par voltage gir jata hai. Low voltage par chalne se motor aur compressor extra current kheench kar jal jaate hain.

![Lambi wiring par voltage drop calculation aur 2.5mm vs 4mm wire AC load comparison chart](/images/voltage-drop-calculation-house-wiring.webp)

### Simple Voltage Drop Formula:
$$\text{Voltage Drop (Volts)} = \frac{2 \times \text{Current (A)} \times \text{Length (m)} \times 0.021}{\text{Wire Size (mm²)}}$$

*(Yahan 0.021 copper ka operating resistance hai, aur 2 isliye kyunki current phase se jaakar neutral se wapas aata hai).*

**Practical Examples:**
- **Case A (Geyser 20 Meter Door, 2.5 mm² Wire):** Drop aata hai lagbhag **2.9 Volts (1.3%)** — Yeh bilkul safe hai.
- **Case B (16A Heavy Load, 30 Meter Door, 2.5 mm² Wire):** Drop aata hai lagbhag **8.1 Volts (3.5%)** — Yeh limit se bahar hai.
- **Case C (Wahi 16A Load, 30 Meter Door, 4.0 mm² Wire):** Drop ghat kar sirf **5.0 Volts (2.2%)** reh jata hai — Appliance ekdam safe!

### Kitna Voltage Drop Allowed Hai?
IEC 60364-5-52 aligned practice mein public supply se chalne wale installation ke liye voltage drop **lighting circuits mein aam taur par 3%** aur baaki **power circuits mein 5% tak** maana jata hai. Kuch IS 732 summaries mein overall 3% bataya jata hai.

Practical field target yeh rakhein ki DB se sabse door wale load tak voltage drop **3% ke andar** hi rahe. Exact permissible limit ke liye IS 732:2019 ka clause apne licensed electrician se confirm karein.

- **Rule of Thumb:** DB panel se load ki doori **15–20 meter (50–65 feet)** se zyada ho toh calculation zaroor karein, aur safe rehne ke liye hamesha ek size upar wire chunein.

**Electrician Niyam:** Hamesha koshish karein ki voltage drop **3% ke andar** rahe. Agar doori 20 meter se zyada ho, toh calculation karke ek size moti wire chunein.

## Copper vs Aluminium: Ghar Ki Wiring Me Kya Use Karein?

Ghar ki concealed wiring ke liye **sirf Multi-strand Copper Wire** hi use karein:
- **Copper Wire:** Iski conductivity bohot high hoti hai, heat kam banti hai, joints par spark nahi karta aur iski umar 20–25 saal hoti hai.
- **Aluminium Wire:** Yeh sasti zaroor hoti hai, lekin brittle (jaldi tootne wali) hoti hai. Switchboard ke screw ke neeche aluminium jaldi oxidize hokar loose ho jata hai aur sparking se aag pakad leta hai. Aluminium sirf bahar ke pole se meter tak aane wali overhead service line ke liye theek hai.

## FR vs FRLS vs HFFR Wires: Kaunsi Kharidein?

1. **FR (Flame Retardant):** Normal aag lagne par flame ko aage nahi badhne deta. Low-risk independent houses ke liye standard hai.
2. **FRLS (Flame Retardant Low Smoke):** Aag lagne par toxic kaala dhuan aur acid gas bohot kam chhodta hai. Ghar ke parivaar ki safety ke liye FRLS **sabse behtar choice** hai (halanki residential ke liye kanooni taur par mandatory nahi hai).
3. **HFFR / LSZH (Halogen Free):** Bilkul zero toxic gas paida karta hai. Yeh mehenga hota hai aur commercial malls, hospitals aur cinema halls me use hota hai.
> 💡 **Mera Suggestion:**  
> Ghar ke liye hamesha **FRLS** wire hi chunein. Dhyan rahe, aam ghar ki wiring ke liye FRLS kanooni roop se mandatory nahi hai — FR bhi allowed hota hai. Lekin parivaar ki safety ke hisaab se FRLS ek behtar aur safer option hai. Public areas, escape routes aur commercial buildings mein fire-safe cables ki requirement zyada sakht hoti hai (**IS 1646** jaisi fire-safety guidelines ke tehat).  
> **Ek baat yaad rakhein:** Sirf box par "FRLS" likha hona kaafi nahi hai, wire par official ISI mark aur manufacturer ki test details zaroor check karein.

## Asli Aur Nakli Wire Kaise Pehchanein? (IS 694 Checklist)

![Asli wire pehchanne ke liye BIS Hallmark, ISI Mark IS 694 aur Registration Mark logos](/images/bis-hallmark-isi-mark-wire-safety-check.webp)

Market mein nakli aur duplicate ISI mark wale copper wire bohot bikte hain. Kharidte waqt in 5 baaton ka dhyan rakhein:

- **Wire Par Laser Printing Check Karein:** Har meter par Company ka naam, "IS 694", wire size (mm²), voltage (1100V) aur licence number (CM/L) saaf chhape hona chahiye.
- **BIS Care App Se Verify Karein:** Wire ke box par likha CM/L licence number government ke BIS Care app par daalkar check karein ki company verified hai ya nahi.
- **Copper Ka Rang Aur Strands:** Asli copper chamakdaar laal-sunhara hota hai. Agar copper kaala, safed ya feeka lage, toh wo scrap mix ya "copper-clad aluminium" ka dhokha ho sakta hai.
- **Standard Coil Weight:** Genuine coil poori **90 meter** ki hoti hai. Bahut halki coil par hamesha shak karein aur saste ke chakkar me na padein.
- **Hamesha Pakka Bill Lein:** Batch number ke sath dealer se pakka GST invoice zaroor lein.

## Top House Wiring Wire Brands In India: Mera Honest Review

Market me darjanon local aur duplicate brands hain. Lekin 10 saal ke practical field experience me hum site par sirf unhi national brands ko recommend karte hain jo **IS 694 aur IS 8130 certified electrolytic copper** banate hain:

### 1. Polycab (Green Wire / Optima+)
- **Kyun Pasand Hai:** India me residential wiring me sabse zyada chalne wala brand hai. Inki *Green Wire (HR-FR-LSH-LF)* lead-free formulation aur 99.97% pure copper ke sath aati hai.
- **Best For:** Poore ghar ki standard concealed conduit wiring aur long runs ke liye.

### 2. Havells (LifeLine Plus S3)
- **Kyun Pasand Hai:** Havells ki S3 series high-grade HRFR (Heat Resistant Flame Retardant) insulation ke sath aati hai jo standard 70°C ke mukable **85°C continuous temperature** jhel sakti hai. Iska leakage current bohot kam (0.01 mA) hota hai.
- **Best For:** Modern apartments, heavy load kitchen circuits aur inverter AC connections ke liye.

### 3. Finolex (FRLSH / Flame Retardant)
- **Kyun Pasand Hai:** Bharat ka sabse purana aur bharosemand brand. Inka conductor annealing bohot smooth hota hai, jisse conduit pipe ke tight bends me wire khinchte waqt wire chhilne ya tootne ka risk nahi rehta.
- **Best For:** Traditional residential homes, budget projects aur commercial shops.

### 4. RR Kabel (Superex FR / Firex LS0H)
- **Kyun Pasand Hai:** RR Kabel fire safety technology me aage hai. Inka *Firex LS0H* (Low Smoke Zero Halogen) wire aag lagne par bilkul safed dhuan aur zero toxic acid gas chhodta hai.
- **Best For:** High-rise residential apartments aur luxury villas jahan fire escape safety primary priority hoti hai.

### 5. KEI Industries (HomeCab / ConFlame FRLS)
- **Kyun Pasand Hai:** Heavy industrial cables me leader hone ki wajah se inki residential *ConFlame FRLS* wire ka copper conductor bohot strong hota hai aur heavy overload jhelne ki kshamta rakhta hai.
- **Best For:** Heavy residential distribution lines aur meter-to-DB main service lines.

> ⚡ **Senior Electrician Tip:**  
> Koi bhi brand tabhi safe hai jab wo **original licensed IS 694 mark aur CM/L number** ke sath pakke bill par kharida gaya ho. Saste ke chakkar me wholesale market se inhi brands ke 'duplicate rolls' khareedne se bachein!

## Wire Kharidte Aur Lagate Waqt 7 Common Galtiyan

1. **Non-ISI Ya Local Brand Wire Lena:** Sasti wire me scrap mix copper hota hai jo 1-2 saal me kala padkar heat hone lagta hai.
2. **Geyser Ya AC Par 1.5 mm² Wire Lagana:** Heavy continuous load par patli wire insulation ko pighla kar direct short-circuit karti hai.
3. **Wire Ki Capacity Se Badi MCB Lagana:** Agar wire 14A ki hai aur MCB 25A ki, toh wire pehle jalegi par MCB trip nahi hogi.
4. **Poore Ghar Ka Load Ek Hi Neutral Par Daal Dena:** Neutral wire par double load aane se neutral cut ho jata hai aur ghar me 400V high-voltage surge aa jata hai.
5. **Aluminium Wire Switchboard Mein Lagana:** Aluminium ke joints loose hokar sparking aur terminal jalne ka sabse bada kaaran bante hain.
6. **Joints Deewar Ke Pipe Ke Andar Chhod Dena:** Wire me jodh (joints) hamesha accessible junction box ke andar hone chahiye, conduit pipe ke andar kabhi nahi.
7. **Colour Code Follow Na Karna:** Galat colour wiring se future maintenance me shock lagne aur phase-neutral short hone ka khatra badh jata hai.

### Standard Wiring Color Code (IS Niyam)

Indian Standard practice ke mutabiq hamesha standard color coding follow karein taaki kisi anjaan electrician ko bhi circuit samajhne me aasani ho:

* **Phase Wire (Live):** Red, Yellow ya Blue (Single phase me aam taur par **Red** use hota hai).
* **Neutral Wire:** Hamesha **Black** wire ka hi istemal karein.
* **Earth Wire:** Har 3-pin socket aur metal frame tak **Green** ya **Green-Yellow** strip wire daalna zaroori hai.

## Wiring Ka Sahi Tareeka (Conduit, Joints Aur Bunching)

Ek achhi wire tabhi saalon-saal safe rehti hai jab use deewar ke andar sahi tareeqe se install kiya jaye:

- **Conduit Pipe Mein Wires Ko Zyada Na Bharein (Bunching):** Ek hi 20mm ya 25mm conduit pipe mein 8–10 taarein zabardasti na thonse. Ek pipe mein zyada wires hone se heat bahar nahi nikal pati aur wires ki current jhelne ki capacity 30% tak ghat jati hai (Derating factor). Pipe mein kam se kam 40% jagah khali honi chahiye.
- **Conduit Bends Smooth Rakhein:** Pipe daalte waqt 90-degree ke sharp bends se bachein. Hamesha smooth curve dein taaki future mein wire khinchne ya badalne par taar phase nahi.
- **Joints Sirf Accessible Junction Box Mein Karein:** Deewar ke pipe ke andar kachhe joints lagane ki galti kabhi na karein. Koi bhi wire joint sirf modular switch box ya ceiling junction box ke andar hi hona chahiye.
- **Dedicated Independent Circuits:** Lights/fans, heavy power sockets, kitchen, geyser aur AC ke phase-neutral circuits hamesha alag-alag rakhein.
- **Terminals Ko Mazbooti Se Kasein:** Switch aur MCB ke brass screw terminals par loose connection sparking aur heat ka sabse pehla kaaran hota hai.
- **Labelling Aur Single Line Diagram:** Wiring poori hone par Distribution Board ke har circuit breaker par marker se label karein (jaise: Bed-1 AC, Geyser, Kitchen) taaki emergency mein kisi ko pareshani na ho.

## Indian Standards Aur CEA Regulations: Wire Se Jude Rules

Ek licensed electrician hone ke naate main hamesha Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) aur Central Electricity Authority ke niyamon ko follow karne ki salah deta hoon:

* **IS 694:2010:** PVC insulated wires aur cables (1100 V tak) ka official standard. Ghar ki internal concealed wiring ke liye yahi standard dekha jata hai, aur iska ISI mark hona mandatory hai.
* **IS 8130:** Insulated cables ke andar lagne wale copper aur aluminium conductors ki pure conductivity aur mechanical strength ka standard.
* **IS 732:2019:** Wiring installations ka code of practice (IEC 60364 aligned). Wire sizing, voltage drop limit, conduit pipe me bunching ka derating factor aur wiring testing sab isi ke daayre mein aate hain.
* **CEA Regulations 2023 (Safety & Electric Supply):** Central Electricity Authority ke niyam ke mutabiq consumer wiring supply shuru hone se pehle test honi chahiye, apparatus ki capacity estimated fault current jhelne layak honi chahiye aur installation RCD (RCCB) se surakshit honi chahiye.
* **IS 17048:** Halogen-Free Flame Retardant (HFFR / LSZH) cables ka official standard.

> 📌 **Note:** Exact clauses aur local state discom ke rules ke liye apne ilaqe ke Electrical Inspectorate ya qualified licensed technician se salaah zaroor lein.

## Wiring Ke Baad Insulation Resistance (Megger Test) Zaroor Karwayein

Wiring poori hone ke baad deewar band karne se pehle apne electrician se **500V Megger Meter** se test zaroor karwayein:
- Phase aur Neutral ke beech resistance **kam se kam 1 Mega Ohm (1 MΩ)** hona chahiye.
- Phase aur Earth ke beech bhi minimum 1 MΩ aana chahiye.

Central Electricity Authority (CEA 2023) safety regulations ke mutabiq supply chalu hone se pehle poori wiring ka leakage-free hona kanooni standard hai.

## Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawal (FAQs)

### Q1. 1.5 Ton AC ke liye kaunsi wire best hai?
1.5 Ton Inverter AC ke liye dedicated **4.0 mm² multi-strand copper FRLS wire** aur **16A ya 20A C-Type MCB** sabse behtar aur safe hoti hai. Chhoti doori par 2.5 mm² bhi chal sakti hai.

### Q2. Geyser ke liye kitne mm² ki wire lagani chahiye?
2 kW geyser ke liye 2.5 mm² copper wire aur 16A MCB kaafi hoti hai. Agar geyser 3 kW ka hai ya DB board 20 meter se door hai, toh 4.0 mm² wire lagayein.

### Q3. Kya ghar me 1.0 mm² wire use kar sakte hain?
Nayi wiring me 1.0 mm² wire sirf chhote LED bulb points ke liye use hoti hai. Sockets ya kisi bhi power point par 1.0 mm² wire lagana sakht mana hai.

### Q4. 2.5 mm² wire par kitna load safe chalta hai?
2.5 mm² copper wire par 16A MCB ke sath lagbhag **2500 Watt se 2900 Watt** tak ka continuous load aasaani se chalaya ja sakta hai.

### Q5. Earth wire ka size kitna hona chahiye?
Indian Standard (IS 732) ke mutabiq 16 mm² tak ke circuits me earth wire ka size phase wire ke barabar hona chahiye. Yaani 2.5 mm² socket circuit me 2.5 mm² green earth wire daalein.

### Q6. FR aur FRLS wire mein kya antar hai?
FR wire aag aage nahi badhne deti. FRLS wire aag lagne par bohot kam dhuan aur zero toxic acid gas chhodti hai, isliye residential gharon ke liye FRLS behtar choice hoti hai.

### Q7. Copper wire behtar hai ya aluminium?
Ghar ki internal concealed conduit wiring ke liye sirf copper wire hi use karein. Aluminium ke joints loose hokar sparking aur aag ka kaaran bante hain.

### Q8. Lambi doori par voltage drop kaise kam karein?
Agar wire ki lambai 20 meter se zyada ho, toh calculation ke hisaab se ek size moti wire lagayein (jaise 2.5 mm² ki jagah 4.0 mm²). Isse voltage drop 3% ke andar rehta hai.

### Q9. Asli ISI mark wire kaise pehchanein?
Wire par "IS 694", manufacturer ka naam aur CM/L licence number laser print check karein. Is licence number ko BIS Care app par verify karein aur hamesha pakka bill lein.
