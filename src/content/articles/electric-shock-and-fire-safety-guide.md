Aksar gharon me log bolte hai: *"Arrey, geyser ya cooler se halka sa current lag gaya, koi baat nahi!"* Ek senior electrician ke taur par main saaf bata doon: **shock lagna ya switchboard se halki si bhi sparking aana bilkul normal nahi hai.** Ye is baat ka clear signal hai ki wiring, earthing ya appliance ke andar kahin na kahin bada fault ban raha hai. Kabhi-kabhi shock nange phase wire ko chhoone se lagta hai, par gharon mein 90% cases leakage current, weak earthing ya pighle huye insulation ki wajah se hote hai.

Shock ya short circuit ki aag warning dekar nahi aati. Haadse ke waqt pehle 2 se 3 minute sabse zaroori hote hai. Ghabrahat mein log do badi galtiyan karte hai: pehli, shock lage vyakti ko nange haath pakad kar kheenchna, aur doosri, bijli ke board ki aag par balti bhar kar paani phenk dena. Ye dono galtiyan seedha jaanleva sabit hoti hai.

Is comprehensive guide mein hum **Indian Standards (IS 732:2019, IS 3043:2018, IS 2190:2024, IS 12640)** aur **CEA Regulations 2023** ke technical rules ke sath ground reality samjhenge: **electric shock kyun lagta hai (बिजली का झटका क्यों लगता है)**, **shock lagne par first aid kaise dein (करंट लगने पर क्या करें)**, **short circuit se aag lage to kya kare (शॉर्ट सर्किट से आग)**, aur distribution box ko safe kaise rakhe.

> **🚨 Emergency Quick Action Box (Turant Kya kare)**
>
> **⚡ Agar Kisi Ko Current Lage:**
> 1. **Pehle Supply Band kare:** Main switch, isolator ya DB ki MCB turant OFF kare, ya appliance ka plug nikal dein. Ghar mein inverter/UPS laga ho to uska output bhi switch off kare.
> 2. **Nange Haath Se Na Chhooein:** Switch tak na pahunch sakein to sukhi lakdi, PVC pipe ya sukhe mote kapde jaise insulator se victim ko wire se alag kare. Khud geeli zameen par khade na hon.
> 3. **112 ya 108 Par Call kare:** Phone turant speaker par daal kar helpline se baat kare.
> 4. **Saans Na Chal Rahi Ho:** Bina der kiye hands-only CPR shuru kare.
>
> **🔥 Agar Short Circuit (Bijli) Se Aag Lage:**
> 1. Safe ho to turant main switch band kare. Na pahunch sakein to door hat jayein.
> 2. **Paani Bilkul Na Daalein:** Live wiring par paani daalna jaanleva shock deta hai.
> 3. Sabhi ko ghar se bahar nikalein, lift (elevator) ka use bilkul na kare.
> 4. **101 (Fire Brigade) aur 112 par call kare.**
> 5. Aag chhoti ho to hi CO2 ya ABC dry powder extinguisher chalayein. Aag fail chuki ho to ghar chhod dein.
>
> **Emergency Numbers:** 112 (National Emergency) | 101 (Fire) | 108 / 102 (Ambulance)

## Part 1: Electric Shock Kyun Lagta Hai? (Ghar Mein Current Aane Ke 8 Kaaran)

Bijli ka sidha niyam hai: current hamesha ek closed circuit khojta hai. Normal wiring mein current Phase wire se hokar appliance mein jata hai aur Neutral wire ke raste wapas chala jata hai. Lekin jab kisi fault ya cut ki wajah se current appliance ki metal body mein aa jaye, aur zameen (Earth) tak jaane ke liye insaan ka sharir raasta ban jaye, tab current hamari body se cross hota hai aur hume tezz jhatka lagta hai.

### 1. Earthing Ka Na Hona Ya Beech Se Toot Jana
Gharon mein lagne wale 80% shocks ka yahi mukhya kaaran hota hai. Fridge, geyser, metal cooler aur washing machine ki body metal ki hoti hai. Agar plug ka mota top pin (Earth Pin) proper earth pit se connected nahi hai, to motor ya heating element ka leakage current body par jam jata hai. Jaise hi aap nange pair use chhoote hai, current aapke zariye ground ho jata hai.  
👉 [Earthing Kya Hai? Ghar Mein Earthing Kaise Kare (2026 Guide)](/articles/earthing-kya-hai/)

### 2. Galat Polarity: Switch Neutral Par Lag Jana
**IS 732:2019 (Clause 5.3.2.1)** aur IEC rules ke mutabiq single-pole switch hamesha **Phase (Line Conductor)** par lagna chahiye. Kai untrained electrician switch ko galti se Neutral par jod dete hai. Iska nuksan yeh hota hai ki switch OFF karne par bhi socket ya bulb holder ke andar 230V Phase wire zinda (live) rehta hai. Bulb badalte waqt ya pin nikalte waqt switch band hone par bhi lethal shock lag jata hai.

### 3. Geeli Skin Aur Bathroom-Kitchen Ka Risk
Sookhi skin bijli ke current ke liye high resistance deti hai. Lekin geeli skin hone par body resistance gir kar 1,000 ohm se bhi kam ho jata hai. Yahi wajah hai ki bathroom ke geyser tap, cooler mein paani bharte waqt ya washing machine ke paas lagne wala jhatka bohot ghatak hota hai.  
👉 [Cooler Kharidne Se Pehle Kya Dhyan De? (2026 Buying Guide)](/articles/cooler-kharidne-se-pehle-kya-dhyan-de/)  
👉 [Geyser Lagane Se Pehle Kya Dhyan Rakhe?](/articles/geyser-lagane-se-pehle-kya-dhyan-rakhe/)

### 4. Kharab Insulation Aur Sasti/Patli Wires
Bina ISI mark wali sasti wire par jab heavy appliance (geyser, AC, room heater) chalte hai, to wire heat hokar andar se pighal jati hai. Pighla hua phase conductor deewar ke metal conduit pipe ya switchboard ke steel screw ko charge kar deta hai.  
👉 [House Wiring Wire Size Chart: Kaunsi Wire Best Hai? (2026)](/articles/ghar-ki-wiring-wire-size-guide/)

### 5. Purane Appliances Aur Internal Vibration
10–12 saal purane iron, cooler pump ya water motor ke lagatar vibration se unke internal connection dheele ho jate hai. Loose contact se sparking hoti hai aur phase wire internal frame se touch ho jata hai.

### 6. Sasti Extension Cord Aur 2-Pin Ka Khatarnak Jugaad
Badi metal body wale upkaran (jaise microwave, fridge) ko 2-pin adaptor ya bina earthing wali cheap extension board par chalana hadse ko dawat dena hai, kyunki 2-pin plug mein leakage current nikalne ka koi third pin nahi hota.

### 7. Low-Height Open Sockets Aur Bachche
Deewar par 1 se 2 foot ki unchai par lage bina shutter wale open sockets mein bachche aksar hairpin, safety pin ya key daal dete hai. Ghar mein hamesha **child-safety shutter type sockets** hi lagwane chahiye.

### 8. Live Wire Par Direct Kaam Karna
Bina main switch band kiye live board kholna, socket replace karna ya nange taar ko hath lagana sidha shock ka kaaran banta hai.

## Current Ka Human Body Par Asar

![Electric current ka manav sharir par asar IEC 60479 chart aur mA safety limit](/images/electric-current-effects-on-human-body-iec-60479.webp)
*IEC 60479-1 Infographic: 1mA se 100mA+ electric current ka sharir par asar, current path aur 30mA RCCB trip safety limit.*

Human body par current ka asar voltage se nahi, balki shareer se kitne **milliampere (mA)** current pass hua aur kitni der tak hua, ispar depend karta hai (Standard: **IEC 60479-1**):

| Current (mA) | Sharir Par Asar | Practical Matlab |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0.5 – 2 mA** | Halka jhanjhanahat mehsoos hona | Threshold of perception (current mehsoos hota hai) |
| **5 – 10 mA** | Tezz jhatka, sharir peeche hat jata hai | Involuntary muscle reaction (haath chhoot jata hai) |
| **10 – 20 mA** | **"Cannot-let-go"** — Manspeshiyan jam jana | Muscles contract ho jati hai, vyakti taar ko chhod nahi pata |
| **30 mA** | Saans lene mein takleef aur muscle freeze | **Critical Line:** Domestic RCCB 30mA par trip hona mandatory hai |
| **50 – 100 mA+** | Heart fibrillation (dil ki beat ruk jana) | **Lethal Shock:** Turant cardiac arrest aur maut ka khatra |

### Shock Se Pehle Ke Warning Signs
In cheezon ko kabhi ignore na kare:
- Appliance ko chhoote hi ungliyon mein vibration ya tingling hona.
- Tester lagane par metal body par bulb ka halka glow hona.
- DB box mein lagi RCCB ka bina kisi wajah ke baar-baar trip hona.
- Board ya switchboard se halki plastic jalne ki smell aana.
- Barish ya nami ke mausam mein appliances se current aane lagna.

## Static Shock Aur Asli Electric Shock Mein Antar

Bahut se log sardiyon mein car ke handle ya sweater se lagne wale jhatke se dar jate hai:

- **Static Shock (Gharshan / Friction):** Kapde ya synthetic seats par friction se banta hai. Isme voltage hazaron volt tak ja sakta hai, par current microamperes mein aur second ke hazarwe hisse (microseconds) ke liye hota hai. Yeh dil ya sharir ko nuksan nahi pahunchata.
- **Mains Electric Shock (230V AC Supply):** Yeh ghar ki live bijli line se lagta hai. Jab tak breaker trip na ho ya switch band na ho, continuous current flow karta rehta hai jo internal organs ko jala deta hai aur cardiac arrest ka kaaran banta hai.

<a id="part-2-shock-lagne-par-turant-first-aid"></a>
## Part 2: Shock Lagne Par Turant First Aid (करंट लगने पर क्या करें)

Pehle 5 minute mein ghabrayein nahi, in sequential steps ko follow kare:

### Kadam 1: Supply Band kare (Insulation Rescue)
- **Sabse Pehla Kaam:** Main switch, DB isolator ya appliance ka plug kheench dein. Agar ghar mein solar ya inverter hai, to inverter ka output switch bhi band kare taaki backup supply cut ho jaye. (Padhein: [Solar Inverter vs Normal Inverter](/articles/solar-inverter-vs-normal-inverter/))
- **Agar Switch Door Ho:** Kisi bhi soorat mein victim ko nange hath se na chhooein! Sukhi lakdi ka danda, mota plastic pipe ya sukhe blanket se victim ko live taar se alag kare.
- **Kya Na kare:** Geeli jagah par nange pair daud kar na jayein, lohe ka rod ya geela kapda use na kare.

### Agar High Tension / Toota Hua Wire Ho
Deewar ya sadak par gire toote hue live wire se kam se kam **6 meter (20 feet)** door rahein. Ground voltage gradient (step potential) ki wajah se paas jane par zameen ke raste current lag sakta hai. Turant DISCOM aur 112 ko call kare.

### Kadam 2: Emergency Call Lagayein
Turant **112 ya 108** dial kare aur phone speaker par daal dein, taaki ambulance aane tak aap paramedical instructions follow kar sakein.

### Kadam 3: Victim Ki Halat Check kare
- **Agar Hosh Mein Hai:** Seedha litayein, kapde dheele kare, shaant rakhein. Paani peene ko na dein.
- **Agar Behosh Hai Par Saans Chal Rahi Hai:** Use **Recovery Position** (ek karwat par) litayein taaki tongue ya vomit se saans ki nali block na ho.
- **Agar Behosh Hai Aur Saans/Pulse Gayab Hai:** Ek second bhi barbad kiye bina Hands-Only CPR shuru kare.

<a id="hands-only-cpr"></a>
### Hands-Only CPR Kaise kare
![Electric shock lagne par first aid aur hands only CPR step by step emergency flowchart](/images/electric-shock-first-aid-hands-only-cpr-guide.webp)
*Emergency Flowchart: Electric shock lagne par safe rescue (MCB OFF/lakdi se alag karna), 112/108 call aur Hands-Only CPR dene ke 3 mukhya kadam.*
1. Victim ko sakht aur flat zameen par peeth ke bal litayein.
2. Apni ek hatheli chhati ke bilkul center (breastbone) par rakhein, doosra hath upar lock kare.
3. Koni seedhi rakhein aur apne body weight se chhati ko **5 cm (2 inch)** tak neeche press kare.
4. Speed: **100 se 120 compressions per minute** (lagbhag har second mein do baar).
5. Tab tak continue kare jab tak ambulance na aa jaye ya victim hosh mein na aa jaye.

### Electrical Burns Ka Sahi Upchar
- Current nikalne wali jagah (exit wound) aur enter hone wali jagah par deep burn ho sakta hai.
- Jale huye hisse par **cool running tap water** 20 minute tak lagatar daale.
- Saaf sterile non-stick bandage se halka cover kare.
- **Strictly Don't:** Barf (ice), toothpaste, ghee, tel, haldi ya raw potato bilkul na lagayein. Chipke huye kapde ko jhatke se na kheenchein.

### Halka Shock Lagne Par Bhi Doctor Ko Dikhana Kyun Zaroori Hai?
Medical science aur cardiology guidelines ke mutabiq, AC current body se guzarne par dil ke natural electrical rhythm ko disturb kar sakta hai jise **delayed arrhythmia** kaha jata hai. Shock ke 2 se 4 ghante baad achanak heartbeat irregular ho sakti hai. Isliye ECG test karwana hamesha safe rehta hai.

## Part 3: Short Circuit Se Aag Lagne Par Kya kare? (शॉर्ट सर्किट से आग)

### Short Circuit, Overload Aur Loose Connection Ka Fark
- **Overload:** Circuit par taar ki capacity se zyada current khinchta hai (jaise 1 sq mm wire par 2 AC chalana). Wire dheere-dheere garam hoti hai aur MCB time lekar trip hoti hai. (Padhein: [Home Load Calculation](/articles/home-electrical-load-calculation/) aur [MCB Selection Guide](/articles/mcb-types-selection-guide/))
- **Short Circuit:** Phase aur Neutral aapas mein direct touch ho jate hai. Resistance lagbhag zero ho jata hai aur hazaron ampere current flow hota hai. MCB fraction of second mein instant magnetic trip hoti hai.
- **Loose Connection (Sabse Khatarnak):** Terminal screw dheela rehne se continuous micro-sparking (arcing) hoti hai. Yahan current MCB ki rating se kam hota hai, isliye **MCB trip nahi hoti**, par terminal 400°C se upar garam hokar aag pakad leta hai.

### Pehle 60 Seconds Ka Action Plan
1. **Supply Cut-Off:** Safe ho to main DB panel se supply band kare.
2. **Paani Kabhi Na Daalein:** Live wiring par paani daalna sidha lethal shock invite karta hai.

## BIS Fire Classes Aur Sahi Extinguisher Ka Chunav

### Bharat Mein Aag Ki Classes (IS 2190:2024 Standard)

| Class | Aag Ka Type | Material Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Class A** | Solid Combustibles | Lakdi, kagaz, kapda, plastic |
| **Class B** | Flammable Liquids | Petrol, diesel, thinner, paint |
| **Class C** | Flammable Gases | LPG, PNG, methane |
| **Class D** | Combustible Metals | Magnesium, aluminium powder |
| **Class F** | Cooking Oils / Fats | Kitchen ki kadhai ka commercial tel |

> **Note On Electrical Fire:** BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) ke IS 2190 standard ke tahat bijli ki aag koi alag "Class" nahi hoti (purane zamane mein log ise Class E kehte the). Bijli ek ignition source hai jo Class A, B ya C aag ko jala sakti hai. Isliye extinguisher par **"Suitable for Live Electrical Equipment"** ka marking check karna zaroori hai.

### Kaun Sa Fire Extinguisher Chunein?

Bijli ki aag bujhane ke liye sahi extinguisher ka chunav karna behad zaroori hai, kyunki galat type ka extinguisher chalana ulta jaanleva ho sakta hai:

* **Carbon Dioxide (CO2) Extinguisher — Sabse Best (First Choice):** Yeh live electrical safety ke liye sabse surakshit mana jata hai. Yeh koi residue ya powder nahi chhodta, jisse costly circuits aur appliances safe rehte hai.

* **ABC Dry Powder (MAP) — Effective & Multipurpose:** Yeh aag ko turant kabu kar leta hai aur multipurpose use ke liye effective hai. Lekin iska white powder residue panel aur electronics ko kharab kar sakta hai.

* **Water / Water Mist — Khatarnak (STRICT NO):** Normal paani current conduct karta hai, isliye live supply par paani ya water extinguisher ka istemal kabhi na karein.

* **Foam Type (AFFF) — Khatarnak (STRICT NO):** Foam based liquid conductive hota hai, isliye live electrical panel par foam extinguisher chalana bilkul mana hai.

### Extinguisher Chalane Ka PASS Formula
- **P (Pull):** Handle par lagi plastic seal todkar safety pin kheenchein.
- **A (Aim):** Pipe/Horn ka munh lapton ke upar nahi, balki aag ki **Jad (Base)** par point kare.
- **S (Squeeze):** Lever ko poora dabayein.
- **S (Sweep):** 1.5 se 2 meter door khade hokar nozzle ko left-se-right jhadu ki tarah sweep kare.

## Part 4: Ghar Ko Safe Kaise Rakhein (Prevention)

### MCB, RCCB Aur Earthing Ka Tri-Layer Protection

| Safety Device | Kisko Bachata Hai? | Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **MCB (Circuit Breaker)** | **Ghar Ki Wiring & Appliances** | High current aur short circuit aane par supply kaatna |
| **RCCB (Safety Switch)** | **Insaan Ki Zindagi (Human Body)** | 30mA leakage current detect hote hi 300ms ke andar trip hona |
| **Earthing (Earth Pit)** | **System Stability & RCCB Helper** | Leakage voltage ko low resistance path dekar ground karna |

**Important Fact:** MCB insaan ko electric shock se nahi bacha sakti! 16A ki MCB tab trip hogi jab 16 ampere se zyada current guzrega, jabki insaan ka dil sirf 0.05 ampere (50mA) par hi fail ho jata hai. Isliye DB box mein 30mA RCCB hona mandatory hai.  
👉 [Ghar Mein RCCB Lagana Kyun Zaroori Hai? Full Guide](/articles/ghar-mein-rccb-lagana-kyun-zaruri-hai/)

### Electrical Maintenance Checklist

Ghar ki wiring aur breakers ko lambe samay tak safe rakhne ke liye routine maintenance calendar follow karein:

* **Har Mahine (Monthly Check):** Distribution board mein lagi RCCB ka **'T' (Test) Button** dabakar test karein. Button dabate hi breaker ko turant snap-trip hona chahiye. Saath hi fire extinguisher ka needle pressure gauge check karein ki wo green zone mein hai ya nahi.

* **Har 3 Mahine (Quarterly Check):** Ghar ke heavy power sockets (jaise AC, geyser, washing machine) aur main distribution board ke internal screw terminals ki tightness check karein. Dheele terminal arcing aur aag ka sabse bada kaaran bante hain.

* **Saal Mein Ek Baar (Annual Check):** Digital Earth Clamp Meter ya Earth Tester se earth pit ka resistance test karwaye. Surakshit ghar ke liye **earth resistance ki value 5 Ohm se kam** honi chahiye. Saath hi fire extinguisher ki service inspection aur expiry date verify karwaye.

## Part 5: Field Case Studies (Real Site Reports)

*Field experience se nikle asli ground reality cases jo theoretical kitabon mein nahi milte:*

### Case 1: Khalilabad Site Par Geyser Tap Se 85V Ka Leakage Shock
Pichle saal Rohtak ki ek residential kothi mein geyser chalane par steel tap (nal) chhoone par tezz jhatka lag raha tha.

- **Field Inspection:** Jab humne Earth Tester se ground pit check kiya, toh resistance **48 Ohm** mila (jo standard 5 Ohm se bohot zyada tha). Bathroom ke switchboard tak aane wala 1.0 sq mm earth wire andar conduit pipe mein chuhon ke kaatne se open ho chuka tha. Geyser ke 2kW heating element ki magnesium oxide insulation leak ho chuki thi, jisse nal aur geeli tile ke beech multimeter se **85V AC** leakage voltage record hua!
- **Field Solution:** Naya 2.5 sq mm multi-strand copper earth wire pipe ke andar pull kiya gaya, earth pit mein chemical compound daalkar resistance 2.8 Ohm par laya gaya, aur main DB mein **30mA RCCB** lagaya gaya. Leakage hote hi circuit ne trip hona shuru kiya aur geyser element replace karke customer ka ghar 100% safe hua.
- **Field Rule:** Geyser jaisi heavy body ke liye 1 sq mm nahi balki dedicated 2.5 sq mm circuit wire honi chahiye. (Padhein: [Geyser Installation Guide 2026](/articles/geyser-lagane-se-pehle-kya-dhyan-rakhe/))

### Case 2: Festive Season Mein Neutral Loose Hone Se Distribution Panel Me Aag
Diwali ke dauran ek 3-storey building mein decorative lighting aur halogen ka heavy load direct DB board par connect kar diya gaya.

- **Field Inspection:** 2 ghante load chalne ke baad distribution board se aag ke gole aur kaala dhuan nikalne laga. Site inspection par paaya gaya ki neutral busbar ka main terminal screw loose tha. Loose connection ke kaaran high-resistance arcing hui, jisse terminal ka temperature 450°C se upar chala gaya aur PVC wiring ne aag pakad li.
- **Field Rule:** Loose connection par MCB kabhi trip nahi hoti, kyunki load current capacity ke andar hi rehta hai. Isliye heavy seasonal lighting se pehle insulated torque screwdriver se panels ke saare terminals tight karna anivarya hai.

## Part 6: Indian Standards Aur CEA Regulations 2023

Indian electrical safety laws aur BIS codes strictly residential installations ko guide karte hai. In niyam aur standards ka seedha matlab aapke ghar ki suraksha se juda hai:

* **CEA Regulations 2023 (Regulation 43 & 44):** Har residential building ki bijli supply **Residual Current Device (RCD/RCCB)** se hokar guzarna kanooni roop se zaroori hai, jiska operating residual current **30mA** se zyada nahi hona chahiye. Saath hi, nayi supply shuru karne se pehle 500V DC Megger se poori wiring ka insulation resistance test hona anivarya hai.

* **IS 732:2019 (IEC 60364 Aligned):** Yeh building wiring ka mukhya code of practice hai. Iske mutabiq single-pole control switch hamesha Phase (Line) wire par hi lagna chahiye taaki switch band hone par appliance mein current na rahe. Isme standard wire color coding bhi tay hai: Phase ke liye Red/Yellow/Blue, Neutral ke liye Black, aur Earth wire ke liye Green/Yellow.

* **IS 12640-1:2016 (IEC 61008-1):** Yeh gharon mein lagne wale RCCB ka standard hai. Is niyam ke hisaab se, agar circuit mein 30mA ka fault current aata hai, toh breaker ko **maximum 300 ms (0.3 second)** ke andar trip ho jana chahiye taaki insaan ko jaanleva shock na lage.

* **IS 3043:2018:** Yeh earthing ka official code of practice hai. Residential installation ke liye earth electrode ka resistance aam taur par **1 se 5 Ohm** ke andar maintain rakhna zaroori hota hai, taaki leakage current bina rukawat zameen mein chala jaye.

* **IS 2190:2024:** Yeh fire extinguisher chunne, sahi unchai par lagane aur har mahine unki service/pressure gauge inspection karne ke practical niyam batata hai.

## Kab Electrician Bulayein?

Inme se ek bhi lakshan dikhe toh kisi professional electrician ko turant call kare:
- Metal appliance (cooler, washing machine, geyser) par tester glow kare ya haath lagane par vibratory shock lage.
- Distribution box ka RCCB reset karne ke bawjood turant bar-bar trip ho raha ho.
- Switch ya socket ke plug pins pighal rahe hon ya kale nishan pad gaye hon.
- Switchboard se chidiya ki chahchahat jaisi arcing crackling awaaz sunai de.
- Ghar ki wiring ko lage 15 saal se zyada ho gaye hon aur earth pit kabhi check na hua ho.

## Aksar Pooche Jane Wale Sawal (FAQs)

### 1. Electric shock kyun lagta hai?
Jab kisi appliance mein wiring insulation cut ho jata hai ya earthing disconnected hoti hai, to current device ki body mein aa jata hai. Zameen par nange pair khade hokar use chhoone par hamara sharir closed circuit ban jata hai aur current hamare through ground hone lagta hai.

### 2. Electric shock lagne par victim ko direct haath se kyun nahi chhoona chahiye?
Jab tak victim live wire ya appliance ke contact mein hai, uska poora shareer charged rehta hai. Use nange haath pakadne par rescuer ke sharir se bhi current pass hone lagega aur dono ko fatal shock lagega. Hamesha lakdi ya PVC pipe se alag kare.

### 3. Switch band hone par bhi shock kyun lagta hai?
Agar switch Phase wire ke bajaye Neutral par laga ho (Reverse Polarity), to switch OFF karne par bhi wire mein 230V live current barkarar rehta hai. Iske alawa weak earthing hone par doosre appliances ka leakage neutral/body ke zariye shock de sakta hai.

### 4. Bijli ki aag par paani kyun nahi daalna chahiye?
Normal supply paani mein ghule minerals ki wajah se highly conductive hota hai. Live electrical panel par paani dalne se paani ki dhar ek live conductor ban jati hai aur paani phenkne wale ko deadly 230V/415V ka lethal shock lag jata hai.

### 5. Bijli ki aag ke liye kaun sa extinguisher chunein?
Sabse behtar Carbon Dioxide (CO2) extinguisher hota hai kyunki yeh non-conductive gas chhodta hai aur koi residue nahi chhodta. Dusra vikalp ABC Dry Powder extinguisher hai jo sabhi aag par kaam karta hai par iska powder electronics ko jam kar deta hai.

### 6. Kya MCB insaan ko shock se bacha sakti hai?
Nahi. MCB sirf wiring ko over-current aur short circuit se bachati hai (jaise 10A, 16A, 32A). Insaan ka sharir sirf 30mA (0.03A) par gambhir roop se behosh ho sakta hai. Insaan ko shock se bachane ke liye sirf **RCCB (Residual Current Circuit Breaker)** kaam karta hai.

### 7. Shock lagne ke baad doctor ko dikhana kyun zaruri hai?
Halka jhatka lagne par bhi internal tissues burn ho sakte hai aur sabse bada khatra **cardiac arrhythmia** (dil ki aniyamit dhadkan) ka hota hai jo ghanthon baad cardiac arrest ban sakta hai. Ek basic ECG checkup karwana behad zaroori hai.

### 8. Electrical burn par kya lagayein aur kya nahi?
Jali hui jagah par lagatar 20 minute tak normal taja behata paani (running cool tap water) daalein. Kabhi bhi barf (ice), toothpaste, desi ghee, burnol ya tel na lagayein, isse infection badhta hai aur heat andar trap ho jati hai.

### 9. Switchboard mein sparking aur jalne ki smell aaye to kya kare?
Ghabrayein nahi, turant sabse pehle main MCB isolator band kare. Us board par lage kisi bhi switch ya plug ko haath na lagayein aur kisi certified electrician se terminals ki tightness aur insulation check karwayein.

### 10. Fire extinguisher ki jaanch kab karni chahiye?
IS 2190 ke mutabiq fire extinguisher ka pressure gauge har mahine check hona chahiye (needle green zone mein honi chahiye). Saal mein ek baar authorized agency se service inspection aur cylinder expiry verify honi chahiye.

## Conclusion: Electrician Ki Seedhi Salah

Ghar ke interior aur appliances par lakhon kharch karne ke sath-sath switchboard ke peeche chhupe circuit protection par dhyan dena zindagi bachata hai. Halka sa jhatka aana ya terminal loose hona koi aam baat nahi, yeh aane wale bade hadse ka pehla warning sign hai. Apne DB panel mein 30mA RCCB anivarya roop se lagwayein, earth pit ka resistance har saal test karwayein, aur kisi bhi electrical aag mein pehle supply band karke emergency help bulayein.
