## <a id="units"></a>14.2 kg LPG Cylinder Ke Barabar Induction Ko Kitni Bijli Lagti Hai?

### Step 1: Cylinder me kitni energy hoti hai
LPG ki calorific value lagbhag **45.8 MJ/kg** maanein to:
- 14.2 kg × 45.8 MJ/kg = **650.4 MJ**
- 1 kWh = 3.6 MJ → 650.4 ÷ 3.6 ≈ **180.7 kWh**

### Step 2: Gas stove cooking me kitni heat deta hai
Gas stove ki saari garmi bartan me nahi jati. Standard domestic LPG stove ki thermal efficiency aam taur par **55–57%** (BIS IS 4246 standard ke kareeb) hoti hai. **56%** maanein to:
- 180.7 × 0.56 ≈ **101.2 kWh useful cooking heat**

### Step 3: Induction ko kitni bijli chahiye
Induction ki illustrative efficiency **84%** maanein to:
- 101.2 ÷ 0.84 ≈ **120.5 units**

### Efficiency badalne par nateeja kaise badalta hai?

| LPG stove ki efficiency | Useful heat | Induction ko units | Break-even (LPG ₹942) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 50% | 90.3 kWh | ~107.5 | ~₹8.76/unit |
| **56% (hamara base)** | **101.2 kWh** | **~120.5** | **~₹7.82/unit** |
| 65% | 117.4 kWh | ~139.8 | ~₹6.74/unit |

Matlab: agar aapka burner zyada efficient hai, to LPG aur sasta padega. Ye **laboratory-style estimate** hai, har ghar ki asli khapat nahi. Bartan ka size, dhakkan ka use, flame aur khana banane ka tareeka result badal dete hain.

## <a id="hourly"></a>Induction 1 Ghante Me Kitni Bijli Khata Hai?

**Units = Power (kW) × Ghante**

| Induction power | 30 minute full power | 1 ghanta full power |
|---|---|---|
| 1200 W | 0.6 unit | 1.2 unit |
| 1600 W | 0.8 unit | 1.6 unit |
| 1800 W | 0.9 unit | 1.8 unit |
| 2000 W | 1.0 unit | 2.0 unit |
| 2200 W | 1.1 unit | 2.2 unit |

Ye **maximum** (lagatar full power) ka hisaab hai. Normal cooking me induction aksar poori power par nahi chalta, isliye **average khapat kam** hoti hai. **Kitni kam, ye bina napey tay nahi hota**, isliye fixed percentage maan lena sahi nahi.

### Apne induction ki asli khapat kaise napein
1. Sahi **rating wala energy meter/plug-in meter** (16A, 2.5 kW se upar ka) lagayein. Normal 6A plug-in meter par 1800–2000W induction na chalayein.
2. Ek normal cooking session ke pehle aur baad meter ki reading note karein.
3. Units ko ghanton se divide karke **average kW** nikalein.

**Example (illustrative):** 30 minute me 0.65 unit aaye to average = 0.65 × 2 = **1.3 kW**. Agar roz 2 ghante is average par chale to 1.3 × 2 × 30 = **78 units/mahina.**

## <a id="infrared"></a>Induction vs Infrared Cooktop: Kya Fark Hai?

Infrared (radiant/ceramic) cooktop ek **electric heating element** se garmi banata hai, jo glass top ke zariye bartan tak pahunchti hai. Induction me bartan khud **magnetic field** se garam hota hai.

| Comparison | Induction | Infrared (radiant) |
|---|---|---|
| Heating technology | Electromagnetic induction | Radiant heating element |
| Efficiency (approx) | Aam taur par 80–90% | Aam taur par 65–75% |
| Heating speed | Tez | Thodi dheemi |
| Bijli ki khapat | Aam taur par kam | Aam taur par zyada |
| Bartan | Magnetic base chahiye | Zyada tar flat-bottom bartan (aluminium bhi) |
| Surface | Kam garam (bartan se) | Surface bahut garam, der tak garam rehti hai |
| Control | Tez response | Dheema response |
| Safety | Relatively safer | Hot surface se jalne ka risk |

*Efficiency approximate hai. Asli performance model, bartan aur cooking par depend karti hai.*

### Infrared cooktop kitni bijli khata hai?
2000 W infrared lagatar full power par 1 ghanta chale to **2 units** lagti hain. Par ye **maximum** hai, asli cooking khapat nahi. **Same cooking heat ke liye infrared ko induction se zyada units lagti hain**, isliye infrared ko induction ke barabar units maan kar calculation na karein.

**Illustrative hisaab:** 101.2 kWh useful heat ke liye
- Induction (84%): ~**120.5 units**
- Infrared (75% / 70% / 65%): ~**135 / 145 / 156 units**

### Kya infrared LPG se sasta hai?
Aam taur par **sirf kam bijli rate par.** LPG ₹942 par infrared ka break-even rate lagbhag **₹6–7/unit** (70% par ~₹6.5) aata hai, jabki induction ka lagbhag **₹7.8/unit.**

### Kaunsa kharidein?
- **Induction:** kam bijli aur tez cooking ke liye.
- **Infrared:** jab aluminium ya non-magnetic bartan hi use karne hon.
- **LPG:** power cut aur traditional phulka ke liye.

> Infrared cooktop **electric** hota hai. “Infrared gas burner” alag cheez hai, dono ko mix na karein.

## <a id="cost"></a>Induction, Infrared Aur LPG: Monthly Cost Comparison

### LPG cylinder ka rate (apne area ka dekhein)
- **Delhi:** ₹942 (indicative non-subsidized rate)
- **Lucknow:** lagbhag ₹979.50 (indicative rate)
- **PM Ujjwala (Delhi):** effective lagbhag **₹642** (₹300 subsidy ke baad)

Domestic LPG ka rate mahine ke hisaab se revise hota hai aur state/VAT ke saath badalta hai.

### Ek cylinder-equivalent cooking ka kharcha (LPG ₹942 par)

| Bijli rate | Induction (~120.5 units) | Infrared @70% (~145 units) | LPG ₹942 | Induction vs LPG |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ₹6/unit | ₹723 | ₹867 | ₹942 | ₹219 sasta |
| ₹7/unit | ₹844 | ₹1,012 | ₹942 | ₹98 sasta |
| ₹8/unit | ₹964 | ₹1,156 | ₹942 | ₹22 mehnga |
| ₹9/unit | ₹1,085 | ₹1,301 | ₹942 | ₹143 mehnga |

### Break-even bijli rate (jahan kharcha barabar ho)
**Break-even = LPG cylinder ka daam ÷ units**

| LPG cylinder | Induction (120.5 units) | Infrared @70% (145 units) |
|---|---|---|
| ₹942 (Delhi) | ~₹7.82/unit | ~₹6.5/unit |
| ₹979.50 (Lucknow) | ~₹8.13/unit | ~₹6.8/unit |
| ₹642 (Ujjwala effective) | ~₹5.33/unit | ~₹4.4/unit |

> **Ujjwala wale ghar me** induction aksar mehnga padta hai, kyunki cylinder pehle se subsidy ke saath sasta hai. Ye table me iske bina chhut jata hai, isliye apna effective rate zaroor jodein.

**Is table me nahi judta:** induction ya infrared cooktop ki price, naye bartan, LPG stove ki price aur bijli ke fixed charge.

## <a id="slab"></a>Marginal Rate Aur Slab Jump: Sabse Aam Galti

Induction ke units **aapke bill ke upar jude** extra units hote hain, isliye unka rate wo hota hai jo aapke **agle slab** ka ho (aur uske upar duty/surcharge). Slab kaise lagta hai ye jaanne ke liye: [slab aur bill ke hisaab ko samajhne ka tareeka](/articles/bijli-ka-bill-zyada-kyun-aata-hai/).

### Illustrative example (kisi state ka tariff nahi)
Maan lijiye slabs: 0–150 units ₹5, 151–300 units ₹6.5, 301+ units ₹8. Ghar ka khapat 220 units aur induction ke 120 units jude:
- Pehli 80 units (220→300): 80 × ₹6.5 = ₹520
- Baaki 40 units (300→340): 40 × ₹8 = ₹320
- **Induction ki 120 units ka average = (₹520 + ₹320) ÷ 120 = ₹7.0/unit**

Aapki asli state ke slab alag honge. **Apne bill se agla slab dekhkar marginal rate nikalein**, sirf total units ya average rate se faisla na lein.

## <a id="png"></a>Agar Aapke Ilake Me PNG (Piped Gas) Hai

PNG (CGD company ki piped natural gas) kai shehron me LPG se alag rate par milti hai. Hisaab ka tareeka wahi hai: **PNG ka per-unit rate** (SCM me) aur uski calorific value se **per kWh heat ka daam** nikalkar induction ke **per kWh heat ke daam** (bijli rate ÷ 0.84) se milayein. Apne CGD provider ka current rate aur calorific value unke bill/website se lein, mai yahan koi number nahi de raha.

## <a id="pros-cons"></a>Induction, Infrared Aur LPG: Fayde Aur Nuksan

| | Fayde | Nuksan |
|---|---|---|
| **Induction** | Efficient, kam heat kitchen me, open flame nahi | Bijli chahiye, magnetic bartan, electronics (PCB/IGBT) kharab ho sakte hain, power cut me band |
| **Infrared** | Zyada bartan chalte hain, aluminium bhi | Zyada bijli, surface bahut garam, dheema response |
| **LPG** | Power cut me bhi chalta hai, sab bartan, phulka aasaan | Cylinder ka kharcha, leak ka risk, kitchen me garmi |

### Hybrid approach (practical)
Chai, doodh, dal-chawal aur sabzi induction par, aur phulka/roti ya power cut ke liye LPG backup. **Hybrid se cylinder kitne din chalega ye family aur cooking pattern par depend karta hai**, isliye apna khud ka record rakhein.

### Induction ke saath 3 aur baatein
- **Pacemaker:** induction ke paas magnetic field hota hai. Pacemaker ya medical implant wale log **doctor aur cooktop ke manufacturer ki salah** ke bina paas na khade hon.
- **Bartan:** magnet bartan ke neeche chipakta ho to wo aam taur par induction par chalega. Aluminium aur copper (bina magnetic base) nahi chalte.
- **Voltage ki dikkat:** voltage kam-zyada hone par electronics ko nuksan ho sakta hai, isliye kitchen line me loose connection ya neutral fault na rehne dein.

## <a id="wiring"></a>Induction Ke Liye Kitchen Wiring Aur Safety

![Induction cooktop 16A power socket and 2.5 sq mm copper wire kitchen safety diagram](/images/induction-kitchen-wiring-16a-safety.webp)

Induction ya infrared ek **high-power appliance** hai. Purani wiring ya loose socket par chalane se overheating aur aag ka risk badh jata hai.

### Kitna current leta hai? (Current = Watt ÷ 230 V)

| Power | Approx current |
|---|---|
| 1200 W | 5.2 A |
| 1600 W | 7.0 A |
| 1800 W | 7.8 A |
| 2000 W | 8.7 A |
| 2200 W | 9.6 A |

Isliye 1800–2200 W ke liye **6A socket theek nahi.**

### Aam taur par kya lagwayein (final confirm electrician se)
- **Wire:** aam taur par **2.5 mm² copper** (FR/FRLS, ISI). Wire ki lambai, grouping aur voltage drop ke hisaab se kuch jagah 4 mm² bhi lagti hai. Size ka chart: [kitchen line ke liye wire ka size](/articles/ghar-ki-wiring-wire-size-guide/).
- **Socket:** appliance ke plug ke hisaab se, aam taur par **16A socket** jo achhi quality ka ho. 6A socket me conversion plug na lagayein, aur saste extension board par na chalayein.
- **MCB:** aam taur par alag line par **16A MCB.** Rating wire aur load ke hisaab se electrician tay karta hai. [dedicated line ke liye MCB ki rating](/articles/mcb-types-selection-guide/).
- **RCCB (30 mA):** shock ke risk ko kam karti hai, rated leakage par aam taur par max ~300 ms me trip hoti hai. [RCCB ka kaam aur trip hone ki wajah](/articles/ghar-mein-rccb-lagana-kyun-zaruri-hai/). RCCB overload ya short circuit ka bachav **nahi** karti.
- **Earthing:** induction ki metal body aur socket ka earth connection theek aur tight hona chahiye.[socket ki earthing kaise check hoti hai](/articles/earthing-kya-hai/).

### Sanctioned load check karein
Agar sanctioned load 2 kW hai aur induction (2 kW), geyser (2 kW) aur AC saath chalte hain, to demand limit se upar ja sakti hai (MCB trip ya kuch DISCOM me load-related rule). Apna load jodein: [sanctioned load aur connected load ka fark](/articles/home-electrical-load-calculation/).

## <a id="solar"></a>Induction Ko Inverter Aur Solar Par Chalana

### Home inverter par
Aam 800–1100 VA inverter par 1800 W induction **nahi chalta.** Sirf VA dekhna kaafi nahi: **continuous output watts, power factor, battery discharge aur waveform** dekhein, aur **manufacturer se compatibility confirm karein.** “3 kVA hai to chalega hi” ye maan lena sahi nahi.

### Solar par
Din ke solar generation se cooking ka bijli kharcha kam ho sakta hai, par **“bilkul free” nahi.** Bachat solar size, cooking ke samay, net metering aur fixed charges par depend karti hai. ## <a id="cases"></a>Typical Field Scenarios (Aam Taur Par Aise Cases Milte Hain)

> Ye **typical scenarios** hain jo kitchen wiring me aksar milte hain, kisi khaas ghar ka dava nahi.

### Scenario 1: Naye induction ke baad socket se jalne ki badboo
**Shikayat:** 2 kW induction lene ke kuch din baad socket garam hota hai aur plug ke pin par kale nishan aate hain.  
**Kya milta hai:** Kitchen ki purani line **patli wire** ki hoti hai aur socket ke terminal **loose**, isliye contact resistance badhta hai aur garmi (I²R) banti hai.  
**Fix:** DB se **alag 2.5 mm² line, achha 16A socket aur sahi MCB.** Sirf socket badalna kaafi nahi hota agar peeche ki wire hi galat ho.

### Scenario 2: Induction ON karte hi RCCB trip
**Shikayat:** Induction chalate hi RCCB trip.  
**Kya milta hai:** Kabhi induction ke andar **nami ya tel** se leakage, kabhi **damaged cord** ya socket ka **loose earth.**  
**Fix:** Insulation resistance aur earth ki jaanch, aur zarurat par induction ki service. **RCCB ko baar-baar reset karke chalate rehna solution nahi.**

### Scenario 3: Induction + geyser saath chalane par main MCB trip
**Shikayat:** Subah induction aur geyser ek saath chalate hi main MCB trip.  
**Kya milta hai:** Total load ghar ke MCB/sanctioned load se upar jata hai.  
**Fix:** Load ka hisaab, heavy load ki alag line, aur zarurat par load enhancement ya chalne ka samay badalna.

## <a id="is-cea"></a>IS Standards Aur CEA Regulations: Is Topic Me Kya Lagu Hai

| Reference | Is topic me matlab |
|---|---|
| **IS 732** | Wiring ka code of practice (design, installation, testing) |
| **IS 3043** | Earthing |
| **IS 12640 / IEC 61008** | RCCB. 30 mA RCCB rated leakage par aam taur par max ~300 ms me trip hoti hai |

## Aam Galtiyan Jo Log Induction Kharidate Aur Chalate Waqt Karte Hain

### 1. Sirf Wattage Dekhkar Poore Mahine Ka Bill Jod Lena
2000W likha dekhkar log seedha 2 units per hour maan lete hain, jabki khana banate waqt power setting badalti rehti hai. Andaza lagane ke bajay 16A plug-in energy meter se apne kitchen ki asli khapat napein.

### 2. Bill Ke Average Rate Se Faisla Karna (Slab Jump Bhool Jana)
Induction ki units aapke regular bill ke upar judti hain, isliye unpar agle mehngi slab ka rate aur surcharge lagta hai. Faisla lene se pehle apne bill me agla tariff slab zaroor dekhein.

### 3. PM Ujjwala Ya Sasti LPG Subsidy Ko Ignore Karna
Agar aapke ghar me Ujjwala subsidy wala cylinder (~₹642 effective) aata hai, toh bijli ka break-even rate ~₹5.3/unit par aa jata hai. Aise gharon me bijli se khana banana aksar gas se mehnga padta hai.

### 4. 6A Socket Ya Saste Extension Board Par Induction Chalana
1800–2000W ka induction lagbhag 8–9A current leta hai. 6A socket me conversion plug lagakar chalane se pin pighal sakti hai aur kitchen me aag lagne ka khatra rehta hai—hamesha dedicated 16A power socket hi lagwayein.

### 5. Inverter Ki Sirf VA Rating Dekhkar Induction Jod Dena
800VA ya 1100VA ke normal inverter par 1800W induction chalane se inverter turant overload hokar trip ya kharab ho sakta hai. Inverter par chalane se pehle continuous output watts aur battery discharge limit zaroor check karein.

## <a id="faqs"></a>Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawal (FAQs)

### 1. Kya induction chulha LPG gas se sasta padta hai?
Ye aapke marginal bijli rate, LPG cylinder ke daam aur cooking habit par depend karta hai. LPG ₹942 par break-even rate lagbhag ₹7.8/unit aata hai, yaani usse kam rate par induction aam taur par sasta padta hai.

### 2. 1800W induction 1 ghante me kitni bijli khata hai?
Lagatar full power par 1 ghanta chale to 1.8 units. Normal cooking me asli khapat power setting aur chalne ke pattern ke hisaab se kam ho sakti hai, isliye energy meter se napna behtar hai.

### 3. 2000W induction ka monthly bijli bill kitna aayega?
Agar roz 1 ghanta full power par chale to mahine me lagbhag 60 units. ₹8/unit par ye ₹480 hota hai. Asli khapat cooking ke pattern par depend karti hai.

### 4. Kya induction ko 6A socket par chala sakte hain?
Nahi. 1800–2000 W induction lagbhag 8–9 A leta hai. 6A socket ya patli wire wale extension board par plug pighal sakta hai. Appliance ke plug ke hisaab se achha 16A socket use karein.

### 5. Induction ke liye kaunsi wire aur MCB lagwayein?
Aam taur par 2.5 mm² copper wire aur alag 16A MCB. Final size wire ki lambai, temperature, grouping aur voltage drop par depend karta hai, isliye electrician se confirm karwayein.

### 6. Kya induction inverter par chal sakta hai?
Suitable inverter par chal sakta hai, par continuous watt capacity, battery discharge, waveform aur manufacturer compatibility dekhna zaroori hai. Aam 800–1100 VA inverter par 1800 W induction nahi chalta.

### 7. Kya induction solar se chal sakta hai?
Haan, suitable solar inverter aur kaafi generation ke saath chal sakta hai. Par system capacity aur ghar ke doosre load ka hisaab zaroori hai, aur ye har case me bilkul free nahi hota.

### 8. Kya induction par aluminium ke bartan chalte hain?
Aam aluminium bartan induction par nahi chalte. Magnetic base wale bartan chahiye. Magnet neeche chipke to wo aam taur par chalega.

### 9. Kya induction par roti aur paratha ban sakte hain?
Haan, induction ke liye bane tawa par ban sakte hain. Par seedhi flame par phulka phulane jaisa result nahi milta.

### 10. Kya induction se sanctioned load badhana padega?
Har case me nahi. Par induction aur doosre appliances ka maximum demand sanctioned load se upar jaye to DISCOM ke rules ke hisaab se load enhancement ki zaroorat ho sakti hai.

### 11. Infrared cooktop kitni bijli khata hai?
2000 W infrared lagatar full power par 1 ghanta chale to 2 units. Same cooking heat ke liye infrared ko induction se zyada units lagti hain, isliye infrared ko induction ke barabar units maan kar hisaab na lagayein.

### 12. Induction aur infrared me kaunsa behtar hai?
Induction aam taur par zyada efficient aur tez hota hai, aur kam bijli leta hai. Infrared tab theek hai jab aapko aluminium ya non-magnetic bartan hi use karne ho. Surface garam hone ke karan infrared me jalne ka risk zyada hota hai.

## Final Verdict

**Induction tabhi sasta hai jab aapka marginal bijli rate break-even se neeche ho** (LPG ₹942 par ~₹7.8/unit). Ujjwala wale ghar me aksar LPG hi sasti padti hai, aur infrared induction se zyada bijli khata hai. Faisla karne se pehle apna **bill ka agla slab, apne area ka cylinder rate aur asli cooking khapat** dekhein. Aur kuch bhi lagane se pehle **2.5 mm² line, 16A socket/MCB aur earthing** electrician se check karwayein. Hybrid (induction + chhota LPG backup) zyada tar gharon ke liye practical hai.
