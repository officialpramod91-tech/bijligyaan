## PM Surya Ghar Yojana: Asli Kahaani Kya Hai?

Sarkar ka maqsad hai har ghar tak solar pahunchana. Iske liye scheme mein do badi cheezein hain:
- **Bhari Subsidy:** 3kW tak ₹78,000 tak central subsidy
- **300 Unit Free Claim:** Har mahine 300 unit tak bachat possible (eligible households ke liye)

Lekin "free" ka matlab ye nahi ki meter ruk jayega — iske piche pura calculation hota hai, jo aage detail mein samjhayenge.

### PM Surya Ghar Yojana Mein Kaunsa Solar System Lagta Hai?
Is scheme mein sirf On-Grid Solar System lagta hai.
- Off-grid (battery wala) system allowed nahi hota is subsidy ke andar
- Extra bijli grid mein export hoti hai
- Net metering se bill adjust hota hai

## Kya Sach Mein Bill Zero Ho Jayega? (Reality Check)

Seedha jawab — har kisi ka nahi.

### Case A: Bill Zero Ho Sakta Hai
- Monthly usage: 300 unit tak
- Daytime usage zyada (din mein ghar pe log, din mein AC/appliances chalte hain)
- 👉 Is case mein bill 90–100% tak kam ho sakta hai.

### Case B: Bill Zero Nahi Hoga
- AC raat bhar chalta hai
- Raat ka load zyada hai
- 👉 Is case mein bill 50–60% kam hoga, zero nahi.

## Sabse Bada Darr: Kya Smart Meter Fast Chalta Hai?

**Sach:** Smart Meter fast nahi hota — accurate hota hai.

Solar lagne ke baad aapke yahan **Net Meter** lagta hai jo bidirectional hota hai:
- **Import** = Grid se li gayi bijli
- **Export** = Solar panels se grid ko bheji gayi extra bijli

Mahine ke end me Discom export units ko import units se ghata deta hai aur bachi hui units ka hi bill banta hai. Agar aapne zyada bijli export ki hai, toh wo agle mahine ke bill me carry forward ho jaati hai.

## Solar Lagwane Ke Baad Bhi Bill Zyada Kyun Aata Hai?
- Load mismatch (jaise ghar ka connected load 5kW hai aur solar sirf 3kW lagaya)
- Net meter install hone me delay
- Panels par dhool-mitti ya shadow aana
- Loose connection aur galat DC wiring

## FAQs — 3kW Solar System Price in India With Subsidy

**Q1. 3kW solar system price in India with subsidy kitna hota hai?**  
Subsidy ke baad ₹1.1 lakh se ₹1.45 lakh tak padta hai. UP ya Delhi jaise states mein total subsidy ₹1,08,000 tak ho sakti hai, isliye final price aur kam ho sakta hai.

**Q2. Kya 3kW solar system se poora ghar chal sakta hai?**  
Basic appliances chal sakte hain, lekin heavy load (AC, geyser ek saath) depend karta hai aapke actual consumption pattern par.

**Q3. Kya solar lagne ke baad smart meter fast ho jata hai?**  
Nahi, smart meter sirf accurate reading deta hai. Net metering se import-export dono track hota hai.

**Q4. Kya 3kW solar system mein battery zaroori hai?**  
Nahi, on-grid system mein battery ki zaroorat nahi hoti — ye PM Surya Ghar subsidy ki eligibility ke liye zaroori hai.

**Q5. 3kW solar system se mahine mein kitni bijli banti hai?**  
Lagbhag 360–450 unit/month, location aur dhoop ke hisaab se.

**Q6. Kya UP ke alawa doosre states mein bhi extra subsidy milti hai?**  
Delhi mein milti hai (₹30,000 extra). Haryana mein nahi milti. Baaki states ke liye apni state ki nodal agency se confirm karna chahiye, kyunki ye scheme time-se-time update hoti hai.
