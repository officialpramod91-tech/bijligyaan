## Electrical Load Kya Hota Hai? (Simple Language)

Electrical load ka seedha matlab hai: **Aapke ghar ke saare appliances ek sath kitni bijli (power) khinchte hain.**

Ise **Watt (W)** ya **Kilowatt (kW)** me naapa jata hai:
- **1000 Watt = 1 Kilowatt (1 kW)**

Jitna bada aapka load hoga, aapko utni hi:
1. **Moti Wire (Gauge/sq mm):** Overheating aur aag se bachne ke liye.
2. **Sahi Rating Ki MCB:** Perfect overload protection ke liye.
3. **Sarkari Sanctioned Load (Meter):** Bijli vibhag ki penalty se bachne ke liye.

## Connected Load vs Sanctioned Load: Asli Antar Samajhiye

Zyadatar log yahin sabse badi galti karte hain:

- **Connected Load:** Agar aap ghar ke har pankhe, bulb, plug, AC aur geyser ka wattage jod dein, toh wo aapka *Connected Load* kehlata hai (maan lijiye 6000W ya 6kW).
- **Sanctioned Load:** Ye wo load hai jo bijli board (DISCOM) aapke meter paper par approve karta hai (jaise 2kW ya 3kW).

### Diversity Factor (Kya Sabhi Cheezein Ek Sath Chalti Hain?)
Nahi! Ghar ke saare appliances kabhi bhi ek hi second me ek sath nahi chalte. 
- Aap subah geyser chalate hain, tab AC band rehta hai.
- Jab iron (press) chal rahi hoti hai, zaroori nahi ki washing machine aur induction bhi usi minute chal rahe hon.

> **Electrician Thumb Rule:** Real running load nikalne ke liye apne total connected wattage ka **70% se 75%** hi actual maximum running demand mana jata hai.

## Simple Step-by-Step Load & MCB Calculation Formula

Agar aapko apne ghar ka exact current (Ampere) nikalna hai taaki sahi MCB aur wire chun sakein, toh is basic formula ka use karein:

> **⚡ Simple Electrician Formula:**  
> **Current (Ampere) = Running Wattage ÷ 220 Volts**  
> *(Safety Margin: Calculation me hamesha 20% se 25% extra safety add karein taaki appliance start hote hi MCB trip na kare.)*

### Example (Real Practical Calculation):
Maan lijiye aapke ghar me peak time par ek sath ye chal raha hai:
- 1 AC (1500W) + 1 Fridge (250W) + 4 Fans (300W) + Lights/TV (200W) = **Total 2250 Watts**

1. **Running Current:** `2250 Watts ÷ 220 Volts = 10.22 Amperes`
2. **Safety Margin (+25% Add Karein):** `10.22 × 1.25 = 12.77 Amperes`
3. **Result:** Is load ke liye market me standard **16 Ampere ya 20 Ampere ki C-Type MCB** lagana standard electrician practice hai, aur **4 sq mm copper wire** safe rehti hai.

## Overload Hone Ke 4 Bade Nuksan

1. **Continuous MCB Tripping:** MCB baar-baar garam hokar trip hone lagti hai aur switchboard damage ho sakta hai.
2. **Wire Insulation Breakdown:** Patli wire par zyada load daalne se PVC cover pighal jata hai jo short circuit ka sabse bada kaaran banta hai.
3. **MDI Penalty in Electricity Bill:** Aaj kal ke smart digital meters peak demand record karte hain. Agar 2kW ke meter par 3.5kW load chalayenge, toh agle mahine penalty lagkar aayegi.
4. **Appliance Failure:** Voltage drop hone se refrigerator aur AC ke compressor jaldi kharab ho jaate hain.

## Electrician Ki Golden Advice

- **AC aur Geyser ke liye direct separate line:** Kabhi bhi AC ya geyser ko purane 5A/15A socket ke loop se na jodein. Inke liye distribution board se sidhi separate 4 sq mm wire aur 16A/20A MCB honi chahiye.
- **Neutral Wire ko kabhi halka na samjhein:** Jitni moti phase wire hai, neutral wire bhi barabar motai ki honi chahiye. Loose neutral pure ghar ke electronics ko uda sakta hai.
- **20% Future Scope:** Agar aaj aapka running load 2kW nikal raha hai, toh meter hamesha 3kW ka sanction karwayein taaki future me naya appliance lene par bar-bar bijli daftar na daudna pade.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. 1 Ton AC ke liye kitna meter load hona chahiye?
1 Ton Inverter AC lagbhag 1000W se 1200W khinchta hai. Iske liye ghar me kam se kam 2kW ka sanctioned load connection hona zaroori hai.

### Q2. Kya load badhne se bijli ka bill zyada aata hai?
Haan. Zyadatar states me sanctioned load ke hisaab se fixed charges (monthly capacity charges) per kW badh jate hain, aur overload hone par penalty alag se judti hai.

### Q3. Kaise pata karein ki mera meter overload chal raha hai?
Apne digital electricity meter par push button dabakar "MD" (Maximum Demand) screen check karein. Agar wahan aapke sanction kW se bada number dikh raha hai, toh meter overload ho chuka hai.
