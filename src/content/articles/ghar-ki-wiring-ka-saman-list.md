## Saman Kharidne Se Pehle: 3 Stages Me Material Planning

Concealed wiring ka kaam ek baar me nahi hota. Material hamesha stage-wise khareedein taaki site par cement, ret ya paani se saman kharab na ho:

- **Stage 1 (Slab / Lenter Stage):** Jab chhat ka sariya bandh raha hota hai. Is samay sirf heavy conduit pipes (HMS grade), fan boxes, aur circular junction boxes lagte hain.
- **Stage 2 (Wall Chipping & Plaster Stage):** Deewaron par jhirri katne ke waqt deewar ke conduit pipes, modular metal concealed boxes aur chemical earthing pit ka kaam complete hota hai.
- **Stage 3 (Finishing Stage):** Plaster aur putty/paint sukhne ke baad wire pulling, distribution board (DB) dressing, MCBs, RCCB, modular switches aur sockets lagte hain.

## 1. Wires Checklist (IS 694 & IS 8130 Standards)

Domestic house wiring me hamesha **IS 694 approved, Class 5 Multi-strand Annealed Pure Copper Conductor** wali wire hi leni chahiye. Conductor ki quality ka standard IS 8130 hota hai.

### FRLSH vs LSZH Wire Me Fark Samjhein
Market me aam taur par bikne wali **FRLSH** (Fire Retardant Low Smoke Halogen) wire PVC insulation wali hoti hai. Aag lagne par ye flame propagation ko rokti hai aur normal PVC ke mukable kam dhuan deti hai, lekin isme halogen mojud hota hai. **LSZH** (Low Smoke Zero Halogen) wire fully halogen-free hoti hai jo commercial projects aur public areas me compulsory hoti hai. Domestic house wiring ke liye ISI marked reputed brand ki FRLSH pure copper wire safe aur standard hai.

### Circuit-Wise Wire Size Selection Chart

| Wire Size (sq mm) | Sahi Use (Kahan Lagayein) | Protection Breaker | Technical Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1.0 mm²** | Inverter Control Line / Decorative Profile LED | 6A / 10A MCB | Chhote control circuits aur pure low-current load ke liye. |
| **1.5 mm²** | Room Lighting, Ceiling Fans, TV Socket, Exhaust Fans | 10A MCB | General illumination circuits ke continuous load ke liye. |
| **2.5 mm²** | Power Sockets (Fridge, Microwave, Mixer, Iron), 1.0–1.5 Ton AC (short run), 2 kW Geyser | 16A C-Curve MCB | Inductive spikes aur medium thermal load ko bina drop ke carry karne ke liye. |
| **4.0 mm²** | 1.5–2.0 Ton AC (long run), 3 kW Storage Geyser, Kitchen Heavy Line | 20A / 25A C-Curve MCB | Continuous high-current draw par voltage drop minimize rakhne ke liye. |
| **6.0 mm²** | Sub-DB Feeder Cable ya Multi-point Induction Hob | 32A MCB / Isolator | Ek sath chalne wale multiple room sub-circuits ke total load ke liye. |
| **10.0 / 16.0 mm²** | **Energy Meter Se Main DB Board Tak Ki Cable** | 40A / 63A DP Isolator | Poore ghar ka total running load safe handle karne ke liye (Service pole se meter tak DISCOM ka hissa hota hai). |

> **Inverter Wire Ka Rule:** Inverter load points ko switchboard me feed dene ke liye hamesha alag identification color (field practice me White ya Grey) wire shuru se pull karwayein. Grid line aur inverter line ko aapas me messy na hone dein.

![House wiring wire size selection chart and appliance load capacity cheat sheet infographic by Bijli Gyaan](/images/house-wiring-wire-size-and-appliance-selection-chart.webp)
*Cheat Sheet Guide: Har appliance ke according safe wire sizes (sq mm) aur unki internal diameter specifications.*

### Earth Wire Ka Size (IS 3043 Rule)
Aksar thekedaar har board par patla 0.75 ya 1.0 mm² ka green wire loop kar dete hain, jo safety ke hisaab se galat hai. IS 3043 ke anusaar:
* Lighting sub-circuits ke sath **1.5 mm²** copper earth wire lagayein.
* 2.5 mm² aur 4.0 mm² power circuits ke sath **same cross-section ya minimum 2.5 mm²** green copper earth wire daalein. Agar phase line par short-circuit hota hai, to earth loop ka impedance kam hona chahiye taaki breaker turnt trip ho.

Sahi wire specifications aur brands samajhne ke liye padhein: [House Wiring Wire Size Chart](https://bijligyaan.in/articles/ghar-ki-wiring-wire-size-guide/).

## 2. Concealed Piping Aur Metal Boxes (Stage 1 & 2)

Conduit pipes plaster ke andar hamesha ke liye pack ho jate hain. Yahan sasta local pipe lena sabse badi galti hoti hai.

- **Rigid PVC Conduits (IS 9537 Part 3):** Slab (lenter) ke liye **Heavy Mechanical Stress (HMS)** grade pipe hi lein. Lenter daalte samay jab mazdoor aur concrete trolleys chalti hain, to normal pipe pichak kar band ho jata hai. Deewaron ke liye Medium (MMS) grade pipe chal sakta hai.
- **Pipe Size:** Main corridors, slab drops aur DB feed ke liye **25 mm**, normal room lighting branches ke liye **20 mm**.
- **Long Radius Bends & Couplers:** Pipe modne ke liye long radius inspection bends lagayein taaki wire kheenchte waqt steel wire na atke. Joints par PVC solvent cement lagana zaroori hai taaki plaster ki nami andar na jaye.
- **GI Fan Boxes (Heavy Hook):** Chhat me fan latkane ke liye 16-gauge GI fan box jisme solid steel rod hook welded ho. Normal plastic box fan ka vibration nahi jhel pate.
- **Modular Concealed Metal Boxes (GI 18-Gauge Zinc Coated):** Deewar me modular plates ke liye 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, aur 18 module sizes me heavy rust-proof boxes.

### 40% Conduit Space Rule (IS 732)
IS 732 ke rule ke mutabik conduit pipe ke andar total wires ka area conduit ke internal space ke **40% se zyada nahi hona chahiye** (yani conduit ke andar 60% khali jagah rehni chahiye). Agar pipe ko taaron se poora thons denge, to wire kheenchne me insulation chhil jayega aur load padne par garmi bahar nahi nikal payegi.

## 3. Distribution Board (DB), MCBs Aur Safety Devices

Yeh aapke ghar ka main defence system hai:

- **Double Door Distribution Board (IS 13032):** Metal body, powder-coated, IP42/IP43 rating ke sath. Double door box board ko dhool aur ungliyon ke direct touch se bachata hai. Selection guide: [MCB Box (DB Box) Kaise Chune](https://bijligyaan.in/articles/mcb-box-db-box-kaise-chune/).
- **Double Pole (DP) Isolator:** 40A ya 63A main isolator jo ek click me phase aur neutral dono ko cut kare.
- **RCCB / RCBO (IS 12640 Standard):**
  - **30 mA Sensitivity:** Domestic life safety aur electric shock se bachav ke liye strictly 30 mA tripping current sensitivity wali unit hi lagayein.
  - **Tripping Time:** 30 mA current limit hai; standard nominal RCCB ka maximum trip time **~300 ms (0.3 second)** hota hai.
  - **Split Protection:** Poore ghar ko ek single RCCB par na daalein. Agar kisi ek geyser me mamooli leakage aayega to poora ghar andhere me doob jayega. Lighting aur power lines ke liye separate sub-RCCBs ya RCBOs lagana sabse safe practice hai. Padhein: [Ghar Mein RCCB Lagana Kyun Zaruri Hai](https://bijligyaan.in/articles/ghar-mein-rccb-lagana-kyun-zaruri-hai/).
- **Miniature Circuit Breakers (MCB - IS/IEC 60898-1):**
  - **C-Curve MCB:** AC, fridge, geyser aur water motor jaise inductive load ke liye 16A, 20A ya 25A.
  - **B-Curve MCB:** Sensitive electronics aur pure lighting circuits ke liye 6A ya 10A. Detail breaker chart: [MCB Selection Guide](https://bijligyaan.in/articles/mcb-types-selection-guide/).
- **Surge Protection Device (Type 2 SPD):** Grid voltage spikes aur aasmani bijli ke surges se smart inverter boards, TV aur LED lights ko safe rakhne ke liye.

## 4. Modular Switches, Sockets Aur Modern Smart Accessories

- **6A Modular Switches (IS 3854):** Light, fan aur standard circuits ke liye. Silver-alloy contact terminals chunein jo sparking nahi hone dete.
- **Safety Shutter Sockets (IS 1293):** 6A multi-pin aur 16A/25A heavy power sockets. Bachchon ki safety ke liye strictly shuttered sockets hi lagayein.
- **Step-Type Fan Regulators:** Capacitor based 4-5 step regulators jo fan me humming noise nahi karte aur speed smoothly control karte hain.
- **Future-Ready Smart Switch Provision (Dedicated Neutral Wire):** Aaj kal Wi-Fi aur smart touch switches ka zamana hai. Har ek switchboard metal box ke andar phase ke sath **ek dedicated neutral wire** zaroor drop karwayein, bhale hi board par socket na lagna ho. Smart switches ko operate hone ke liye constant neutral supply chahiye hoti hai.
- **Modular Blank Plates (Dummy covers):** Switchboard me khali bache hue extra slots ko pack karne ke liye.
- **Coaxial TV & RJ-45 LAN Ports:** Wi-Fi router aur dish cable ki deewar ke bahar latakti wiring se chhutkara pane ke liye.

## 5. Earthing Aur Chemical Grounding Material (IS 3043)

Bina ground earthing ke metallic body wale appliances (fridge, washing machine, geyser) par current aane par safety breaker sahi response nahi de pate. Padhein: [Neutral Aur Earth Wire Mein Kya Antar Hai](https://bijligyaan.in/articles/neutral-aur-earth-wire-mein-kya-antar-hai/).

### Zaroori Saman Ki List:
- **Chemical Earth Electrode:** 2.5 se 3.0 meter lambi copper-bonded steel rod (minimum 250 micron copper coating).
- **Backfill Earth Compound (BFC/Bentonite):** Rod ke chaaro taraf soil moisture banaye rakhne aur dry weather me bhi ground resistance ko 2 Ohm se kam maintain rakhne ke liye. Purana namak-koyla system waqt ke sath baith jata hai aur corrosive hota hai.
- **Earth Conductor (Pit Se DB Tak):** Pure copper bare wire (minimum 8 SWG) ya 25x3 mm GI/Copper earth strip.
- **Inspection Pit Chamber:** Concrete pit cover testing aur future maintenance ke liye. Full process: [Earthing Lagwane Ka Kharcha (2026 Estimate)](https://bijligyaan.in/articles/earthing-kya-hai/).

## Real Field Case Studies: Galat Saman Ka Anjaam

### 🛠️ Field Case 1: 16A Geyser Socket Par 1.0 mm² Wire Aur Protection Mismatch
* **Kahan Ka Mamla:** Noida Sector 62 (Naya Makaan Wiring).
* **Asli Problem:** Bathroom me 25L (2000W) storage geyser chalte hi lagbhag 40–45 minute me switchboard ke peeche se plastic jalne ki badboo aayi aur junction box ke paas plaster garam hone laga.
* **Technical Reason:** 230V par 2000W geyser lagbhag **8.7A** continuous current leta hai. Conduit ke andar doosri lighting wires ke sath 1.0 mm² wire dali thi jisse heat dissipation kam ho raha tha. Sabse bada blunder ye tha ki DB me **16A MCB** lagi thi. 8.7A continuous load par 16A MCB kabhi trip nahi ho sakti thi, aur wire lagatar overheat hokar insulation crack hone laga.
* **Humne Kaise Fix Kiya:** Pehle conduit pipe ki thermal condition inspect ki (pipe safe thi). Uske baad purani wire nikaal kar dedicated **2.5 mm² copper wire** daali aur board par heavy brass terminal socket lagaya.
* **Seekh:** Sirf modular socket 16A ka laga lene se line safe nahi hoti. Wire gauge aur uske peeche lagi MCB rating ka coordination hona life safety ke liye zaroori hai. Geyser install karne se pehle dekhein: [Geyser Lagane Se Pehle Kya Dhyan Rakhe](https://bijligyaan.in/articles/geyser-lagane-se-pehle-kya-dhyan-rakhe/).

### 🛠️ Field Case 2: Inverter Wiring Me Common Neutral Ka Ghotala
* **Kahan Ka Mamla:** Lucknow (Indira Nagar - Kothi Renovation Project).
* **Asli Problem:** Ghar me naya 1100VA pure sine wave inverter lagane ke baad, jaise hi main grid light cut hokar load inverter par shift hota, main DB me lagi 30 mA RCCB turant trip ho jati thi aur pura ghar andhere me band ho jata tha.
* **Technical Reason:** Thekedaar ne phase wire toh inverter se switchboard tak alag daal di thi, lekin switchboard ke andar inverter supply ke neutral ko grid ke general neutral loop ke sath aapas me joda chhod diya tha (Common Neutral System). Jab inverter operate karta tha, toh return current grid-side RCCB ke through unbalance leak path dhundhta tha, jisse RCCB fault sense karke trip kar deti thi.
* **Humne Kaise Fix Kiya:** Inverter se chalne wale lighting/fan points ke neutral return path ko grid neutral se alag identify kiya aur distribution board me neutral sub-circuits ki proper segregation ki.
* **Seekh:** Wiring ke saman me inverter circuits ke liye alag color (aam field practice me White ya Grey) wire rakhein. Inverter load ka neutral kabhi bhi grid RCCB ke input neutral se mess nahi hona chahiye. Inverter vs solar inverter samajhne ke liye padhein: [Solar Inverter vs Normal Inverter](https://bijligyaan.in/articles/solar-inverter-vs-normal-inverter/).

### 🛠️ Field Case 3: Washing Machine Ki Body Par Shock (Open Earth Conductor)
* **Kahan Ka Mamla:** Kanpur (Utility Area Balcony).
* **Asli Problem:** Balcony me rakhi washing machine ke metal panel aur paani ke nal ko ek sath chhoone par halka electric shock (tingle) lag raha tha. Customer ko laga machine ke andar motor short ho gayi hai.
* **Technical Reason:** Multimeter se socket test karne par Phase–Neutral 228V tha, lekin Phase–Earth terminal ke beech healthy voltage nahi mil rahi thi (meter par reading float/unstable thi). Switchboard kholne par pata chala ki thekedaar ne wire bachaane ke liye power socket ke top pin par green earth wire daali hi nahi thi—wo pin khali chhooti hui thi. Machine ke internal filter aur motor static ka induced current ground na hone ke karan metal body par jama ho raha tha.
* **Humne Kaise Fix Kiya:** Kaam theek hone tak customer ko machine unplug rakhne ki safety instruction di. DB se utility board tak dedicated 2.5 mm² green copper earth wire daali aur chemical pit se connect kiya. Re-test karne par Phase–Earth 227V aur Neutral–Earth <2V mila, aur body par induced voltage zero ho gayi.
* **Seekh:** Har 3-pin socket ka top pin koi decorative design nahi hai; metallic appliances ke liye ye life safety line hai.

## Saman Kharidte Samay 4 Zaroori Quality Checks

1. **ISI Mark Aur BIS CML Number:** Sirf "ISI" likha hona kafi nahi hai; wire coil, switch aur MCB par BIS ka 7-digit CML license number check karein. Aap ise government ke **BIS Care App** par scan karke authorized manufacturer verify kar sakte hain.
2. **Wire Coil Weight & Batch Label:** Reputed brands (Havells, Polycab, Finolex, KEI, RR Kabel) ki 90 meter coil par gross weight, manufacturing date aur IS 694 marking saaf hoti hai. Local duplicate coils me 90 meter ki jagah 65–70 meter hi wire nikalta hai.
3. **Switch Terminals (Magnet Fallacy):** Log terminal par magnet chipka kar check karte hain, par ye galat tarika hai kyunki genuine branded switches me bhi terminals ko tight karne wale screws plated steel ke hote hain. Hamesha genuine authorized dealer se khareedein aur terminal base ka heavy contact material dekhein.
4. **GST Bill With Brand & Specifications:** Dukandar se kachhi pachi lene ke bajaye pukka GST bill lein jisme wire ka sq mm size aur MCB model clearly mentioned ho taaki manufacturer warranty claim ki ja sake.

## Commissioning Se Pehle 3 Zaroori Tests (IS 732 & CEA 2023)

Central Electricity Authority (Measures relating to Safety and Electric Supply) Regulations 2023 ke mutabik ghar me supply chalu karne se pehle ye 3 tests hone zaroori hain:

1. **Insulation Resistance Test (Megger Test):** 500V DC megger tester se phase, neutral aur earth conductors ke beech insulation resistance test karein. Yeh minimum **1 Mega-Ohm** se zyada hona chahiye taaki deewar ke andar koi current leakage na ho.
2. **Continuity & Polarity Test:** Multimeter se ensure karein ki switchboard me switch hamesha Phase line ko cut kar raha ho, Neutral ko nahi.
3. **Earth Resistance Test:** Earth tester se inspection pit ka resistance check karein. IS 3043 ke guidelines ke anusar residential installation me earth resistance 2–3 Ohm se kam hona ideal maana jata hai. Agar ghar me low voltage issue rehta hai to padhein: [Ghar Me Low Voltage Kyun Aati Hai](https://bijligyaan.in/articles/ghar-me-low-voltage-kyun-aati-hai/).

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Ghar ki wiring ke liye kaunsi wire sabse achhi aur safe hoti hai?
Ghar ki concealed wiring ke liye ISI marked (IS 694) Class 5 multi-strand pure electrolytic copper conductor wali FRLSH (Fire Retardant Low Smoke Halogen) PVC insulated wire standard aur safe mani jaati hai. Commercial ya high-density apartments ke liye fully halogen-free LSZH wire aur behtar hoti hai.

### 2. 1.5 Ton AC chalane ke liye kitne sq mm ki wire aur MCB lagani chahiye?
1.5 Ton Inverter AC ke liye standard short run par 2.5 mm² copper wire aur 16A C-Curve MCB kaafi hoti hai. Agar DB board se AC point ki running length 15–20 meter se lambi hai, to voltage drop rokne ke liye 4.0 mm² copper wire aur 20A C-Curve MCB lagana behtar rehta hai.

### 3. Kya bina earthing ke RCCB lagane se electric shock se suraksha milegi?
Bina earthing ke RCCB metal appliance par halka leakage aane par pehle se detect karke trip nahi ho pati. Aise me jab koi vyakti us appliance ko chhoota hai aur current uske shareer se leak hota hai, tabhi RCCB trip hoti hai. Fault pehle hi isolate karne aur complete shock protection ke liye solid ground earthing mandatory hai.

### 4. Slab (lenter) ke conduit pipe me kitni taarein daal sakte hain?
IS 732 standard ke mutabik conduit pipe ke internal cross-sectional area ka maximum 40% hissa hi wires se bhara hona chahiye. Bacha hua 60% space khali rehna chahiye taaki wire kheenchte waqt chhile nahi aur load par heat generation na ho.

### 5. 2BHK ghar ki wiring me lagbhag kitne bundle wire lagte hain?
Ek standard 1000 sq ft 2BHK ghar me lagbhag 2 coil (1.0 mm²), 2–3 coil (1.5 mm²), 2–3 coil (2.5 mm²), 1–2 coil (4.0 mm²) aur 2–3 coil earth wire (1.5/2.5 mm² green) lagti hain. Har coil 90 meter length ki hoti hai.

### 6. Wiring ke baad supply on karne se pehle kaun se test karwane chahiye?
Supply chalu karne se pehle teen test aniwarya hain: 500V Megger se Insulation Resistance test (minimum 1 MΩ), Phase-Neutral polarity test, aur Earth pit resistance test (target 2–3 Ohm se kam).
