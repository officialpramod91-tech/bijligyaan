## 1. Room Size Ke Hisaab Se Kaun Sa Cooler Best Hai?

Sabse pehla aur sabse important factor hai — **aapke kamre ka square feet area**. Galat size ka cooler lene ka matlab hai zero cooling aur paise ki barbadi.

| Room Size (Sq Ft) | Recommended Cooler Type | Ideal Water Tank Capacity | Best Air Throw Setup |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Up to 100 sq ft** | **Personal Cooler** | 20L – 35L | Bed ke paas direct hawa ke liye |
| **100 – 200 sq ft** | **Tower / Window Cooler** | 40L – 55L | Medium bedroom aur silent airflow |
| **200+ sq ft (Bada Hall)** | **Desert Cooler** | **65L – 90L** | Heavy fan, long throw aur wide oscillation |

> **Thumb Rule:** Agar aapka room 200 sq ft se bada hai, toh kabhi bhi table ya personal cooler na lein. Bade kamre ke liye hamesha **Desert Cooler** hi sahi thandak de paata hai.

## 2. Honeycomb Pads vs Wood Wool (Grass) Pads

Cooler me lagne wale cooling pads hi tay karte hain ki hawa kitni thandi aayegi:

### ✔ Honeycomb Pads (Sabse Zyada Recommended)
- **Cell Structure:** Yeh paper aur cellulose material se bane madhumakkhi ke chhatte jaise design me aate hain.
- **Faayde:** Paani ko lambe samay tak hold karte hain, kam maintenance lagti hai, aur 3 se 4 season aasaani se chal jaate hain.
- **No Bad Odour:** Isme se ghaas jaisi ajeeb badbu ya fungal smell nahi aati.

### ❌ Wood Wool (Ghaas / Grass Pads)
- **Saste Hote Hain:** Inki initial cost bohot kam hoti hai.
- **Nuksan:** Ek season me hi gal jaate hain, paani ke kharepan se holes block ho jaate hain, aur har saal badalna padta hai.

> **Final Electrician Verdict:** Hamesha **Honeycomb Pad** wala cooler hi chunein. Yeh ₹500–₹800 mehnga padta hai lekin cooling double deta hai.

## 3. Air Cooler Kitne Watt Ka Hota Hai? (Power Consumption Guide)

Bijli ki khapat ke maamle me cooler AC ke mukable bohot sasta padta hai:

- **Personal / Tower Cooler:** 100 Watt – 165 Watt
- **Desert Cooler (Heavy Fan):** 180 Watt – 250 Watt

### Real Unit Calculation:
- Ek 200 Watt ka desert cooler **1 unit bijli me lagbhag 5 ghante** chalta hai.
- Agar aap roz 10 ghante cooler chalate hain, toh roz lagbhag **2 Units** kharch honge (mahine ka lagbhag ₹450 se ₹500 ka bijli bill).
- Iske muqable ek 1.5 Ton AC utne hi ghante me roz 8 se 10 units bijli khata hai.

## 4. Body & Motor Quality (Long Term Investment)

Cooler khareedte waqt in 2 internal cheezon ko zaroor dekhein:

1. **100% Pure Copper Winding Motor:** Saste cooler me aluminium wire ki motor lagi hoti hai jo lagatar 8 ghante chalne par overheat hokar jal jaati hai. Hamesha **Copper Motor** hi lein.
2. **Thermoplastic / Engineered ABS Body:** Lohe ke cooler me current leakage aur zang (rust) ka khatra rehta hai. Heavy-duty ABS plastic body shock-proof aur rust-free hoti hai.

## 5. Humidity Aur Cross-Ventilation (AC Jaisi Cooling Ka Secret)

Bahut se logon ki complaint hoti hai ki: *"Cooler chalane se kamre me chip-chip hone lagti hai aur garmi badh jaati hai."*

### Iska Asli Solution:
- **Cooler ko band kamre me kabhi na chalayein:** Cooler tabhi best cooling deta hai jab taazi hawa bahar se kheench kar kamre ke andar phenke.
- **Opposite Window Thodi Khuli Rakhein:** Kamre ki doosri taraf ek khidki ya darwaza 2 se 3 inch khula rakhein taaki purani nam (moist) hawa bahar nikal sake. Is cross-ventilation se humidity control rehti hai aur bilkul AC jaisi thandak mehsoos hoti hai.

## Cooler Khareedte Waqt In 4 Galtiyon Se Bachein

- ❌ **Inverter Compatibility Check Na Karna:** Hamesha aisa cooler lein jo inverter ke modified sine-wave par bina buzzing sound ke smoothly chal sake.
- ❌ **Aluminium Motor Lena:** ₹500 bachane ke chakkar me aluminium winding motor lena sabse bada nuksan hota hai.
- ❌ **Bina Ice Chamber Wala Model Lena:** Peak 45°C ki garmi me ice chamber me barf ke cubes daalne se temperature turant 3–4 degree down ho jata hai.
- ❌ **Earthing Wire Ignore Karna:** Cooler ke 3-pin plug me third earthing pin zaroor honi chahiye taaki paani me current utarne ka zero risk rahe.

## Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawal (FAQs)

### Q1. Ghar ke liye kaun sa cooler sabse best hota hai?
Medium se bade kamre ke liye **Honeycomb pad wala Desert Cooler (60L–75L capacity)** sabse best hota hai. Chhote kamre ke liye Tower ya Personal cooler sahi rehta hai.

### Q2. Air cooler kitne watt bijli khata hai?
Normal cooler 150 Watt se 250 Watt bijli consume karta hai, jo 1.5 Ton AC ke muqable lagbhag 80% kam hota hai.

### Q3. Honeycomb aur Ghaas pad me kya farq hai?
Honeycomb pad 3-4 saal tak bina badbu ke chalte hain aur paani zyada hold karke behtar cooling dete hain, jabki grass pad har saal kharab ho jaate hain.

### Q4. Kya normal home inverter par desert cooler chal sakta hai?
Haan! Ek 150W–200W ka cooler normal 850VA ya 1100VA inverter par 3 se 5 ghante aasaani se chal sakta hai.
