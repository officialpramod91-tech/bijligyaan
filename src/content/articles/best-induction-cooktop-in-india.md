## <a id="checklist"></a>Kharidne Se Pehle 8 Checks
 
1. **Plug rating:** box ya manual me dekhein ki kaunsa plug aaya hai. 1600 W (~6.96 A) aur 1800 W (~7.83 A) bhi 6A ki limit se upar hain.
2. **BIS mark aur licence:** rating plate par ISI/BIS mark aur licence number dekhein, aur **BIS Care app** se licence verify karein.
3. **Warranty:** product aur coil warranty alag dekhein, aur warranty card padhein. Glass tootna, liquid andar jana aur insect damage aam taur par cover nahi hote.
4. **Bartan ka base:** manual me minimum aur maximum base diameter dekhein.
5. **Safety features:** child lock, residual heat warning, auto-off.
6. **Cooling aur jagah:** neeche/peeche ke vents khule rakhein, aur induction ke neeche akhbar, kapda ya plastic na rakhein.
7. **Authorised seller:** refurbished ya open-box unit se bachne ke liye.
8. **Voltage ki sthiti:** agar aapke ilake me voltage bahut upar-neeche hota hai, to voltage protector ya electrician se jaanch pehle karwayein. Surge rating iska replacement nahi hai.
> **Pacemaker ya medical implant wale log** induction ke paas khade hone se pehle doctor aur manufacturer ki salah lein.

## <a id="safety"></a>Induction Ke Liye Wiring Aur Safety (IS 732 Aur CEA)
 
### Kitna current leta hai? (Current = Watt ÷ 230 V)
- 1600 W ≈ **6.96 A** · 1800 W ≈ **7.83 A** · 2000 W ≈ **8.70 A** · 2100 W ≈ **9.13 A**
Isliye **ordinary 6A socket par chalane ki salah nahi di jati.** Induction electronic load hai, asli input current power factor par bhi depend karta hai, isliye rating plate dekhein.
 
### Aam taur par kya lagwayein (final confirm electrician se)
- **Socket:** appliance ke plug ke hisaab se heavy-duty socket (aam taur par 16A, IS 1293 ke anuroop). Adapter aur saste extension board par na chalayein.
- **Wire:** aam taur par **2.5 mm² copper.** Par asli size wire ki lambai, conduit ya concealed wiring, temperature, grouping aur voltage drop par depend karta hai. Detail: [kitchen line ke liye wire ka size](/articles/ghar-ki-wiring-wire-size-guide/).
- **MCB:** rating wire ki capacity aur circuit design se tay hoti hai, har jagah “16A” rule nahi. Selection: [dedicated line ke liye MCB ki rating](/articles/mcb-types-selection-guide/).
- **RCCB 30 mA:** shock ke risk ko kam karti hai, aur rated leakage par aam taur par max ~300 ms me trip hoti hai. Ye overload ya short circuit ka bachav **nahi** hai: [RCCB ka kaam aur trip hone ki wajah](/articles/ghar-mein-rccb-lagana-kyun-zaruri-hai/).
- **Earthing:** socket ka earth theek ho: [socket ki earthing kaise check hoti hai](/articles/earthing-kya-hai/).
- **Load:** induction aur geyser jaise heavy load ek saath chalte hon to demand dekhein: [sanctioned load aur connected load ka fark](/articles/home-electrical-load-calculation/).
### IS Standards Aur CEA Regulations
 
| Reference | Is topic me matlab |
|---|---|
| **IS 302 (Part 1):2024** | Household electrical appliances ki general safety (IEC 60335-1 ke anuroop) |
| **IS 302 (Part 2/Sec 6):2009** | Induction stoves, cooking ranges, hobs aur ovens ki particular safety. BIS ke anusaar ye stationary appliances par lagu hai, portable cooktop kis part me aata hai wo rating plate/BIS licence se confirm karein |
| **Safety of Household, Commercial and Similar Electrical Appliances (QCO), 2026** | Kai electrical appliance categories ke liye BIS mark zaroori, general implementation 1 Oct 2026 bataya gaya. **Induction cooktop is list me hai ya nahi, BIS/brand se confirm karein** |
| **IS 732:2019** | Wiring ka code of practice (conductor sizing, installation, testing) |
| **IS 1293 / IS 3043** | Plug-socket aur earthing |
| **IS 12640 / IEC 61008** | RCCB. 30 mA RCCB rated leakage par aam taur par max ~300 ms me trip hoti hai |
| **CEA (Measures relating to Safety and Electric Supply) Regulations, 2023** | Installation ka test aur verify, leakage se bachav, RCD ka provision, qualified person |
 
> Standards ke **edition aur clause** badalte hain. Latest ke liye BIS aur CEA/Gazette ke official text dekhein. Mai yahan CEA ke clause number nahi de raha. Ye legal advice nahi hai.

## <a id="field-cases"></a>Typical Field Scenarios (Aam Taur Par Aise Cases Milte Hain)
 
> Ye **typical scenarios** hain jo kitchen wiring check me aksar milte hain, kisi khaas ghar ka dava nahi.
 
### Scenario 1: Naya induction aur 6A socket/adapter
**Shikayat:** 7–9 A ka induction 6A socket ya ₹40 ke adapter par chalaya, aur kuch din me socket garam hota hai, plug ke pin par kale nishan aate hain.
**Kya milta hai:** Loose terminal aur chhota socket, isliye contact resistance badhta hai aur garmi banti hai (P = I²R). Aksar peeche ki wire bhi patli hoti hai.
**Fix:** DB se alag line, sahi size ki wire, achha heavy-duty socket aur sahi MCB. Sirf socket badalna kaafi nahi.
 
### Scenario 2: Loose neutral aur bahut zyada voltage
**Shikayat:** Raat me induction achanak kharab, aur ghar ke kuch aur appliance bhi jal gaye.
**Kya milta hai:** Supply ka neutral kamzor hone par voltage bahut upar chala gaya. Induction par likhi **3–4 kV surge rating sirf millisecond ke spike** ke liye hoti hai, **lagatar zyada voltage** ke liye nahi.
**Fix:** Turant electrician aur DISCOM se supply jaanch, aur zarurat par voltage protector. Loose joint aur voltage ki pehchaan: [loose joint aur voltage drop ki pehchaan](/articles/ghar-me-low-voltage-kyun-aati-hai/).
 
### Scenario 3: Induction aur geyser saath chalate hi main MCB trip
**Shikayat:** Subah induction aur geyser ek saath chalate hi main MCB trip.
**Kya milta hai:** Total load ghar ke MCB ya sanctioned load se upar jata hai. MCB trip ke peeche overload, short circuit ya leakage koi bhi ho sakta hai.
**Fix:** Load ka hisaab, heavy load ka alag samay ya alag line.
 
### Scenario 4: Induction baar-baar band hota hai
**Shikayat:** Kuch minute baad induction apne aap band.
**Kya milta hai:** Neeche akhbar ya kapda rakhne se vents band, ya vents me dhool aur tel jam gaya.
**Fix:** Vents saaf karein, cooktop ko hard flat surface par rakhein.

## <a id="cost"></a>Bartan Aur Bijli Kharch Ka Hisaab
 
### Kaunse bartan chalenge? (Magnet test)
Magnet bartan ke neeche chipke to wo aam taur par induction par chalega.
- **Chalte hain:** magnetic stainless steel, cast iron, enameled iron.
- **Nahi chalte:** aam aluminium, copper aur peetal (bina magnetic base), kaanch.
### Monthly bijli (illustrative)
**Units = average kW × ghante × 30**
 
| Average power | Roz ka samay | Mahine ke units |
|---|---|---|
| 1.2 kW | 1 ghanta | ~36 |
| 1.5 kW | 1 ghanta | ~45 |
| 1.8 kW | 1 ghanta | ~54 |
 
Ek 14.2 kg LPG cylinder ke barabar cooking heat ke liye induction ko lagbhag **120 units** lag sakti hain (illustrative efficiency par). Isse LPG ₹942 par break-even rate lagbhag **₹7.8/unit** banta hai. Slab ka asar samajhne ke liye: [slab jump se bill kyun badhta hai](/articles/bijli-ka-bill-zyada-kyun-aata-hai/). Asli khapat plug-in energy meter (16A rating) se napein.

## Aam Galtiyan Jo Log Induction Kharidate Aur Chalate Waqt Karte Hain

### 1. Plug Rating Dekhe Bina Induction Ghar Le Aana
Zyadatar log online sirf wattage aur discount dekhkar induction order kar dete hain, aur jab box khulta hai to usme mota 16A plug nikalta hai. Phir kitchen me 16A socket na hone par jugaad shuru hota hai. Kharidne se pehle box ya user manual par plug ki rating zaroor check karein taaki baad me pareshani na ho.

### 2. 6A Socket Ya Saste Conversion Adapter Par Induction Chalana
Ye kitchen me aag lagne ka sabse bada kaaran banta hai. 1600W se 2100W ka induction lagbhag 7A se 9A current khinchta hai, jabki normal 6A socket ya ₹40–₹50 ka plastic adapter itna load lagatar nahi jhel pata. Kuch hi dinon me pin garam hokar pighalne lagti hai—isliye hamesha model ke manual ke hisaab se heavy-duty power socket aur sahi wiring hi use karein.

### 3. Surge Protection (3kV–4kV) Ko High Voltage Ka Bachav Samajhna
Kai logon ko lagta hai ki box par "4kV Surge Protection" likha hai to gaon ya colony me 270V–280V aane par bhi induction nahi jalega. Asal me surge protection sirf pal bhar (millisecond) ke achanak spike se bachata hai, lagatar high voltage se nahi. Agar aapke area me neutral loose hone ya voltage badhne ki dikkat hai, to pehle electrician se supply check karwayein.

### 4. Bartan Ka Magnet Test Kiye Bina Cooking Shuru Karna
Induction lene ke baad kai log purane aluminium ke bhagone ya peetal ke bartan rakh dete hain, aur screen par E0 error aate hi samajhte hain ki naya chulha kharab nikal gaya. Induction sirf magnetic base wale steel ya lohe ke bartan ko hi garam karta hai—ek chhota sa magnet neeche lagakar pehle hi check kar lein.

### 5. Induction Ke Neeche Akhbar, Kapda Ya Plastic Sheet Bichhana
Kitchen slab ganda na ho, is chakkar me kai log induction ke neeche akhbar ya kapda rakh dete hain. Aisa karne se neeche lage cooling fan ki hawa ruk jati hai aur andar ka IGBT transistor overheat hokar cooktop ko baar-baar band karne lagta hai—hamesha induction ko saaf, hard aur flat surface par hi rakhein.

## <a id="faqs"></a>Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawal (FAQs)
 
### 1. 4 member family ke liye kaunsa induction best hai?
4–5 log ke liye 2000–2100 W ka induction practical hai, jaise Philips Viva HD4938/01 (2100 W) ya Prestige PIC 16.0 Plus (2000 W), kyunki isme boiling aur cooking jaldi hoti hai.
 
### 2. Small family (2–3 log) ke liye kaunsa induction lena chahiye?
Choti family ke liye 1600 W ka induction practical hai, jaise Prestige PIC 20 Neo (1600 W). Plug ki rating box ya manual me zaroor dekhein.
 
### 3. ₹2,000 se kam me kaunsa induction theek hai?
₹2,000 ke neeche Pigeon Cruise (1800 W) ek budget option hai (lagbhag ₹1,500–₹1,700). Light cooking aur backup ke liye theek hai, par heavy daily use ke liye Philips, Prestige ya V-Guard behtar rehte hain.
 
### 4. Kya 1600 W ya 2000 W induction ko normal 6A socket par chala sakte hain?
Nahi. 230 V par 1600 W lagbhag 6.96 A aur 2000 W lagbhag 8.70 A current leta hai, jo 6A socket ki limit se zyada hai. Apne model ke plug aur manual ke hisaab se heavy-duty socket lagwayein.
 
### 5. Induction me surge protection (3–4 kV) ka kya kaam hai?
Ye achanak aane wale voltage spike se circuit ko bachane ka feature hai. Lagatar zyada voltage (jaise 270 V ke upar) ke liye ye nahi hai, aise me electrician se supply aur neutral ki jaanch karwayein.
 
### 6. Induction par E0 ya E1 error kyun aata hai?
Error codes brand ke hisaab se alag hote hain. Aksar E0 ka matlab bartan detect na hona (non-magnetic ya bahut chhota base) hota hai. Exact code ke liye apne model ka manual dekhein.
 
### 7. Kya induction normal home inverter par chal sakta hai?
Aam 800–1100 VA inverter par 1600–2100 W induction nahi chalega aur inverter overload hokar trip ho jayega. Pehle inverter ki continuous watt capacity aur manufacturer compatibility check karein. Inverter chunne ka tareeka: [heavy load ke liye inverter ka chunav](/articles/solar-inverter-vs-normal-inverter/).
 
### 8. Induction mahine me kitni bijli khata hai?
Roz 1 ghanta average 1.2 kW par chale to lagbhag 36 units, 1.5 kW par 45 units aur 1.8 kW par 54 units. Ek LPG cylinder ke barabar cooking heat ke liye lagbhag 120 units lag sakti hain (illustrative).
 
### 9. Induction kharab hone ke mukhya kaaran kya hain?
Cooling vents band hona (neeche kapda ya akhbar), ubalte waqt doodh ya paani ka andar jana, aur bahut zyada voltage induction kharab hone ke mukhya kaaran hain.
 
### 10. Induction aur infrared cooktop me kya fark hai?
Induction magnetic field se seedha magnetic bartan ko garam karta hai (zyada efficient). Infrared heating element se glass top garam karta hai, isliye aluminium bartan bhi chalte hain, par same cooking heat ke liye zyada units lagti hain.
 
### 11. Kya induction ke liye alag wiring karwani padti hai?
Agar kitchen me pehle se heavy-duty circuit nahi hai, to DB se alag line (wire size aur MCB rating electrician IS 732:2019 ke hisaab se tay karega) aur 30 mA RCCB sabse safe rehta hai.
 
### 12. Kya 2000 W induction ko extension board par chala sakte hain?
Nahi. Normal extension board me patli wire aur chhote switch hote hain, jo lagatar 8–9 A load par garam ho sakte hain. Wall-mounted heavy-duty socket hi use karein.
 
### 13. Induction ki warranty kitni milti hai?
Zyada tar models me product par 1 saal ki warranty hoti hai. V-Guard VIC 2.0 BRE me coil par 3 saal alag se likhi hai, aur Prestige ke official listing me bhi coil par 3 saal likha hai. Warranty card me coverage aur exclusions padh kar kharidein.

- **4–5 log, heavy cooking:** Philips HD4938/01 (2100 W) ya value me Prestige PIC 16.0 Plus (2000 W).
