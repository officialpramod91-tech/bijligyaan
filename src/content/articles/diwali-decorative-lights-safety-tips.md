Diwali yaani roshni ka tyohar! Kartik Amavasya ki kaali raat me jab poora ghar chamchamati hui decorative lights aur ladiyon se jagmagata hai, to festive vibe alag hi hoti hai. Lekin is roshni aur khushi ke beech hum ek bohot badi cheez aksar ignore kar dete hain—**electrical safety**.

Diwali par decorative lights aur jhalar lagane ka sabse safe aur seedha tareeka hai: **BIS mark wali LED jhalar chunein, lagane se pehle wire ka insulation check karein, outdoor joints ko waterproof banayein, ek hi extension board par saara load na daalein, aur DB me RCCB ka test button zaroor dabayein.** Is saal Diwali 2026 me **8 November (Sunday)** ko hai aur Dhanteras 6 November se ghar-aangan ki roshni shuru ho jayegi, isliye tyohar ki taiyari me safety rules pehle se samajh lena zaroori hai.

Main pichle 10+ saal se senior electrician ka kaam kar raha hoon, aur har saal festive season me ground level par wahi same galtiyan dekhta hoon: purani cut-chhiti jhalar bina check kiye tang dena, balcony ki lohe ki railing par metal binding wire se lights baandhna, aur ek hi board me 5-6 multi-plug thuns dena. 

Is practical guide me hum Bureau of Indian Standards (**IS 10322 Part 5/Sec 7**, **IS 1293**, **IS 694**, **IS 732:2019**) aur **CEA Regulations 2023** ke mutabiq simple Hindi me samjhenge ki decorative lights safe kaise lagayein, load kaise calculate karein, aur emergency me parivar ko safe kaise rakhein.

## Short Summary (Key Takeaways)

- **IS 10322 Standard:** Sirf wahi lighting chain khareedein jis par Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) mark aur registered 8-digit CRS R-number ho.
- **Railing Safety:** Balcony ya terrace railing par jhalar baandhne ke liye lohe ke binding wire ki jagah hamesha non-conductive **plastic zip-ties (cable ties)** use kare.
- **Waterproof Outdoor Joints:** Nami aur subah ki os se bachne ke liye outdoor me IP-rated connectors lagayein aur wire me Drip Loop banaye.
- **Daisy Chaining Na Karein:** Ek extension board ke upar dusra multi-plug jodkar continuous load na badhayein; packets par likhi maximum connectable length follow karein.
- **RCCB Test Mandatory:** Lights on karne se pehle distribution board par **[Ghar Mein RCCB Lagana](/articles/ghar-mein-rccb-lagana-kyun-zaruri-hai/)** verify karein aur 'T' button dabakar trip test karein.
- **Emergency Rule:** Bijli ki aag par kabhi paani na daalein; pehle main switch band karein aur sukhi ret (dry sand) ya ABC fire extinguisher use karein (**Helpline: 112 / 101**).

## Diwali Par Bijli Ke Haadse Kyun Badh Jate Hain?

Diwali ke 5–7 dino me ghar ke normal load ke upar decorative outdoor load achanak judta hai aur lights lagatar 6 se 8 ghante chalti hai. Is dauran teen-chaar risk factors ek sath activate ho jaate hain:

1. **Brittle Insulation:** Saal bhar band box me rehne se purani jhalar ka PVC kadak ho jata hai aur chuhe taar kutar dete hain.
2. **Moisture Aur Dew Contact:** Kartik maheene ki halki sardi, outdoor os (ओस) aur nami phase conductor ke direct contact me aati hai.
3. **Socket Overload:** 6A ke light point par multi-pin adapter lagakar multiple heavy chains aur flood lights jod dena.
4. **Diya Aur Flammable Items:** Mitti ke diye, mombatti aur synthetic parde decorative wiring ke bilkul paas rakhna.

Delhi Fire Service aur electrical safety records ke mutabiq festive season me domestic aag ke lagbhag 70% mamle short circuit, loose connection sparking aur wire overheating ki wajah se hote hain.

> ⚠️ **Field Reality:** Log hazaron rupaye mehangi lights par kharch kar dete hain, lekin unhe connect karne ke liye ₹50 ka sasta, bina fuse wala local extension board use karte hain. Yeh local board terminal heating aur short circuit ka primary spot banta hai.

## Rule 1: Lights Lagane Se Pehle Poori Jhalar Check Karein

Diwali aate hi purani lights ke dabbe store room se nikale jaate hain. Bina visual inspection kiye unhe seedha switchboard me lagana sabse bada risk hai.

### Lagane Se Pehle Ye Visual Check Karein
Jhalar ko zameen par failakar poori lambai me dekhein:
- **Cracked ya Chhila Hua Insulation:** Wire par ungli firakar check karein. Kahi se bhi conductor khula dikhe toh use balcony me na lagayein.
- **Kale Ya Jale Nishan:** Plug pin, adaptor housing ya bulb holder par kala nishan purani sparking ya overheating ka saboot hai.
- **Dheele Plug Pins:** Agar 2-pin top socket me loose hil raha hai, toh continuous micro-sparking karega.
- **Garam Adaptor (Driver):** Agar light on karne ke 5 minute baad hi adaptor haath lagane layak na rahe ya jhanjhanahat de, toh circuit unsafe hai.

> 🔴 **Electrician's Golden Rule:** Cut wire par sasti black cello tape lagakar chalane ki galti kabhi na karein. Festive lights ka copper gauge bohot patla hota hai aur tape ke andar garmi trap hokar plastic ko aag pakda deti hai. Aisi ladi ko turant discard karein.

### Kaunsi Jhalar Chunein? (LED vs Purani Bulb Wali)

Festive shopping karte waqt purani bulb wali jhalar aur modern LED lights ke beech ka farq samajhna zaroori hai:

* **Garmi Aur Heating:** Purani filament bulb wali jhalar bohot jaldi garam hoti hain, jisse patli wire ka plastic pighalne aur short circuit hone ka risk rehta hai. Wahi modern LED lights ghanto chalne ke baad bhi cool-running rehti hain.
* **Bijli Ki Khapat:** Ek purani bulb wali ladi lagbhag 50W se 100W tak load leti hai, jabki certified LED ladi sirf 4W se 8W me chal jati hai—yani lagbhag 90% bijli ki bachat.
* **Safety Standard:** Purani series lights me ek bulb kharab hone par baki bulbs par voltage surge aur heat badhti hai. Dusri taraf, high-quality parallel LED chains me balanced current distribution rehta hai.

> 🔴 **Mera Faisla:** Purani filament bulb wali sasti ladiyon se poori tarah bachein. Hamesha BIS certified parallel LED string lights hi chunein jo safe bhi hain aur bijli bill par bhari bhi nahi padti.

**Mains 230V Wali Ya Low-Voltage Adaptor Wali?**  
Mains wali ladi 230V direct phase par chalti hai, jisme cut aane par direct fatal shock ka khatra hota hai. Modern LED jhalar 12V ya 24V ke DC adaptor (Class III / SELV) ke sath aati hain. Bachon ke kamre aur terrace railing par low-voltage adaptor wali lights lagana 10 guna zyada safe hota hai.

### BIS Mark Aur IS 10322: Packaging Par Certified Marking Check Karein

![BIS mark aur IS 10322 certified Diwali LED jhalar box packaging](/images/bis-mark-jhalar-packet-is-10322.webp)
*Diwali jhalar khareedte waqt packaging box par BIS CRS mark, R-number aur IS 10322 (Part 5/Sec 7) certification zaroor dekhein.*
Decorative lighting chains Bharat sarkar ke **IS 10322 (Part 5/Section 7)** standard ke tahat compulsory registration (CRS) me aati hain. Packet par yeh details verify karein:
1. **BIS Standard Mark** aur uske neeche 8-digit **R-number**.
2. **Rated Voltage (V) aur Wattage (W)** clear printed ho.
3. **IP Rating:** Outdoor use ke liye kam se kam IP44 marking honi chahiye.
4. Wire ka standard **IS 694** aur plug top ka **IS 1293** compliance.

## Rule 2: Balcony, Railing Aur Outdoor Lighting Ka Sahi Tarika

Outdoor lighting me moisture aur current leakage ka khatra sabse zyada hota hai. Yahan do baton ka dhyan rakhna sabse anivarya hai:

### Metal Railing Par Plastic Zip-Ties Use Karein

![Balcony railing par plastic zip tie se bandhi decorative jhalar](/images/diwali-lights-railing-zip-tie.webp)
*Metal railing par decorative lights lagate waqt taar ke insulation ko cutne se bachane ke liye non-conductive plastic cable ties (zip-ties) ka hi prayog karein.*
Field me 90% log jhalar ko lohe ke patle binding wire se grill par baandh dete hain. Hawa chalne se metal wire ladi ke patle insulation ko kaat deti hai aur live 230V phase direct poori metal grill me utar aata hai.
- Hamesha non-conductive **Plastic Cable Ties (Zip-Ties)** ya sooti dhaage ka prayog karein.
- Gate ya pole par lights lapetne se pehle insulation double-check karein.

### Outdoor Decorative Lights Ke Liye IP-Rated Waterproof Connectors

![Outdoor Decorative Lights Ke Liye IP-Rated Waterproof Connector](/images/ip-rated-waterproof-connector-diwali-lights.webp)
*Outdoor jhalar aur wiring joints ko os aur paani se safe rakhne ke liye heavy-duty IP68 waterproof connector.*
- **Outdoor Open Joints Se Bachein:** Do ladiyon ko jodh kar open tap lagakar mat chhodiye. Hamesha IP65 rated waterproof connector box use karein.
- **Drip Loop Banayein:** Wire ko switchboard ya extension socket me enter karane se pehle halka sa U-shape dekar neeche jhuka dein. Isse baarish ya os ka paani taar ke zariye socket me nahi ghusega, balki mod se neeche tapak jayega.
- **Self-Amalgamating Tape:** Normal PVC black tape outdoor ki nami ko nahi rok paati. Agar joint lagana majboori ho, toh self-amalgamating rubber tape kheench kar lapetein jo waterproof rubber layer bana leti hai.
- **Overhead Line Se Door Rahein:** Chhat se guzarne wali utility overhead wires ya khambhe ke paas kabhi jhalar na lagayein, aur na hi direct "katiya" daalkar power lein.

## Rule 3: Extension Board Overload Aur Load Calculation

### Load Kaise Calculate Karein?
Current nikalne ka seedha formula hai: **Current (A) = Total Watts ÷ 230V**. Ek standard 6A socket lagbhag 1,100 Watt tak continuous load safely jhel sakta hai.

| Load Setup | Total Watts | Current (230V par) | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **10 LED Jhalar (~8W each)** | ~80 W | ~0.35 Ampere | 100% Safe (Cool running) |
| **5 Purani Bulb Jhalar (~100W each)** | ~500 W | ~2.17 Ampere | Moderate Heating |
| **4 Halogen/Flood Lights (~150W each)** | ~600 W | ~2.60 Ampere | High Local Heating |
| **5 Bulb Jhalar + 4 Flood Lights (Same Socket)** | ~1,100 W | ~4.77 Ampere | **Extreme Overload Risk** |

> 📊 Poore ghar ka sanctioned load aur appliance current nikalne ke liye padhein hamara — **[Home Electrical Load Calculation Guide](/articles/home-electrical-load-calculation/)**.

### Daisy Chaining Se Bachein
Ek extension board me dusra multi-pin aur usme teesra adapter lagane ko **daisy chaining** kehte hain. Isse terminal points par contact resistance badhta hai, wire garam hoti hai aur socket pighal kar aag pakad leta hai.
- Jhalar ke packet par likhi **maximum connectable length** se zyada chains aapas me na jodein.
- Heavy focus lights ko normal 6A socket ke bajaye dedicated power socket par connect karein. Sahi wire size coordination ke liye dekhein:

## Field Case Study: Balcony Railing Me 230V Current Ka Asli Khatra

Pichle saal festive season me mujhe ek residential colony se emergency call aayi jahan terrace railing chhoone par ghar ke bachon ko current ke jhatke lag rahe the. 

Site par inspection ke dauran electrician ka fatal blunder samne aaya: **Unhone lohe ke patle binding wire se Chinese jhalar ko metal grill par tight baandha hua tha!**

Hawa chalne aur continuous vibration ki wajah se binding wire ne jhalar ke patle PVC insulation ko chhil diya tha, jisse live conductor direct lohe ki railing se touch ho gaya. Sabse badi laparwahi ye thi ki ghar ke main panel me RCCB install nahi tha aur earthing wire disconnected thi!

Humne turant supply isolate ki, saari binding wires hatakar plastic zip-ties lagayi, connections ko waterproof enclosures me shift kiya aur DB me 30mA RCCB install karwaya.

> 🔴 **Electrician's Golden Rule:** Lohe ki balcony par decorative lights lagate waqt metal binding wire ka use karna seedha maut ko dawat dena hai. Hamesha non-conductive plastic cable ties lagayein aur switch on karne se pehle earthing verify karein.

## Rule 4: Lights Lagane Se Pehle RCCB Aur MCB Test Karein

Aapne lights kitni bhi achhi lagayi hon, agar distribution board ka main protection failure stage par hai toh choti si wire leakage bhi deadly ho sakti hai.

### DB Connection Hierarchy (Sahi Sequence)

![House Electrical Distribution Board Wiring Diagram Incomer to RCCB and MCB](/images/distribution-board-wiring-diagram-rccb-mcb.webp)
*Ghar ke distribution board me sahi sequence: DP Incomer Breaker ke baad 30mA RCCB aur uske baad individual room MCBs lagayi jaati hain.*

Indian Standard **IS 732:2019** aur **CEA Regulations 2023** ke mutabiq DB ka sahi hierarchy sequence yeh hota hai:

1. **Main Incomer (DP MCB / Isolator - 32A/40A):** Poore panel ko short circuit aur heavy overload se cut karta hai.
2. **RCCB (Residual Current Circuit Breaker - 30mA):** Human shock aur earth leakage par 30 millisecond me trip karta hai.
3. **Sub-Circuit MCB (6A/10A B-Type):** Individual lighting aur fan lines ko protect karta hai.

### RCCB Testing: 'T' Test Button Se Protection Kaise Check Karein

![Electrician testing RCCB with T test button](/images/rccb-test-button-testing-guide.webp)
*Diwali lights lagane se pehle RCCB ke 'T' test button ko press karke tripping mechanism zaroor check karein.*
1. Distribution board par jayein aur RCCB par bana chhota **'T' (Test)** button dabayein.
2. Button dabate hi RCCB ka switch instant 'chatak' awaz ke sath neeche girna chahiye aur supply cut honi chahiye.
3. Agar 'T' button dabane par bhi switch trip nahi hota, toh RCCB internal mechanical damage ki wajah se fail ho chuki hai; use festival se pehle turant badalwayein.

## Rule 5: Fire Safety: Diya, Mombatti Aur Patakhe

- **Safe Distance:** Mitti ke diye, mombatti aur camphor ko electrical wires, switchboards aur extension cords se kam se kam **3 feet door** rakhein.
- **Pardon Par Lights Na Chipkayein:** Decorative lights ko synthetic pardo, kagaz ki sajawat ya sookhe phoolon ke direct contact me na lagayein.
- **Coil Karke Na Jalayein:** Jhalar ko poora khole bina bundle (coil) me switch ON na karein; heat dissipate na hone se wire aapas me chipak kar short circuit bana leti hai.
- **Night Shut-Off:** Sone se pehle ya ghar se bahar jaate waqt decorative lights band karein. Automation ke liye 10A Wi-Fi smart timer plug use karein.
- **Terrace Safety:** Chhat par paani ki tanki, overflow pipe ya AC outdoor unit ke paas extension boards bilkul na rakhein.

## Emergency: Electrical Fire Ya Shock Lagne Par Kya Karein?

1. **Pehle Supply Cutoff Karein:** Dhuan, spark ya burning smell aate hi sabse pehle main switch (Incomer DP MCB) band karein.
2. **Emergency Helpline Par Call Karein:** 
   - **National Emergency Number:** `112`
   - **Fire Service:** `101`
   - **Ambulance:** `102` ya `108`
3. **Shock Lage Vyakti Ko Direct Haath Se Na Pakdein:** Jab tak supply cut na ho, sukhi lakdi (dry wood), plastic pipe ya rubber slipper se vyakti ko taar se alag karein.
4. **Paani Bilkul Na Daalein:** Bijli ki aag par paani daalne se current paani ke raste aapke shareer me daud sakta hai. Chhoti aag bujhane ke liye **Sukhi Ret (Dry Sand)** ya **ABC Dry Powder / CO2 Fire Extinguisher** use karein.
5. Agar vyakti behosh hai aur saans nahi chal rahi ho, toh turant CPR shuru karein aur ambulance ka intezaar karein.

## Quick Checklist: Diwali Lighting Do's And Don'ts

### ✅ Kya Karein (Do's)
* **Plastic Zip-Ties:** Balcony railing par lights baandhne ke liye non-conductive plastic cable ties use karein.
* **IP-Rated Connectors:** Outdoor connections par waterproof connectors lagayein aur wire me Drip Loop banayein.
* **RCCB Testing:** Diwali se pehle distribution board me jakar 'T' test button daba kar check karein.
* **BIS Certified Lights:** Hamesha **IS 10322** certified aur R-number wali LED jhalar hi khareedein.
* **Night Shut-Off:** Sone se pehle ya bahar jaate waqt decorative lights ke switch band karein.

---

### ❌ Kya Na Karein (Don'ts)
* **Lohe Ki Taar:** Railing par ladi baandhne ke liye lohe ke binding wire ka use kabhi na karein.
* **Sasti Black Tape:** Outdoor joints par normal PVC ya stationery tape lagakar khule me na chhodein.
* **Daisy Chaining:** Ek hi 6A socket par 3–4 extension boards ek ke upar ek na lagayein.
* **Damaged Wire:** Nangi ya cut-chhiti purani jhalar par tape lagakar chalane ka risk na lein.
* **Diya Near Wires:** Mitti ke diye ya mombatti ko decorative wire aur pardo ke paas na rakhein.

- **5 Purani Filament Jhalar (~500W total, 6 ghante, 5 din):**  
  (500 × 6 × 5) ÷ 1000 = **15 Units** (Lagbhag ₹100–₹120).

## Aksar Pooche Jane Wale Sawal (FAQs)

### 1. Diwali par decorative lights safe kaise lagayein?
BIS mark wali LED jhalar chunein, lagane se pehle wire aur plug visual check karein, railing par plastic zip-ties lagayein, outdoor joints ko IP-rated connector se seal karein, extension board par overload na karein aur RCCB ka test button dabakar protection verify karein.

### 2. Ek extension board par kitni jhalar lagayi ja sakti hain?
Manufacturer ke packet par likhi maximum connectable length ya count se zyada kabhi na jodein. Modern LED ladiyan kam load leti hain (4W-8W), par socket par multiple multi-pins lagakar daisy-chaining karne se terminal heat hokar aag pakad sakta hai.

### 3. Balcony ki railing chhoone par jhatka lage to kya karein?
Turant saari outdoor lights ka switch band karein aur plug board se bahar nikalein. Railing se metal binding wires hatakar plastic zip-ties lagayein aur certified electrician se ghar ki neutral leakage aur earthing continuity test karwayein.

### 4. Outdoor lights ke liye waterproofing kaise karein?
IP44 ya IP65 rated waterproof outdoor connectors aur sockets use karein. Wire ko socket me daalne se pehle drip loop (neeche curve) banayein aur joints par normal black tape ke bajaye self-amalgamating rubber tape lapetein.

### 5. Jhalar lagate hi MCB baar-baar trip kyu hoti hai?
MCB trip hone ke do main karan hain: ya toh jhalar ke live aur neutral copper taar aapas me touch hokar dead short circuit bana rahe hain, ya us circuit ka total current MCB ki rated capacity (jaise 6A) se zyada ho gaya hai. Faulty ladi ko alag karein.

### 6. BIS mark wali jhalar kaise pehchanein?
Packaging par Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) mark ke sath 8-digit ka CRS R-number, Indian Standard code **IS 10322 (Part 5/Sec 7)**, rated voltage (230V) aur wattage clearly print hona anivarya hai.

### 7. LED jhalar behtar hain ya purani bulb wali?
LED jhalar behtar hain kyunki yeh chalte waqt heat generate nahi karti, 80% bijli bachati hain, aur inka shock risk purani high-voltage incandescent series chains ke muqable bohot kam hota hai.

### 8. RCCB ka test button kaise check karein?
Distribution board par RCCB par bane chote 'T' button ko press karein. Button dabate hi mechanism switch ko instantly trip karke supply cut kar dega. Agar button dabane par trip na ho toh RCCB replace karein.

### 9. Kya poori raat decorative lights chalu rakhna safe hai?
Sone se pehle ya ghar se bahar jaate waqt decorative lights off karna hamesha safe rehta hai. Continuous run se adaptors overheat ho sakte hain aur raat ke waqt kisi chote spark ka pata nahi chal pata.

### 10. Electrical fire ya shock lagne par turant kya karein?
Sabse pehle distribution board se Main Switch band karein aur helpline **112 / 101** par call karein. Victim ko sukhi lakdi ya plastic pipe se alag karein. Bijli ki aag par paani bilkul na daalein; sukhi ret (dry sand) ya ABC fire extinguisher use karein.
