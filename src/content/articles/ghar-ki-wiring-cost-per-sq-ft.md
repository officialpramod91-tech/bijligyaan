## Short Summary (Key Takeaways)

* **Average Cost:** 2026 me concealed modular wiring ka total kharcha (Material + Labour) **₹120 se ₹185 per sq ft** ke beech aata hai.
* **Budget Share:** Total budget ka lagbhag **45% copper wire**, **20% modular switch/plates**, **12% conduit pipe/boxes**, **8% DB protection**, aur **15%–20% electrician labour** par kharch hota hai.
* **Labour Rate:** Metro shehron me **₹20 – ₹28 per sq ft**, jabki Tier-2/3 shehron me **₹16 – ₹22 per sq ft** chalta hai. Per-point theka aamtaur par **₹180 se ₹260 per point** hota hai.
* **Safety First:** Distribution board me incomer ke baad RCD (ghar ke liye aam taur par 30mA RCCB) lagana chahiye. CEA Regulations 2023 ke mutabiq installation ki supply RCD se control honi chahiye.
* **Electrician's Rule:** Deewar todkar bar-bar taar badla nahi ja sakta. Isliye wire aur MCB/RCCB hamesha certified ISI mark hi lagayein; paisa bachana ho toh fancy plates ke bajaye standard modular plate chunein.

> 💡 **Budget Tip:** Wiring budget banate waqt sirf 6 ya 8-way box lene ki galti na karein. Future expansion aur double door safety ke liye sahi box lene ka kharcha aur tarika jaanein: [MCB Box (DB Box) Price Aur Selection Guide (2026)](/articles/mcb-box-db-box-kaise-chune/).

## Step-by-Step Material Breakdown: Kharcha Kahan Kitna Hota Hai?

Total bill ko mukhya 4 hisson me baanta jata hai:

### 1. Copper Wires (Budget Ka ~45%)
Wiring ka sabse bada hissa copper wire hota hai. Indian Standard **IS 694** ke mutabiq multi-strand pure copper conductor aur **FRLS (Flame Retardant Low Smoke)** insulation wali wire hi lagayein, jo aag lagne par kam dhuan aur kam acid gas chhodti hai.

Ek standard 2BHK (1000 sq ft) ghar me lagne wale average wire bundles (90 meter coil) ka hisaab:
* **1.0 sq mm (Earthing line):** 2 se 3 coil — ₹1,100 se ₹1,400 per coil.
* **1.5 sq mm (Lights & Fans circuit):** 4 se 5 coil — ₹1,800 se ₹2,300 per coil.
* **2.5 sq mm (Power sockets, Fridge, Geyser):** 4 se 6 coil — ₹3,000 se ₹3,800 per coil.
* **4.0 sq mm (AC lines, Kitchen power):** 2 se 3 coil — ₹4,800 se ₹5,900 per coil.
* **6.0 sq mm (Main Incomer line):** 1 coil — ₹7,200 se ₹8,800 per coil.

### 2. Modular Switches Aur Accessories (Budget Ka ~20%)
* **Standard Modular Fitting:** Safed rang ki basic modular plates (jaise Anchor, Havells Crabtree, Legrand, Schneider) lagane par 2BHK ka kharcha **₹20,000 se ₹28,000** me nipat jata hai.
* **Fancy Glass / Touch Plates:** Agar decorative glass plates ya designer wooden finish chunte hain, toh yahi kharcha badhkar **₹50,000 se ₹75,000+** ho sakta hai.

### 3. Conduit Pipes Aur Concealed Metal Boxes (Budget Ka ~12%)
* Hamesha **IS 9537 Part 3** certified medium ya heavy duty (20mm aur 25mm) PVC conduit pipe hi lagayein. Saste pipe slab dhalte waqt dab jaate hain.
* Ek 1000 sq ft ghar me 80-110 PVC pipes aur switches, fan boxes, foot lights milakar lagbhag **45 se 60 metal boxes** lagte hain, jinka kharcha lagbhag **₹12,000 se ₹16,000** hota hai.

### 4. MCB, RCCB Aur Distribution Board Panel (Budget Ka ~8%)
* **8-Way ya 12-Way SPN Enclosure Box:** ₹1,500 – ₹3,000.
* **Main Incomer (DP Isolator / MCB):** ₹600 – ₹1,100.
* **RCCB (30mA Residual Current Device):** ₹2,400 – ₹3,800.
* **Individual Circuit MCBs (6A, 10A, 16A, 20A):** 8 se 12 pieces (₹180 – ₹280 per piece).

> ⚡ **Electrician Pro Tip (Load Pehle Calculate Karein):**  
> Wiring ka samaan aur MCB khareedne se pehle apne ghar ke sabhi appliances (AC, Geyser, Fridge, Induction) ka running aur starting load zaroor calculate kar lein. Agar bina load samjhe patli wire ya galat rating ki MCB laga di, toh tripping aur wire jalne ka khatra rehta hai.

## Labour Charges: Electrician Ka Theka Kaise De?

Field me electrician ke kaam ka theka lene ke 3 mukhya tareeqe hote hain:

* **1. Per Square Foot Rate (₹18 – ₹26 per sq ft):**
  * **Kiske Liye Best Hai:** Naye makaan ki complete wiring ke liye sabse transparent tareeqa.
  * **Faida:** Budget pehle se lock rehta hai aur room me extra light ya point badhne par bill achanak nahi badhta.

* **2. Per Point System (₹180 – ₹260 per point):**
  * **Kiske Liye Best Hai:** Chhota repair, renovation ya ghar me 2–3 naye board lagane ke liye.
  * **Savdhani:** Pure makaan me point system lene se final bill bohot mehenga pad sakta hai.

* **3. Lump-Sum Fixed Theka (Total Kaam Ka Ek Contract):**
  * **Kiske Liye Best Hai:** Jab drawing aur total points ka scope pehle se clear ho.
  * **Faida:** Kaam fast hota hai, bas pehle se likhwa lein ki DB termination aur earthing rate me shamil hai.

### Kaam Ke 3 Stages Aur Payment Schedule
1. **Stage 1 (30% Payment):** Slab piping + deewaron ki jhirri (wall chasing) kaat kar conduit pipe aur metal box jamana.
2. **Stage 2 (40% Payment):** Plaster ke baad taar kheench kar (wire pulling) distribution board (DB) me dressing karna.
3. **Stage 3 (30% Payment):** Paint ke baad switch, socket, sheet fitting karna aur supply on karke test karna.

## Field Case Study: 'Per Point' Ke Chakkar Mein ₹21,400 Ka Extra Kharcha

Pichle mahine site par ek client ne 1200 sq ft makaan ke liye **₹170 per point** par theka de diya, yeh soch kar ki square foot ke muqable ye sasta padega.

False ceiling profile lights, foot lamps, two-way switches aur AC points milakar total **240 points** ban gaye:
* 240 points × ₹170 = **₹40,800 fitting labour**.
* Iske upar electrician ne MCB distribution board dressing ka ₹4,000 aur earthing pit digging ka ₹3,000 alag maang liya. Total bill bana **₹47,800**.

Agar wahi client 1200 sq ft par **₹22 per sq ft** ka lumpsum theka lock karta, toh pura kaam (DB aur earthing sahit) **₹26,400** me ho jata. Client ka bina wajah **₹21,400 ka extra kharcha** ho gaya.

> 🔴 **Electrician's Advice:** Naye makaan me hamesha **Square Foot Rate** ya **Lump-sum Fixed Scope Theka** karein. Pehle se likhwa lein ki piping, wire pulling, switch fitting aur DB dressing sab isme shamil hai.

## Indian Standards Aur CEA Regulations: Wiring Niyam

* **IS 732:2019:** Alag circuits (lights, power sockets, AC, geyser) ke liye alag protection rakhein, aur conduit pipe me hadd se zyada taar na bharein, kyunki garmi se wire ki current carry karne ki kshamta ghat jati hai.
* **IS 694:** Domestic single-core copper wire ISI certified standard. Wire par manufacturer ka licence number aur batch code check karein.
* **CEA Regulations 2023:** Installation ki supply RCD (jaise 30mA RCCB) se control honi chahiye, jo leakage current detect hote hi supply kaat sake.
* **IS 9537 (Part 3):** Deewaron me use hone wale conduit pipes ISI standard PVC ke hone chahiye jo aasan physical impact se na tootey.

## Aksar Pooche Jane Wale Sawal (FAQs)

### 1. 1000 sq ft makaan ki complete electrical wiring mein kitna kharcha aayega?
1000 sq ft (2BHK) ghar ki concealed modular wiring mein material aur labour milakar aam taur par ₹1,05,000 se ₹1,43,000 ka kharcha aata hai. Ye shehar, brand aur points ki ginti ke hisaab se badal sakta hai.

### 2. Ghar ki wiring ka sabse transparent theka kaunsa hota hai?
Per square foot ya fixed-scope lump-sum theka aam taur par zyada transparent hota hai, kyunki points badhne par bill achanak nahi badhta. Theka dene se pehle likhwa lein ki slab piping, chasing, wire pulling, switch fitting aur DB dressing rate mein shamil hai.

### 3. Ghar ki wiring ke liye kaun si copper wire lagani chahiye?
ISI mark (IS 694) wali multi-strand copper wire lein. Safety ke liye FRLS wire behtar choice hai, jo aag lagne par kam dhuan chhodti hai. Sirf brand nahi, wire par IS 694, size aur licence number bhi dekhein.

### 4. Kya har kamre ke liye alag MCB lagana zaroori hai?
Har kamre ke liye alag MCB zaroori nahi, par lights/fans, sockets, kitchen, geyser aur har AC ke circuit alag rakhna achhi practice hai. Isse ek circuit mein fault hone par poore ghar ki supply band nahi hoti.

### 5. Open wiring aur concealed wiring ke kharche mein kitna antar hota hai?
Open (surface) wiring mein deewar mein chasing aur plaster repair nahi hoti, isliye ye aam taur par sasti padti hai. Lekin naye ghar ke liye safety aur durability ke hisaab se concealed conduit wiring behtar maani jati hai.
