## <a id="bill-banta"></a>Bijli Ka Bill Kaise Banta Hai? (Units Se Zyada Cheezein)

Bill sirf units ka nahi hota. Aam taur par isme ye hissa hote hain (naam aur rates state ke hisaab se alag):

### Energy charge (slab ke hisaab se)
Units badhne par rate bhi badh sakta hai, yaani agle slab me jaate hi **har extra unit mehngi** padti hai. Isliye thode se extra units bhi bill ko bahut badha sakte hain.

### Fixed ya demand charge
Aapke **sanctioned load** (kW) ke hisaab se har mahine lagne wala charge. Bijli kam jalao tab bhi ye lagta hai.

### Electricity duty, surcharge aur fuel/power purchase adjustment
Kai jagah duty, surcharge aur fuel/power-purchase cost adjustment alag se judta hai, jo mahine ya quarter ke hisaab se badal sakta hai.

### Arrear aur late fee
Pichhla baaki ya late payment charge bhi isi bill me juda hota hai.

> **Smart meter wale gharon me** kuch DISCOM time-of-day rate bhi lagate hain. Apne DISCOM ke tariff order ya bill ke detail se dekhein.

## <a id="meter-test"></a>1-Minute Meter Creep Test (Safe Tareeke Se)

Ye test bata sakta hai ki **meter ke baad** koi load ya meter ki dikkat to nahi. Isme **meter ke seal ya terminal ko chhune ki zaroorat nahi.**

![1-Minute Meter Creep Test step-by-step and Watt calculation scale](/images/meter-creep-test-load-calculation.webp)

### Steps
1. **Ghar ke saare switch aur main MCB OFF** kare. Fridge, router, CCTV jaise sab band ho jayen.
2. Meter ke paas jakar **pulse LED** dekhein. Uske paas **imp/kWh** likha hota hai (jaise 3200 ya 1600).
3. 1–2 minute dekhein.
   - **LED blink na kare ya bahut rare ho:** sab theek lagta hai.
   - **LED baar-baar blink kare:** koi load abhi bhi meter ke baad connected hai, ya meter ki dikkat hai.

### Pulse se load kaise nikalein
Do blink ke beech ka samay (second) napkar load ka andaza lagayein:

**Watt ≈ 3,600,000 ÷ (imp/kWh × do pulse ke beech ke second)**

| Meter | Do pulse ke beech | Lagbhag load |
|---|---|---|
| 3200 imp/kWh | 30 second | ~37 W |
| 3200 imp/kWh | 10 second | ~112 W |
| 3200 imp/kWh | 5 second | ~225 W |

### Agar main MCB OFF hone par bhi LED blink kare
- **Pehle check kare:** koi connection **main MCB se pehle** to nahi juda (jaise purana alag line, CCTV, pump ya kisi aur ka tapping)? Ye kaam **electrician** se karwayein.
- **Meter ki dikkat** bhi ho sakti hai. Kuch meters ki LED alag status bhi dikhati hai, isliye meter ka manual ya DISCOM se confirm kare.
- Dono sambhavna me **DISCOM ko meter test** ke liye likhit application dein (neeche).

> **Meter ke seal ya terminal cover ko kholna ya chhedna na kare.** Wo DISCOM ka hissa hai aur isse tampering ka mamla ban sakta hai.

## <a id="wajah"></a>Bijli Ka Bill Zyada Aane Ki 10 Wajah (Aam Se Kam Aam Ke Order Me)

### 1. Mausam ka load aur slab ka jump
Garmi me AC aur cooler, sardi me geyser aur room heater. Thodi si extra units bhi agle slab me pahunchakar bill bahut badha sakti hain. Sardi me geyser aur heater ka load kaise sambhalein: [sardi me geyser aur heater ka bill kaise sambhalein](/articles/winter-me-bijli-bill-kaise-kam-kare/). AC ke star aur running cost ka fark: [AC ka star rating aur running cost](/articles/5-star-vs-3-star-vs-inverter-ac-bijli-bill-comparison/).

### 2. Billing ya meter reading ki galti
Estimated billing, galat reading, ya doosre meter ki reading apke consumer number par chadh jana. Bill ki reading ko meter ki photo se milayein.

### 3. Tariff, fixed charge aur sanctioned load
Tariff ya fuel adjustment badhne par bill badhta hai. Agar ghar ka **connected load sanctioned load se kaafi zyada** ho, to kuch DISCOM me fixed charge ya penalty alag se lag sakti hai (**ye sabhi DISCOM me nahi hota**). Apna load samjhein: [sanctioned load aur connected load ka fark](/articles/home-electrical-load-calculation/).

### 4. Purane ya kam efficiency wale appliance
8–10 saal purana fridge ya window AC zyada bijli khata hai. Naye BEE star wale appliance kam bijli lete hain. Geyser ke star aur capacity ka asar: [geyser ke star aur capacity ka bijli par asar](/articles/best-geyser-for-small-family-in-india/).

### 5. Standby (vampire) load
TV, set-top box, router, charger aur microwave jaise device band dikhne par bhi kuch watt khate hain. Hisaab seedha hai: **10 W × 24 ghante × 30 din ≈ 7.2 units/mahina.** Asli load **plug-in watt meter** se napein, kisi ke "30–35 units" ke daave par bharosa na kare.

### 6. Pump ya motor ki kharabi
Agar paani ki motor ko tanki bharne me pehle se zyada samay lag raha hai, to impeller ghisna, bearing jam hona ya line me rukawat ho sakti hai. **Clamp meter se running current** napkar technician se jaanch karwayein. Motor ka current naye hone par kitna tha, wo name plate par hota hai.

### 7. Fridge ka gasket aur hawa ka leak
Darwaze ka rubber gasket loose ho to bahar ki garam hawa andar jati hai aur compressor zyada chalta hai. **Paper test:** ek kagaz darwaze me dabakar band kare aur kheenchein. Agar bina rukawat ke nikal jaye, to gasket check karwayein.

### 8. Geyser ka scaling aur galat use
Khare paani me element par scale jama hone se garam hone me zyada samay lag sakta hai. Geyser ko **24 ghante ON** na chhodein. Nahane se 20–25 minute pehle ON kare aur baad me OFF. Scaling saaf karna ya element badalna **technician ka kaam** hai, supply band karke. Geyser ki wiring aur safety: [geyser ki alag line aur MCB](/articles/geyser-lagane-se-pehle-kya-dhyan-rakhe/).

### 9. Loose joint aur chhota leakage (aksar chhota asar)
- **Loose joint** garam hota hai aur thodi bijli waste karta hai, aur aag ka khatra bhi hota hai. Pehchan: [loose joint aur voltage drop ki pehchaan](/articles/ghar-me-low-voltage-kyun-aati-hai/).
- **Leakage current** (jaise earth fault) meter se guzarta hai isliye units me count hota hai, par chhota leakage bahut kam units banata hai. Hisaab: 30 mA ka leakage ≈ 7 W, yaani lagbhag **5 units/mahina.** Bada leakage hota to **RCCB trip** kar jati. Isliye “neutral leakage se bill double” jaisi baat aam taur par sahi nahi hai. Earthing aur leakage ka rishta: [earthing aur leakage current ka rishta](/articles/earthing-kya-hai/).

### 10. Meter ki kharabi ya tapping
Meter tez chalna rare hai, par asambhav nahi. Ek aur sambhavna **main MCB se pehle juda koi extra load ya tapping** hai. Upar ka meter creep test aur DISCOM ka **meter accuracy test** isi liye hai.

## <a id="audit"></a>Apne Ghar Ka Load Audit Kaise kare

### 1. Plug-in watt meter se
Fridge, TV, router, microwave jaise appliances ka watt aur units napein. Seedha formula: **Units = kW × ghante.** Jaise 2 kW geyser 1 ghanta chale to 2 units.

### 2. Clamp meter se (electrician)
Main wire par poore ghar ka current napein, aur ek-ek karke appliance ON karke dekhein ki current kitna badhta hai. Isse bada load turant pakda jata hai. Heavy load ke liye wire ka size: [heavy load ke liye wire ka size](/articles/ghar-ki-wiring-wire-size-guide/).

## <a id="plan"></a>Bill Kam Karne Ka Realistic Plan

1. **Bill aur meter ki reading milayein** aur photo rakhein.
2. **AC 24–26°C par chalayein.** 24°C BEE ka default setting maana jata hai, aur har degree badhane par bijli kuch % kam hoti hai (aam taur par lagbhag 6% bataya jata hai). 16°C par room jaldi thanda nahi hota, bas compressor zyada chalta hai.
3. **Geyser ko timer ya manual ON–OFF** par rakhein, 24 ghante ON na rakhein.
4. **Fridge ka gasket aur piche ki safai** dekhein. Fridge ko deewar se kuch gap par rakhein.
5. **Standby load** ke liye switch wali power strip lagayein, aur raat ko band kare.
6. **Purane heavy appliance** (purana AC, geyser, pump) ki efficiency check kare.
7. **Sanctioned load** apne asli load se match kare.
8. **Lambe samay ke liye solar** sochein: system type ke liye [solar lagwane se pehle system type](/articles/on-grid-vs-off-grid-vs-hybrid-solar-system/) aur bill ka hisaab [net metering se bill ka hisaab](/articles/solar-net-metering-kya-hai/). ## <a id="shikayat"></a>Bill Ya Meter Par Shikayat Kaise kare

1. **Pehle DISCOM ko likhit complaint** dein (sub-division office/app/portal), aur **1912** par bhi call kare. **Docket number** note kare.
2. **Meter test ki application** dein. Aam taur par iske liye **tay fee** lagti hai, aur meter galat nikla to fee wapas ya adjust ho jati hai. Fee, test ka tareeka aur timeline **aapki state ke Supply Code** me hote hain.
3. **Test report** maangein. Rights of Consumers Rules ke hisaab se test ke nateeje par aapati ho to **third-party testing lab** ka option bhi hota hai (list aapke state commission ki).
4. **Jawab na mile ya santusht na ho** to **CGRF**, phir **Electricity Ombudsman** me jayein.
5. Saath me **meter ki photo-reading**, bill ki copy aur complaint ka docket rakhein.

> Bill ki galti sudhaar (back-billing) kitne mahine tak hoti hai ye state ke rule par depend karta hai. Apne Supply Code me dekhein.

## <a id="is-cea"></a>IS Standards Aur CEA Regulations: Is Topic Me Kya Lagu Hai

| Reference | Is topic me matlab |
|---|---|
| **IS 732** | Ghar ki wiring ka code of practice (installation aur testing) |
| **IS 3043** | Earthing |
| **IS 12640 / IEC 61008** | RCCB. 30 mA RCCB rated leakage par aam taur par max ~300 ms me trip hoti hai |
| **Meter standards** | Meter ki accuracy aur no-load (creep) test meter ke standards me aate hain (jaise IS 13779, IS 16444 jaise references) aur CEA ke meter regulations. **Exact standard DISCOM/vendor se confirm kare.** |
| **CEA (Measures relating to Safety and Electric Supply) Regulations, 2023** | Installation ka test aur verify, leakage se bachav, RCD ka provision, qualified person |
| **Electricity (Rights of Consumers) Rules, 2020** | Meter testing aur grievance redressal ke consumer rights (state ke rules ke saath) |

- **RCCB bill kam nahi karti**, ye shock ke risk ko kam karti hai: [RCCB bill nahi, shock se bachati hai](/articles/ghar-mein-rccb-lagana-kyun-zaruri-hai/).
- **Meter ke seal** DISCOM ke hain. Unhe kholna ya chhedna na kare.

> Standards ke **edition aur clause** badalte rehte hain. **Latest edition BIS ki site aur CEA/Gazette ka original text** dekhein. Ye legal advice nahi hai.

## <a id="cases"></a>Typical Field Scenarios (Aam Taur Par Aise Cases Milte Hain)

> Ye **typical scenarios** hain jo aksar dekhne ko milte hain, kisi khaas ghar ke asli case nahi. Asli nateeje ghar ke hisaab se alag hote hain.

### Scenario 1: Sardi me achanak bhaari bill
Ghar me ek se do geyser aur room heater roz kai ghante chalte hain, aur geyser ko 24 ghante ON rakha jata hai. Units agle slab me chale jate hain aur bill bahut badh jata hai.
**Kya milta hai:** Watt meter/clamp meter se geyser aur heater ka load aur chalne ka samay.
**Fix:** Geyser ko nahane ke time ON karna, timer lagana, aur heater ka use kam. Bill ka bada hissa isi se kam hota hai.

### Scenario 2: Bill me estimated reading aur arrear
Meter ki reading nahi li gayi to average units ka bill aaya, aur agle mahine adjustment ke saath arrear juda hua.
**Kya milta hai:** Meter ki photo ki reading aur bill ki reading me fark.
**Fix:** Reading ke saath written complaint, aur correct reading par bill ka sudhaar.

### Scenario 3: Main MCB OFF par bhi LED blink
Ghar ke saare switch aur main MCB OFF hain, par meter ki LED har 10 second me blink karti hai (3200 imp/kWh meter par ~112 W ka load).
**Kya milta hai:** Electrician ne dekha ki ek alag line **main MCB se pehle** juda hua tha (jaise purani line ya extra load).
**Fix:** Us line ko sahi tareeke se MCB ke baad karwana, aur zarurat ho to DISCOM se meter test.
> Ye jaanch aur kaam licensed electrician aur DISCOM kare. Meter ka seal na kholein.

## Aam Galtiyan Jo Log Bijli Bill Badhne Par Karte Hain

### 1. Bina Bill Padhe Seedha “Meter Tez Chal Raha Hai” Maan Lena
Zyada bill aate hi log sabse pehle meter ko dosh dete hain aur meter badalne ke peeche bhagte hain. Asal me 10 me se 8 baar meter bilkul sahi hota hai—ya toh garmi/sardi me heavy appliance chalne se slab jump hua hota hai, ya pichhle mahine estimated bill aane ke baad is mahine adjustment juda hota hai. Pehle purane bill ki reading aur meter ki photo milayein, phir koi faisla lein.

### 2. RCCB Ko Bijli Bachane Wala Device Samajhna
Kai log dukandar ya kisi ke kehne par is ummeed me RCCB lagwa lete hain ki isse current leakage rukega aur bijli ka bill kam hoga. Sachchai ye hai ki RCCB aapke **bill ko kam nahi karti**, balki agar kisi appliance ya wiring me current leak ho raha hai toh turant trip hokar aapko **shock lagne aur aag se bachati hai**. Bill bachane ke liye aapko load aur appliances par kaam karna hoga.

### 3. Khud Electrician Bankar Meter Ka Seal Ya Terminal Kholna
Bill zyada aane par gusse ya shak me kabhi bhi energy meter ke terminal cover, seal ya service wire ko kholne ki koshish na kare. Meter aur uska seal DISCOM ki property hoti hai—ispar chhed-chhad karne par bijli chori ya tampering ka bhari jurmana (penalty) lag sakta hai. Aap sirf apne ghar ke main MCB ke baad ki wiring check karwayein.

### 4. WhatsApp Ke "Standby Load" Daavon Par Bharosa Karna
Internet par kai log likhte hain ki sirf TV aur charger plug me chhodne se mahine ka ₹1,500 bill badh jata hai—ye sach nahi hai. Chhote standby devices se sirf kuch units ka hi fark padta hai. Agar aap sach me pakadna chahte hain ki kaunsa device kitni bijli kha raha hai, toh andaza lagane ke bajay ₹500–₹700 ka ek **plug-in watt meter** laakar fridge, TV aur geyser ka asli load khud check kare.

### 5. Office Jaakar Zabani Shikayat Karna (Bina Proof Ke)
Bijli office ke chakkar lagane aur counter par bol kar aane se bill kam nahi hota. Jab tak aapke paas **meter ki date ke sath photo-reading**, pichhle bill ki copy aur **online/1912 par darj complaint ka docket number** nahi hoga, aapki baat record me nahi aayegi. Hamesha likhit ya portal par proof ke sath complaint darj kare.

## <a id="faqs"></a>Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawal (FAQs)

### 1. Bijli ka bill achanak zyada kyun aata hai?
Sabse aam wajahein mausam ka load (AC ya geyser/heater), slab ka jump, estimated ya galat reading, aur tariff ya charges ka badalna hain. Pehle bill ki reading ko meter ki photo se milayein, phir load audit kare.

### 2. Kya MCB band karne ke baad bhi meter chal sakta hai?
Aam taur par nahi. Agar saare load aur main MCB band hone par bhi meter ki pulse LED baar-baar blink kare, to koi load main MCB se pehle juda ho sakta hai ya meter ki dikkat ho sakti hai. Electrician se jaanch karwayein aur DISCOM se meter test ki application dein.

### 3. Kya neutral leakage se bijli ka bill badhta hai?
Leakage current meter se guzarta hai isliye units me count hota hai, par chhota leakage bahut kam units banata hai (jaise 30 mA lagbhag 5 units/mahina). Bada leakage hota to RCCB trip karti hai. Isliye isse bill double hone ki baat aam taur par sahi nahi hai.

### 4. Standby power se kitna bill aata hai?
Ye device par depend karta hai. Jaise 10 W ka device 24 ghante chale to lagbhag 7.2 units/mahina banta hai. Asli load plug-in watt meter se napein.

### 5. Smart meter tez chalne par kya kare?
Meter ki photo-reading rakhein, bill se milayein, aur 1-minute creep test kare. Phir DISCOM me likhit complaint aur meter test ki application dein. Jawab na mile to CGRF aur Electricity Ombudsman ka rasta hai.

### 6. Kya AC ko 16 degree par chalane se room jaldi thanda hota hai?
Nahi. Thermostat temperature tay karta hai, cooling ki speed nahi. 16°C par compressor zyada chalta hai aur bijli zyada lagti hai. 24°C BEE ka default setting maana jata hai.

### 7. Kya RCCB lagane se bijli bill kam hota hai?
Nahi. RCCB shock aur badi leakage se bachati hai, bill kam nahi karti. Bill kam karne ke liye load, appliance aur billing par dhyan dein.

### 8. Bijli bill ki shikayat kaise kare?
Pehle DISCOM ko likhit complaint dein aur 1912 par docket number lein. Zarurat ho to meter test ki application dein. Jawab na mile ya santusht na ho to CGRF aur phir Electricity Ombudsman me jayein. Meter ki photo-reading aur bill saath rakhein.

Bill zyada aane par pehle **bill padhein, meter ki reading milayein aur load audit kare.** Zyada tar jhatka mausam ke load, slab aur billing se aata hai.
