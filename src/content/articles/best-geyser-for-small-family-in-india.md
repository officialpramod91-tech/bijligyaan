## Storage Geyser vs Instant Geyser: Kaun Sa Better Hai?

Dono geysers ka purpose aur mechanical design bilkul alag hota hai:

### 1. Storage Geyser (Small Family Ke Liye Sabse Best)
- **Khaasiyat:** Isme 15 se 25 litre paani ka insulated tank hota hai jo paani ko ghanton tak garam store karke rakhta hai.
- **Faayde:** Shower connection ke liye perfect hai, steady continuous garam paani deta hai, aur 2kW element par chalta hai jisse ghar ke meter par achanak jhatka nahi padta.
- **Kiske Liye:** Small aur medium families jahan subah back-to-back log nahate hain.

### 2. Instant Geyser (1L se 3L)
- **Khaasiyat:** Isme chhota heating chamber hota hai aur 3000W (3kW) ka heavy element paani ko instantly garam karta hai.
- **Nuksan:** Shower ke liye bilkul bekaar hai (pressure aate hi paani thanda ho jata hai) aur high instantaneous peak load khinchta hai.
- **Kiske Liye:** Kitchen sink (bartan dhone) ya single bachelors ke bucket use ke liye.

## Geyser Khareedte Waqt In 5 Technical Features Ko Zaroor Check Karein

Showroom me sirf look dekhkar na lein, carton par ye 5 technical specifications zaroor check karein:

1. **BEE Star Rating:** Hamesha **4-Star ya 5-Star** rated model hi lein. 5-Star geyser me PUF insulation thick hoti hai, jisse paani 4–6 ghante tak bina bijli kharch kiye garam rehta hai.
2. **Inner Tank Coating:** Simple stainless steel tank khare paani me 2–3 saal me leak ho jata hai. Hamesha **Glass-Lined, Titanium ya Polymer coated tank** hi chunein.
3. **Magnesium Anode Rod:** Tank ke andar anode rod hona zaroori hai jo paani ke minerals ko khud par kheench kar tank ko scaling aur zang se bachata hai.
4. **Safety Valves (Multi-Function Valve):** Pressure release valve, vacuum release aur thermal cut-off sensor zaroor hona chahiye.
5. **Bar Pressure Rating:** High-rise multi-story buildings me paani ka pressure zyada hota hai, isliye kam se kam **8 Bar Pressure** rated geyser hi lein.

## 2kW vs 3kW Geyser: Kaun Sa Better Hai?

Aksar log poochte hain: *"Bhaisahab, 2000 Watt ka geyser lein ya 3000 Watt ka?"*

- **2 kW Geyser (Best Choice for Homes):**  
  Yeh paani garam karne me 15 se 20 minute leta hai, lagbhag 9 Ampere current khinchta hai aur ghar ke standard wiring system par load nahi daalta. Small family ke liye 2kW geyser sabse safe aur economical hota hai.
- **3 kW Geyser (Fast Heating):**  
  Yeh 10 minute me paani garam kar deta hai lekin lagbhag 13.6 Ampere current khinchta hai. Iske liye main distribution board se alag 4 sq mm ki dedicated wire honi zaroori hai.

## Electrician Ki Expert Advice (Sahi Use & Long Life)

1. **Nahane se 20 minute pehle ON karein:** Din bhar geyser ko ON chhodne par thermostat baar-baar trip hota hai jisse roz 3–4 extra units waste hoti hain.
2. **Thermostat Temperature 55°C par rakhein:** Isse paani comfortable garam rehta hai, scaling kam banti hai aur bijli ki khapat ghat jaati hai.
3. **Dedicated 16A Circuit:** Meter board se seedha 2.5 ya 4.0 sq mm copper wire ke sath 16A modular power point hi use karein.

## Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawal (FAQs)

### Q1. Small family (3-4 members) ke liye kitne litre ka geyser best hota hai?
3 se 4 members ki family ke liye **15 Litre Storage Geyser** sabse practical aur comfortable choice hoti hai.

### Q2. Kya 5-Star rated geyser lena sach me faydemand hai?
Haan. 5-Star geyser me high-density PUF insulation hoti hai jo tank ka paani lambe samay tak garam rakhti hai. Isse mahine me lagbhag 20% se 25% bijli ki bachat hoti hai.

### Q3. Instant geyser shower ke sath kaam karta hai?
Nahi. Shower ke water flow ke liye Instant geyser me paani theek se garam nahi ho pata. Shower ke liye hamesha Storage geyser hi lena chahiye.

### Q4. Hard water (khare paani) ke liye kaunsa geyser lena chahiye?
Khare paani ke liye **Glass-Lined inner tank** aur **Incoloy/Titanium heating element** wala geyser lena chahiye jisme heavy Magnesium Anode rod lagi ho.
