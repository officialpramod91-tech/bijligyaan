On-grid solar lagwane ke baad zyada tar log sochte hain ki panel lagte hi bill ghat jayega. Par mere hisaab se **net meter ke bina on-grid solar ka pura fayda nahi milta.** Purana ek-tarfa meter export ki gayi bijli ka hisaab hi nahi rakh pata, aur uske baad ghar me galat units ya bill ki shikayat aa sakti hai.

Is guide me mai bina jhanjhat ke batata hoon ki net metering kya hai, meter kaise padhein, apply kaise karein, bill kaise banta hai, aur electrician ki nazar se **kaunsi safety cheezein** zaroori hain.

## <a id="kya-hai"></a>Net Metering Kya Hai?

Normal meter sirf ek taraf ka hisaab rakhta hai: DISCOM se ghar me kitni bijli aayi. Par on-grid solar din me aksar ghar ki zaroorat se zyada bijli banata hai.

Net metering me:
1. **Din me:** extra solar bijli grid me chali jati hai (export).
2. **Raat ya kam dhoop me:** aap grid se bijli lete hain (import).
3. **Bill:** export ki gayi units import se ghata di jati hain, aur sirf **net units** ka energy charge lagta hai.

### Net meter ke bina kya hota hai?
Ek-tarfa meter export ko alag napne ke liye bana hi nahi hota, isliye kuch case me units ka hisaab galat ho sakta hai. Isi liye on-grid solar ke saath DISCOM ka certified **bi-directional ya net meter** lagaya jata hai.

On-grid ka power cut me kya hota hai aur hybrid kab sochna chahiye, iska pura comparison: [power cut me solar band kyun hota hai, aur backup kaise milta hai](/articles/on-grid-vs-off-grid-vs-hybrid-solar-system/).

## <a id="meter"></a>Bi-Directional Meter Kaise Kaam Karta Hai?

![Bi-Directional Meter working day vs night flow and import export registers](/images/bi-directional-meter-working-flow.webp)

### Import, Export aur Net reading
- **Import (kWh):** grid se li gayi bijli.
- **Export (kWh):** solar se grid ko bheji gayi extra bijli.
- **Net units:** import − export. Kai meters me ye alag register me nahi dikhta, aur bill banate waqt **DISCOM ke system me** nikalta hai.

### Meter ki reading kaise padhein (bahut kaam ki baat)
1. Meter ke button se **import** aur **export** ka display chunein.
2. Mahine ke **pehle din aur aakhri din** ki reading ki **photo** rakhein.
3. Bill aane par dekhein ki bill me import aur export units meter ke hisaab se match karte hain ya nahi.

### Smart aur prepaid meter ke saath
Kai jagah naye smart/prepaid meter lag rahe hain. Net metering ke saath wahi meter kaam karega ya alag net meter lagega, ye **DISCOM ke rules** par depend karta hai. Apne DISCOM se pehle hi likhit me poochh lein.

## <a id="bill"></a>Net Metering Bill Calculation: Bachat Kaise Hoti Hai?

Maan lijiye 3 kW rooftop solar wale ghar ka ek mahine ka hisaab:

| Detail | Units |
|---|---|
| Grid import | 350 |
| Solar export | 300 |
| **Net (billed) units** | **350 − 300 = 50** |

**Ek aur example (surplus):** import 250 aur export 300 ho to net units 0 maane jate hain, aur bachi **50 units** aage ke mahine ke liye **carry forward** (banking) ho sakti hain, agar aapki state ka rule isko allow karta hai.

### Bill zero kyun nahi hota?
Energy charge zero ya bahut kam ho sakta hai, par **fixed/demand charge, electricity duty aur kai jagah surcharge** alag se aate hain. Isliye **bill kaafi kam** hota hai, par hamesha zero nahi.

### Saal ke aakhir me bachi units ka kya hota hai?
Ye **state par depend karta hai.** Zyada tar states me financial year ke aakhir me bachi units **APPC (average power purchase cost)** ya kisi notified rate par settle hoti hain, aur kai jagah wo **lapse** bhi ho sakti hain. Ek source ke mutabik West Bengal ke 2025 regulation me leftover units zero ho jati hain, jabki Telangana jaise states me alag rate par settlement hota hai. **Apni state ke SERC regulation ya DISCOM se exact rule confirm karein.**

Solar se kitna bill bachega ye apne units par depend karta hai. Aapka kharcha aur subsidy ka hisaab 3 kW ke liye: [3 kW ka kharcha aur subsidy ka hisaab](/articles/3kw-solar-system-price-india-subsidy/).

## <a id="types"></a>Net Metering, Net Billing Aur Gross Metering Me Fark

| Type | Kaise kaam karta hai | Dhyan dein |
|---|---|---|
| **Net metering** | Export ki units import se ghata di jati hain | Export ko lagbhag retail-rate jaisa fayda |
| **Net billing** | Export ek alag (aam taur par kam) rate par credit hoti hai | Fayda kam ho sakta hai |
| **Gross metering** | Poori solar bijli DISCOM ko bechi jati hai, aur ghar ki bijli alag kharidi jati hai | Do meter lagte hain |

Kaunsa model lagu hai, ye **aapki state ka SERC regulation** tay karta hai. Kai states me consumer ko option bhi milta hai. Central level par Electricity (Rights of Consumers) Rules, 2020 (22 Feb 2024 ke amendment ke saath) rooftop solar ke liye frame deta hai, aur 500 kW tak ya sanctioned load (jo kam ho) tak net metering ki baat aati hai, par details state ke regulation se tay hoti hain.

## <a id="process"></a>Net Meter Apply Kaise Karein? (PM Surya Ghar Process)

![PM Surya Ghar net meter online application process roadmap 5 steps](/images/pm-surya-ghar-net-meter-process-steps.webp)

PM Surya Ghar me zyada tar process **national portal** par online chalta hai, par kuch steps aur forms **state/DISCOM ke hisaab se alag** ho sakte hain.

### Step 1: Portal Registration & Feasibility
PM Surya Ghar National Portal par state aur DISCOM chun kar apne Consumer Number, mobile number aur email se register karein. Feasibility check me DISCOM verify karti hai ki aapke transformer par solar load space hai ya nahi.

### Step 2: System Installation (Registered Vendor)
Approval milte hi portal par listed authorized vendor chunein. Hamesha DCR (Domestic Content Requirement) modules, certified on-grid inverter aur standard ACDB/DCDB boxes hi lagwayein.

### Step 3: Work Completion Submission
Installation poora hote hi vendor portal par system details, single-line diagram (SLD) aur safety declarations upload karke net meter ki request bhejta hai.

### Step 4: DISCOM Inspection & Net Meter Installation
Bijli vibhag ke engineer site par aakar do mukhya jaanch karte hain:
- **Earthing Resistance:** Solar structure aur inverter AC/DC earthing sahi honi chahiye.
- **Anti-Islanding Test:** Grid supply band karke dekha jata hai ki inverter turant band hota hai ya nahi.
Jaanch theek milne par purana meter hata kar certified Bi-directional Net Meter laga diya jata hai.

### Step 5: Commissioning & Subsidy Credit
Meter lagte hi portal par Commissioning Certificate generate hota hai. Iske baad bank details submit karne par central subsidy direct account (DBT) me credit ho jati hai.

> **Dhyan dein:** Central rules me 10 kW tak ke rooftop solar ke liye **alag technical feasibility study ki shart hataai gayi hai**, aur installation report ke baad **15 din** ke andar commissioning ka timeline central rules me hai. Portal par steps ke naam aur state ke rules alag ho sakte hain, isliye **apni DISCOM ki latest guideline dekhein.**

### Documents (aam taur par)
Latest bijli bill ya consumer number, ID proof, bank account details, aur DISCOM ke format ke form. Exact list portal aur DISCOM se confirm karein.

### Fees
Testing/meter/processing fees **state aur DISCOM ke hisaab se alag** hoti hain. Apni DISCOM ki official fee list dekhein, kisi aur state ka number na maanein.

## <a id="safety"></a>Electrical Safety Aur Standards (Electrician Ki Nazar Se)

DISCOM engineer meter lagane se pehle kuch cheezein check karta hai. Installer aur electrician ke taur par ye mere liye sabse zaroori hain:

### Anti-islanding
Grid ki supply band hone par grid-tie inverter **apne aap band** ho jana chahiye, taaki line par kaam kar rahe lineman ko solar se bijli na mile. Inverter ka anti-islanding test aam taur par **IEC 62116** jaise standard ke hisaab se hota hai (inverter ke datasheet me dekhein).

### Earthing aur surge protection
Solar structure, inverter aur DB ki earthing sahi honi chahiye, aur SPD (surge protection) DC aur AC side par lagti hai. Aam practice me electrodes alag hote hain aur aapas me bonded hote hain, par exact design **IS 3043 aur MNRE guidelines** ke hisaab se installer se confirm karwayein. Basic niyam: [solar ke liye earthing ke basic niyam](/articles/earthing-kya-hai/). Neutral aur earth ko galat tareeke se jodne ke khatre: [neutral aur earth ko alag kyun rakhte hain](/articles/neutral-aur-earth-wire-mein-kya-antar-hai/).

### AC side ka protection
ACDB me sahi rating ki MCB, RCCB aur isolator lagte hain. Rating kaise chunein: [AC side ki MCB rating kaise chunein](/articles/mcb-types-selection-guide/). RCCB ka kaam: [RCCB kab trip hoti hai aur kyun zaruri hai](/articles/ghar-mein-rccb-lagana-kyun-zaruri-hai/). DB me solar ke liye jagah: [DB me solar ke liye jagah aur breaker](/articles/mcb-box-db-box-kaise-chune/).

### Meter ke standards
Net/smart meter ki specification aam taur par **DISCOM ke tender/spec** aur **CEA (Installation and Operation of Meters) Regulations** ke hisaab se hoti hai. Meter standards ke numbers (jaise IS 13779, IS 16444 jaise references) **DISCOM ya meter vendor se confirm karein.** Kuch jagah ek-hi standard sab meters par lagu nahi hota.

### CEA Regulations, 2023
CEA (Measures relating to Safety and Electric Supply) Regulations, 2023 ke mutabik consumer ki installation supply se pehle **test aur verify** honi chahiye, **leakage nahi** honi chahiye, aur kaam **qualified person** se hona chahiye. Grid se jodne ke technical standards ke liye CEA ke connectivity regulations aur apni DISCOM ke niyam bhi dekhein. **Exact clause ke liye CEA/Gazette ka original text dekhein.** Ghar ki shock-fire safety ka overview: [solar aur ghar ki shock-fire safety](/articles/electric-shock-and-fire-safety-guide/).

### Voltage ki dikkat aur inverter ka trip hona
Grid voltage limit se bahar jaye to inverter **trip** kar sakta hai. Agar baar-baar trip ho raha hai, to pehle voltage ki jaanch karein: [grid voltage ki dikkat ki pehchaan](/articles/ghar-me-low-voltage-kyun-aati-hai/).

## <a id="field-cases"></a>Field Cases (Site Se)

<div style="background-color: #f8fafc; border-left: 4px solid #2563eb; padding: 1.25rem; margin: 1.5rem 0; border-radius: 4px;">

### Field Case 1: Pehle bill me export ka credit na dikhna
UP me 3 kW solar lagne ke baad consumer ka pehla bill aaya, par export units ka koi credit nahi kata tha. Humne meter ki <strong>import aur export display reading ki photo</strong> check ki aur paya ki meter me export units record ho rahi thi, lekin bill generate karte waqt DISCOM ke software me meter serial number bi-directional update nahi hua tha. Local sub-division me photo aur commissioning paper ke sath complaint file karne par agle billing cycle me pichli saari export units adjust ho gayi.  
<strong>Seekh:</strong> Meter lagte hi pehle din aur har mahine meter screen ki photo zaroor rakhein.

### Field Case 2: Inverter ka baar-baar "Grid Overvoltage" trip hona
Ek site par dhoop tez hote hi on-grid inverter baar-baar *Grid Overvoltage (OV)* fault dekar band ho raha tha. Multimeter se inverter ke AC output par voltage check kiya toh 256V tha, jabki main DB par grid ka voltage 242V hi tha. Wajah yeh thi ki inverter se DB tak bohot patli cable dali gayi thi. Cable ka resistance zyada hone ke kaaran current push karne ke liye inverter ko apna voltage zabardasti badhana pad raha tha. Humne patli wire hata kar sahi gauge ki copper cable dali, jisse resistance kam hua aur inverter ka terminal voltage safe limit (243V) par aa gaya aur tripping band ho gayi.  
Seekh: Inverter se main DB tak hamesha sahi motaai (gauge) ki copper cable lagayein taaki cable resistance ki wajah se inverter voltage boost hokar trip na kare.

### Field Case 3: Solar capacity sanctioned load se zyada hona
Ek residential consumer ne 5 kW on-grid solar plant plan kar liya, jabki unka ghar ka sanctioned load sirf 2 kW tha. DISCOM ke portal par feasibility application reject ho gayi kyunki grid rules ke mutabik rooftop solar sanctioned load se zyada nahi ho sakta. Humne pehle DISCOM portal par jakar online <strong>Load Enhancement</strong> ke liye apply kiya, load 5 kW approve hone ke baad net meter ki file pass ho payi.  
<strong>Seekh:</strong> Solar vendor ko advance dene se pehle apne bijli bill par sanctioned load verify karein.

## <a id="bill-check"></a>Pehla Bill Aane Par Ye 5 Cheezein Check Karein

1. **Meter ki reading** (import aur export) bill ki reading se match karti hai?
2. **Net units** sahi nikale gaye hain?
3. **Carry-forward (banking) units** dikh rahi hain?
4. **Fixed charges aur duty** sahi lagi hain?
5. Galti ho to **1912** par complaint karein, aur jawab na mile to **CGRF** aur phir **Electricity Ombudsman** ka rasta hai. Complaint ke saath meter ki photo-reading rakhein.

## <a id="faqs"></a>Aksar Pooche Jaane Wale Sawal (FAQs)

### 1. Net metering ke liye kitni fees lagti hai?
Fees state aur DISCOM ke hisaab se alag hoti hain. Testing, meter aur processing ka charge alag ho sakta hai. Apni DISCOM ki official fee list dekhein.

### 2. Saal ke aakhir me bachi hui extra units ka kya hota hai?
Ye state ke rules par depend karta hai. Zyada tar jagah financial year ke aakhir me bachi units APPC ya kisi notified rate par settle hoti hain, aur kuch jagah lapse ho sakti hain. Apni SERC ya DISCOM se confirm karein.

### 3. Kya 1 kW solar par bhi net meter lagta hai?
Haan. Residential rooftop solar ke liye chhoti capacity (jaise 1 kW) par bhi net metering ki suvidha hoti hai. Capacity ki limit aur sanctioned load ki shart apni state aur DISCOM ke rules se dekhein.

### 4. Kya net metering aur net billing me fark hai?
Haan. Net metering me export ki units import se ghata di jati hain. Net billing me export alag (aam taur par kam) rate par credit hoti hai. Kaunsa model lagu hai ye aapki state ka regulation tay karta hai.

### 5. Net metering ke baad bhi bill zero kyun nahi aata?
Energy charge kam ya zero ho sakta hai, par fixed charge, electricity duty aur kuch jagah surcharge alag se lagte hain. Isliye bill kaafi kam hota hai par hamesha zero nahi.

### 6. Net meter lagne me kitna time lagta hai?
Central rules me installation report ke baad commissioning ka timeline 15 din rakha gaya hai, par state ke rules aur DISCOM ki process me farq ho sakta hai. Apni DISCOM se latest timeline confirm karein.

### 7. Power cut ke waqt kya on-grid solar se ghar chalega?
Nahi. Grid band hone par on-grid inverter safety (anti-islanding) ke liye apne aap band ho jata hai. Backup ke liye hybrid ya battery wala system chahiye.

### 8. Kya hybrid solar me bhi net metering hoti hai?
Hybrid system grid-connected ho to net metering ho sakti hai, par configuration aur DISCOM ke rules par depend karta hai. Battery ke liye subsidy alag nahi milti. Pehle DISCOM aur installer se likhit me confirm karein.

## Final Verdict

Net metering on-grid solar ka asli fayda deti hai, par jeet usi ki hai jo state ke rules, sanctioned load aur meter ki reading ko pehle se samajh le. Pehle apna load aur bill dekhein, phir registered installer chunein, aur installation ke baad **meter ki photo-reading** rakhna na bhoolein. Aur solar me earthing, SPD aur RCCB ko extra na samjhein, ye wahi cheezein hain jo aapke ghar aur lineman dono ko bachati hain.
